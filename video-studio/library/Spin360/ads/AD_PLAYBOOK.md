# Spin360 ad playbook

Built 2026-11-14 from the Creative Roadmap sheet (1,626 video ads, 806 uploaded to Meta) and **272 of its 281 briefs**, read in
full (1,183 ads, 501 uploaded). Per-brief data: `briefs_index.json`. Lists: `CATALOGUE.md` (every brief), `HOOK_BANK.md`
(824 hooks). Running notes per batch: `briefs_out/PATTERNS_NOTES.md`.
**Not yet watched:** the exported videos. Drive exports are private; 84 Frame.io exports are public. So this playbook is how
the ads were *briefed*. How they were *cut* (pace, captions, music) gets confirmed once the exports are shared.

## 1. Who buys and what they're told
- **Audience:** women 45–65+ (one brief: Sleeping Porch spend was 87% women, mostly 65+). Also fan-war couples (he needs the fan,
  she hates the blast), hot sleepers, people who turn the fan to the wall in winter, new moms (nursery), AC-bill homeowners, closed
  home offices, luxury home-fragrance buyers, racing-thoughts insomniacs.
- **The core promise (said nearly the same way everywhere):**
  - "Pulls air in from the top **and** the bottom and sends it out 360°, like Saturn's rings."
  - "It doesn't feel like a fan, it feels like a breeze." / "You'd swear the walls were removed: my bed is outside / on a beach in Hawaii."
  - "One single temperature in the room." / "Is a window open?"
  - Sound: quiet on the low speeds, a steady "shhh, like wind through trees" above 5, not the "whoop-whoop" chop of a regular fan.
  - Scent pods: "spa in 10 seconds", citronella keeps bugs away (pods: Lux, Fresh, Clean; also pine and cedar in scripts).
  - Credential: the inventor (David Patrick) invented the **Shark Wheel** and got a **Shark Tank** deal (Cuban, O'Leary, Woodman).
- **Angles that recur:** sealed still air / "your bedroom is the experiment" (CO2 near the face) · sleep better · AC companion ("74
  feels like 67–68", thermostat back up, lower AC bill) · fan sleepers in winter / heater · nursery and babies · husband-wife · scent
  ("smell expensive", luxury hotel) · price justification (~50¢ a night).

## 3. Hooks
- **The testing system:** one locked body, **3–8 hooks** (up to 12). Only the first 3–6 s change: the visual, the text card or the
  spoken line. Keep the same VO across visual hooks so the test stays clean.
- **First-frame rules:** bright, thumbnail-like, no fade-in; on-screen text in the first second that raises a question **on mute**.
- **Hook types that recur** (824 hooks in `HOOK_BANK.md`):
  - pattern interrupt: "what is this guy doing 😂" (drill), "IT'S A SCAM", "TRYING TO SLEEP AFTER 50 BE LIKE"
  - sarcastic or ironic: "I invented a fan that sucks", "5 reasons my husband hates this fan", "Do not fall for this fan"
  - call-outs: "If you need cold air to sleep…", "If you're 53…", "Fan sleepers, here are three reasons…"
  - yes/no: "Is it an AC? No. Does it feel like one? Yes."
  - strange-fact openers (babies in snow, husky, Biosphere 2) and absurd first frames (talk-show baby, laid-off sheep)
  - big numbers ("$469,000"), "It's 104 degrees", POV text cards, "Here's what happens when…" timelines
- From the library: Kylie "Do not fall for this fan. This thing is a scam.", Script 3 "This is legal in Sweden…", Vicky "My
  husband begged for a fan in our room for 3 years…", Lauren "This thing does not feel like a fan", Kylie/Michael "Last summer, I hit
  a wall", the sweat-stained sheet, the "Hot Sleepers… this one's for you" sticky note, the aerosol spray pulled through the fan.

## 4. Structure (the shared back half)
Hook (3–6 s) → problem lands (still, stuffy, sealed room; a regular fan is only ever the "harsh straight stream" enemy) →
mechanism (top + bottom intake, 360°) → **proof shown** (ribbons / tissue / smoke / thermometer, "pulse vs flow", never a still
side; 2 s of real fan audio with no music) → feeling (walls gone, bed outside) → extras (quiet "shhh", scent pod) → credential
(Shark Wheel / Shark Tank) → soft CTA ("Go check it out", "Tap below and check it out", "Cool the room around you with Spin360").
The product appears only **after** the problem has landed. One brief keeps a 19-beat template from the Swedish Babies winner.

## 5. Tone, captions, music, edit
- **Tone:** calm, warm, "a friend telling you something", never an announcer and never salesy ("anti-salesman lock").
- **Pacing:** older briefs asked for the first 3 s fast and no clip over 2 s in the first 10 s; **later feedback: "a lot of these edits
  lately have been too fast"**, so cut calm and smooth. Documentary slow for VSLs.
- **Captions:** burned in throughout, white, a few words at a time (word-by-word, bold, outlined); founder ads use red ALL-CAPS anchors.
- **Music:** sparse piano or drone; drops to silence right before the reveal and turns warm at the product; let the real fan sound through on the white-noise beat.
- **Voice:** ElevenLabs ("Siren"; feedback says switch TTS to ElevenLabs), fish.audio, Gemini "Orus" with pause tags; David from stringouts or a clone.
- **Look:** visual congruency (same room, light and grade) beats literal illustration. The product is the **white floor model** in most AI
  and briefed shots; real footage has both colourways and both heights (see `../CAST_AND_SETS.md`).
- **Sizes:** 9:16 always; Chris's briefs also 4:5.

## 6. Offers and CTA, over time (check which one is live before every ad)
1. Early summer: "$110 off this spring/summer" (held on screen 7–10 s), "first 1,000 get $100/$110 off", "consistently sells out".
2. "$189, before it goes back to $299" / "$189, down from $300", with a guarantee; BOF sale clips and apology/FOMO lines.
3. **Latest (Gabriel briefs): no discount, no price.** End card only: "Try it for 30 nights, send it back" (one outlier says 90
   nights). Even the LiveLonger winner was re-cut without the discount.

## 7. Claim rules (the newest briefs win; see also `../CLAIMS.md`)
- Never "silent" or "quiet" as a promise; never say it **cools or heats**; no "money-back"; no discounts unless the brief says so;
  no season words in evergreen ads; "spa", not "forest"; **don't invent claims**; real reviews only.
- Nursery: no fear and no SIDS framing in the nursery brief. **Conflict:** the SIDS VSL uses the Kaiser study's "72% lower risk",
  and baby ads reuse "2 hours → 5 hours". Get a client ruling before using either.
- Older scripts and library takes still contain "dead silent", "cools the room", "make you live longer", "patented double vortex".
  Treat them as banned unless the client re-approves.

## 9. Recipe for a new Spin360 ad (until told otherwise)
1. Pick angle + awareness + format from §1–2; check `CATALOGUE.md` for the closest past brief and its winner.
2. Write one body using the §4 structure and the latest claim rules (§7) and offer (§6). Pull lines from `../resources/SCRIPT_TAKES.md`
   (real takes) before writing new ones.
3. Write 3–6 hooks (§3), each working on mute in the first second; same VO across visual hooks.
4. Pick footage with `../tools/find.py` / `../resources/BROLL_INDEX.md`: OK status, the right colourway and height, one person and
   one house per ad, proof shots (ribbons, tissue, thermometer, display) for the mechanism.
5. Cut calm: white word-by-word captions, sparse music that drops before the reveal, 2 s of real fan audio, end card per §6.
6. Log it: `vs task new <CODE>`, and `vs learn use Spin360 <ids> --project <CODE>` after delivery.
