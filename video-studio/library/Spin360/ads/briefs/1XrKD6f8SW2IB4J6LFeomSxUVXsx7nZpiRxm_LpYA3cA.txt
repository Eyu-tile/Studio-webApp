The idea: The ScamConfession structure applied to the Swedish Babies story. David opens over the video's first scenes warning you NOT to fall for it — it's a scam. Then he retells the video's story (babies, the air, your sealed bedroom, the fan that feels like outside moving air)... confirms every word of it is true ("that's my fan — I built that thing")... and confesses the real scam: calling this thing a "fan." A fan blows air at your face. His pushes it — soft, from every direction, like wind. You sleep like a Swedish baby, without the snow. Reverse CTA: "Do not buy this. ...I'm kidding. Buy it."


Why this works: the account's two best engines in one piece — the Swedish Babies story (top seller) + the Scam confession structure (highest-ROAS device). The "scam" framing keeps the viewer hunting for the lie through the entire story; the confession pays it off with the product.


Format: the winning Scam layout — b-roll from the Swedish Babies ad fills the frame, David cutout PiP bottom-left talking the whole time, continuous captions. David goes FULL FRAME on exactly one stretch: the confession block ("So what's the scam?..."), then the final wide. ~88s, 9:16.


Audio: David's voice is the ONLY voice — the reference video's audio is never used. Its scenes are reused as silent b-roll. No claims are inherited from the original ad's soundtrack.


The burned-in captions: the reference scenes carry the original ad's own captions ("In Sweden", "on purpose", etc.). They STAY — they're proof that "the video" David is talking about actually exists. David's spoken captions live in a distinct style so the two text layers never blur.


Tone: the Scam register — playful warning, storyteller through the middle, honest confession at the turn, friend at the close.


How to read the storyboard: the visuals are the Swedish Babies ad's own scenes, re-cut to follow David's narration. The VISUAL IDEA column names the scene from the reference video; timings follow the VO. Battle plan, not a cage.


MUSIC: the account's Scam recipe — music from frame zero, light and curious under the story · warms on "Then it says somebody built a fan..." · CUTS on "So what's the scam?" (the confession plays close and dry) · returns warm for "Do not buy this... I'm kidding" · resolves on the wide. Duck for the shhh if used.


CAPTIONS + TEXT: continuous spoken captions (David's layer, distinct style) · the reference video's burned-in captions stay · red caps anchors fired on their lines: "THIS IS A SCAM" (hook) · "IT'S THE AIR" · "THAT'S WHERE YOU'RE TRYING TO SLEEP" · "THAT'S MY FAN" · "PUSHING, NOT BLOWING" · "...I'M KIDDING.
