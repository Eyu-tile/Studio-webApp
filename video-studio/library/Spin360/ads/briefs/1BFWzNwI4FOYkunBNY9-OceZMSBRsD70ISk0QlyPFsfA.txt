SPIN360 — "The Porch"
Video Prompts · Kling 3.0
Cinematic two-friend conversation, golden hour, wooden deck of a hillside beach house. 14 clips (~10–12s each) · product B-roll layered on top in editing · breeze always SOFT.
How to use: the Global Look + Characters block below applies to every clip. Prepend it to each clip's timestamp breakdown when generating — Kling has no memory between generations.
________________


🎨 GLOBAL LOOK + CHARACTERS
(applies to every clip)
Cinematic luxury commercial, shot on ARRI Alexa with vintage cinema lenses, shallow depth of field, creamy bokeh. Warm dreamy aspirational aesthetic — the visual language of a high-end fragrance ad or a Nancy Meyers film. Golden hour on the wooden deck of a hillside beach house, late afternoon, ocean far below and green cliffs behind.


	

	LIGHTING
	soft glowing golden-hour sunlight, warm amber key wrapping the subjects, gentle rim light on their hair, subtle controlled lens flare from the low sun, soft halation on highlights — designed, intentional, expensive light.
	COLOR GRADE
	warm and filmic, golden highlights, soft teal shadows, gentle contrast, warm healthy skin tones, slightly desaturated and elegant.
	CAMERA
	subtle organic movement, slow and deliberate as if on a high-end gimbal; composed framing with layered depth — soft foreground, sharp subject, dreamy ocean-and-cliffs background.
	TEXTURE
	fine 35mm film grain, gentle motion blur on moving hair and fabric; cinematic and filmic, never plastic or AI-smooth.
	BREEZE
	keep it SOFT and GENTLE: only a light, lazy breeze barely moving a few loose strands of hair, a sheer linen curtain swaying softly and slightly, a potted plant with leaves gently trembling, a calm ocean. Subtle, serene late-afternoon air — NOT windy, NOT strong, no hair flying, no flapping or billowing fabric.
	CHARACTERS
* LINDA (about 60) — warm natural woman, silver-grey hair in a soft medium cut with a few fine strands gently drifting, graceful light wrinkles, a gentle tiredness around her eyes that softens as she relaxes, sand-toned neutral linen. Beautiful at her real age, not de-aged, not a model. The guest — the one who hasn't slept well in years.
* CAROL (about 60) — her lifelong friend, longer loose greyish-blonde hair, sun-warmed skin of someone who lives by the sea, calm rested content face, off-white / cream linen, easy and at home. She owns the house and the fan.
They sit close on comfortable deck chairs facing the golden ocean, turning to each other as they talk.
SOUND (every clip) — gentle ocean swell, soft wind, distant birds throughout, intimate and warm. No intrusive music.
