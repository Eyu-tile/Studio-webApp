[2.1] AI Direction 


INSTRUCTIONS TO COPY AND PASTE INTO YOUR AI FOR GENERATION:
All AI-generated images for this ad must look like documentary photography shot on 35mm film, Kodak Portra 400. Muted Scandinavian color palette — desaturated blues, grays, muted whites for exterior Sweden scenes. Warm muted amber for interior bedroom scenes. Real textures everywhere: dirty packed snow (not fresh powder), worn wool with pills, smudged glass with condensation, skin with visible pores, redness from cold, dry lips. Natural light ONLY — no studio lighting, no fill light. Overcast Nordic winter sky for exteriors. Visible lamps for interiors. No AI glow, no plastic skin, no clean perfect edges, no HDR look. If it looks like AI, it doesn't work. Every frame must pass as a still pulled from a real BBC documentary about Scandinavian culture.
Documentary, not cinematic. The camera OBSERVES life. It does not dramatize it. No one looks at camera. Scenes feel found, not staged. The beauty comes from reality, not production.
Two color worlds: Sweden exterior = cool blue-gray, 6500K. Bedroom interior = warm amber, 2700K tungsten. The contrast between the two tells the story before the audio

[4] Creative Direction 


Visual World — Unaware Section 
* Cold, beautiful, cinematic. Snow-covered Scandinavian villages at night. Warm cabin lights glowing. Nordic interiors — thick blankets, candles, frost on windows, a curtain moving gently from a cracked window. Should feel aspirational and peaceful. Color grade: cool blues, warm amber from candlelight. 
Visual World — Bridge 
* Hard contrast cut. Hot, still, uncomfortable. Summer bedroom. A woman lying awake staring at the ceiling. The ceiling fan barely moving. AC unit humming. Warm color grade. Should feel stuffy and restless compared to everything before it. 
Visual World — Product Reveal 
* Dark, calm bedroom. Spin360 glowing with soft blue LED. Woman sleeping peacefully under a blanket with one arm out. Complete stillness. Should feel like the Nordic scenes from the opening — cool, peaceful, resolved. 
The Emotional Arc: 
* Fascination → Recognition → Relief. The viewer should feel a quiet 'that's me' moment in the Bridge, and a quiet 'I need that' moment in the Feeling section. Never pushed. Never rushed. 
What NOT to do: 
* No fast cuts in the unaware section. No upbeat music. No product close-ups until the reveal. No mechanism diagrams — this ad sells feeling, not features. Do not mention the price until the CTA.

	[7] Editing Rules 


● DO NOT rush. This ad earns its watch-through with pacing and atmosphere, not speed. 
● Unaware section (0–55 sec): slow, cinematic cuts. 3–5 seconds per clip minimum. 
● Bridge contrast cut should feel like a bucket of warm water — uncomfortable after the cool Nordic world. 
● Product reveal onward: return to the slow, cool visual world. Should feel like resolution.
● No dead air — but silence and near-silence are intentional tools here. Use them. 
● VO pacing: short punchy lines (Cool. Fresh. Gentle.) need a [pause] between each word. Do not rush them. 
● Music: starts cool and atmospheric, builds very subtly through the Bridge, softens again at the Feeling section. Never dramatic. 
● Sound design: subtle ambient wind/nature sounds throughout. Should feel like cool night air in the room. 
● Add captions for entire video. 
● All visuals must match audio — no random stock shots. 
● Export: 9x16 for all hooks.
