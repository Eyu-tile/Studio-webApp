Creative Brief
	Direction
	Concept / Working Name
	Spin360 — Shut Your Brain Off Mashup
	Format
	9:16 vertical social ad. No voiceover. Music-driven visual mashup with minimal text on screen.
	Primary Avatar
	Adult woman who gets to the end of the day physically exhausted but mentally still “on.” She gets into bed and her brain starts running through work, tomorrow, unfinished tasks, conversations, random thoughts. Do not make this specifically a “wife ad.” We are speaking directly to her.
	Core Desire
	“I just want to finally switch off, sink into bed, and feel done for the day.” This is the emotional sale. We are not fundamentally selling a fan.
	Core Tension
	Her body is in bed, but her brain has not arrived there yet. Quiet can make everything in her head feel louder.
	Product Role
	Spin360 changes the sensory environment around her: soft natural-feeling breeze + soft steady shhhh + room-wide airflow. It should feel like the bedroom itself becomes easier to settle into.
	Emotional Arc
	Start with busy / mentally on / overstimulated → transition into release → finish in deep comfort, softness, calm, eyes closing, peaceful sleep. The edit should make the viewer feel this transition.
	Opening / Hook Territory
	Must stop the scroll with a recognizable female bedtime moment. Examples: woman exhausted in bed but eyes wide open; rapid visual fragments of the day still running through her head; contrast between a visually “busy” head and a physically still bedroom. Possible text: SHUT YOUR BRAIN OFF AT NIGHT or YOUR BODY’S TIRED / YOUR BRAIN’S STILL ON.
	Editing Style — First Section
	Faster cuts, slightly more tension in the music, close shots, phone/work/calendar/to-do fragments, turning in bed, staring at ceiling. It should feel like thoughts that refuse to stop, not like a clinical anxiety commercial.
	The Hinge
	There needs to be a noticeable moment where the ad changes feeling. Music opens up, edit slows, room starts breathing, curtains move, hair moves gently, Spin360 appears. This is the “ahhh” moment.
	Editing Style — Second Section
	Longer shots, slower motion, tactile visuals. Woman sinking into pillow. Soft fabric. Hair moving slightly. Curtains breathing. Eyes gradually closing. Warm nighttime lighting. Product naturally present in the room.
	Most Important Sensory Images
	Visually communicate breeze across skin, air moving around the room, open-window / perfect-night feeling, soft shhhh, and finally her body visibly releasing tension. Customer VOC around Spin360 is strongest in experiential terms such as “breeze,” “gentle,” “soft,” “moving air,” “outdoor breeze” and “open window.”
	Product Shots
	Mix polished product shots with realistic UGC. Spin360 running bedside, woman turning it on, fan in foreground with woman relaxing behind it, curtains moving, low-light nighttime bedroom. Do not make the whole piece feel like a product demo.
	Text Philosophy
	Text is punctuation, not narration. Ideally 2–6 words per card. The viewer should understand the ad from the edit even with most text removed.
	Optional Text Pool — Not a Fixed Script
	SHUT YOUR BRAIN OFF AT NIGHT / YOUR BRAIN’S STILL ON / WHEN QUIET GETS LOUD / SOFT BREEZE / SOFT SHHH / SOMETHING TO SETTLE INTO / LET THE DAY GO / FINALLY UNWIND / A ROOM READY FOR SLEEP / SPIN360
	Music
	Extremely important. Begin with something slightly restless, rhythmic or mentally “busy,” then create a clear emotional drop/release when the Spin360 experience enters. The second half should feel dreamy, spacious, warm and satisfying. Music transitions should drive picture transitions.
	Creative Freedom
	Editor is encouraged to rearrange shots, repeat sensory moments, use speed ramps, match cuts, subtle sound design, visual metaphors, micro-montages and music hits. Do not feel obligated to put text over every shot. Several moments should simply breathe.
	What NOT to Do
	No explainer-video feel. No long copy cards. No “5 benefits” structure. No medical/anxiety claims. No implication that Spin360 literally stops thoughts. No loud/harsh “white noise” positioning. No generic stock woman smiling at a fan. No overemphasis on blade engineering.
	Ending Feeling
	The viewer should finish thinking “God, I want my bedroom to feel like that tonight.” Product hero + simple Spin360 branding/CTA is enough.
