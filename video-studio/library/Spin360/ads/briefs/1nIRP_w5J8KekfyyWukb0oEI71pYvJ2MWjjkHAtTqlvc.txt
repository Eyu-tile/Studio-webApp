3 ads, same body. Each ad has its own visual hook (0–8 s) and its own first on-screen line. The last two on-screen lines and the whole body are the same in all 3. Cut the body once and reuse it. Images in the Visual Inspiration column are inspiration, not final frames.
Strategy
# Element
	Answer
	1 · Desire
	"I want my brain to actually relax when I go to bed." Not knocked out — relieved. Real sleep, wake up feeling like a person again, patience for the people she loves.
	2 · Avatar
	Experiences: "tired but wired" — exhausted, but the second her head hits the pillow her brain switches on: tomorrow's to-dos, work, conversations, something awkward from years ago. Behaviors: sleep apps, podcasts, a fan even in winter. Emotions: exhausted, frustrated, quietly blaming herself. Cast: a woman ~38 (the singer's voice is early 30s) and her husband.
	3 · Angle
	Quiet room, loud brain. It's not you, it's the quiet: in dead silence your own thoughts become the loudest thing in the room.
	4 · Mechanism
	White noise and a relaxing breeze in one. One steady sound her brain can latch onto, that doesn't loop like a sleep app, and a breeze with no air blasting in her face.
	5 · Authority
	First-person voiceover (her own experience) · 30-night trial.
	6 · Awareness
	Problem aware in the hook (she knows racing thoughts keep her up) → product aware by the end.
	7 · Emotion
	Hook: calm, aspirational, curious ("how is she asleep?"). Body: relief and confidence. End: easy, risk-free decision.
	8 · Positioning
	Enemy: lying awake with racing thoughts, sleep apps that loop, a regular fan blasting air. Spin360: white noise and a relaxing breeze in one.
	Production brief
Format: vertical 9:16, ~35 s, all scenes AI-generated. 0–8 s: no voice. Only white noise + a night scene + 3 on-screen texts. Hard cut at ~8 s to the body: her first-person voiceover over B-roll. She never talks to the camera and never looks into it.
Look: realistic, cinematic footage in one bedroom. Night scenes dark with a soft blue glow; morning scenes in natural window light. The hook shots must stop the scroll on their own.
No product in the hook (0–8 s). We never see what makes the sound or the breeze. Spin360 appears only after the cut.
Audio: real white noise from the first frame (0.0 s), no music, no voice, no fade in. At the cut, her voiceover takes over.
On-screen text: hook texts in white with the emoji, center of the screen, one at a time (~2.5 s each). Body: captions of the voiceover.
Cast & set (same in every scene): SHE (40s, long dark brown hair, light grey long-sleeve pajama set), HIM (her husband, bald, green t-shirt, in bed asleep), one bedroom: dark wood headboard, black-and-white abstract art above the bed, a tall palm plant, white textured duvet, big window with a forest view. Every scene in the ad is generated with AI using this same woman and this same room. She is only ever relaxing or asleep, except the last morning shot (rested, after 30 nights).
Product: Spin360, white floor model only (reference: FLOOR_0.1.jpg). Always on the floor next to her side of the bed, never on furniture. The air is a breeze, not wind: a couple of strands of hair and the sheet edge move, nothing more. No app logos.
