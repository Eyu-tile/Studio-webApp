What is one thing that every human wants 


Good Sleep

Sleep is a $585 billion dollar industry.

From random supplements to Jet Cooled Beds 


Thousands of products that promise that sought after shut-eye 


So why do so many people struggle to get good sleep? 

It comes down to how we evolved as a species. 


Our ancestors spent thousands of years living in nature 


Sleeping with open moving air

We live in major cities with bad air, starting at our screens, away from nature. 


For context, it took 2.4 million years for humans to master fire 


66 years to go from the the frist airplane to men on the moon. 


And 40 years from the first cell phone, to today’s AI Boom. 


Our bodies haven’t evolved fast enough for our current environment. 


NEED A GOOD TRANSITION HERE about how airflow is the fix 


So we set out to re-design airflow from the ground up. 


The goal was to mimic that amazing breeze found in nature


And bring it into your home. 


The result is the most relaxing, natural feeling airflow
