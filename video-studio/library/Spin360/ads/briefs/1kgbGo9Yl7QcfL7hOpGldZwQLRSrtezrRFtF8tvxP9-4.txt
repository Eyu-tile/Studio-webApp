	Strategy
# Element
	Answer
	Desire
	A cold room to fall asleep in.
	Avatar
	Adults, mostly women 45–60, who sleep hot and need cold air in the room to fall asleep.
	Angle
	If you need cold air to sleep, this fan gives it to you.
	Mechanism
	A 360 fan that moves cool air in all directions, so it can go anywhere in the room. Levels 6 to 9 give a nice white noise for sleeping. Scent pods, including citronella for bugs.
	Authority
	A real person talking about her own room ("This thing makes my room feel so cold"), plus concrete specs (9 speeds, 6 to 9 for sleep).
	Awareness
	Hook: Problem Aware (she knows she needs cold air to sleep). Body: Product Aware.
	Emotion
	Recognition ("that's me") in the hook, then relief.
	Positioning
	The fan for people who need cold air to sleep.
	Format
	Iteration of our winner DontHaveAC: same structure, same voiceover line by line, same kind of shots, with new footage. The only change in the words: no mention of AC.
	Hooks
	3 spoken hooks, same opening visual. Control / age 53 / hot and sweaty, so we learn which call-out stops the scroll.
	Offer
	30 days to try it. If you don't love it, you send it back.
	Production brief
Format: vertical 9:16, about 32 s, voiceover over simple product shots, captions a few words at a time. Same structure and the same kind of shots as our winner DontHaveAC, with new footage.

This thing makes my room feel so cold.

It has nine speeds, and up to level five it's super quiet, and level six through nine have a really nice white noise that's perfect for sleeping.
You can also drop in scent pods so the room smells nice, and even citronella to keep bugs away.
