Strategy
# Element
	Answer
	1 · Desire
	"I want my whole room to feel warm, not just the ceiling."
	2 · Avatar
	Women 45+ in US homes with central heat and a wall thermostat. The heat is on and their feet are still cold, so they turn the thermostat up and put on socks.
	3 · Angle
	It makes your heater work better: the heat comes down to where you are.
	4 · Mechanism
	Heat rises and the cold sinks. Spin360 pulls the hot air down and the cool air up, so the room becomes one temperature. Proof: ceiling 78, floor 71, fan on: the whole room 73 (David's own test).
	5 · Authority
	David, the inventor, on screen: "David Patrick, Inventor of Spin360". He explains it and gives the test numbers.
	6 · Awareness
	Problem aware. She knows the heat is on and her feet are cold. The ad shows why, and what fixes it.
	7 · Emotion
	Warm and cozy. Small surprise in the hook ("not a heater, but it makes yours work better"), then relief.
	8 · Positioning
	Enemy: the heat stuck at the ceiling. Spin360 is not a heater: it brings the heat you already pay for down to you.
	Production brief
David: every David shot is Seedance 2.5 in the new set below (start frame: the 0:04 row image), using the LA footage as the reference for his face, voice and mannerisms. 
The set: a cozy American living room at night: curtains closed, warm table lamps, a beige sofa with a knit throw, a white wall thermostat behind David, the Spin360 glowing blue next to him. No window view, no radiator.
Heater on screen: the wall thermostat and an orange space heater (no visible fan blades). Spin360 is the only fan on screen.
Look: warm and evening everywhere. Grade the reused winner shots warmer; crop out any bright window or summer clothes, or replace the shot.
