	Production brief
Video: the original is the timeline (minus the discount line, see "The new ending"). Lay it down first, audio locked from start to finish. The dog shots are cutaways on top of it (generated clips), each one a few seconds, placed on the lines marked in the storyboard. Between cutaways the original plays full screen, and it should be full screen most of the time: the mechanism, the product demo, the Shark Tank proof and the offer are never covered by the dog.
David: real footage everywhere, except the one new line. That line is generated from a real frame with his real voice as reference, and it has to pass as the same shot.
The dog: one adult golden retriever, the same in every shot (same fur, size, collar or no collar), generated cinematic and photorealistic (Seedance / Kling, from the images below as start frames). He behaves like a dog: head tilt, ears, a step closer, a look at the phone. Never talks, never has human expressions, no cartoon takes. The one joke is the paw on Add to Cart at the end, and even that is shot like it really happened.
The room: one cozy American living room at night, wooden floor, rug, sofa, warm lamps, big TV on the wall. The TV light is cool teal on the dog, the lamps are warm. Smooth, slow camera moves, shallow depth of field, reflections on the screen.
The TV: the real video goes inside the TV (corner-pinned, with the room's reflections and light). The source is 9:16 and the TV is landscape: show the whole portrait video centered with a soft blurred fill on the sides, never stretched, never with the captions cut off. When we are on the dog, add a light "TV speaker" feel to David's voice; when the original is full screen, the audio is the master, clean.


The new ending (read this first)
The original ends with a discount we are not running. Here is exactly what to do, in order:
1. Keep the original from 0:00 to 1:43.1 — it ends on "…and look, we're just getting started with this."
2. Cut 1:43.1 → 1:49.8. That is the whole discount: "So the first thousand people that come in right now are getting a hundred bucks off. And I'm telling you, that is not going to last." Nothing of it stays, audio or picture.
3. Insert the new David line (about 5 s), generated with Seedance: "So try it for 30 nights. And if you don't love it, send it back." It plays inside the TV, like the rest of David.
4. Resume the original audio at 1:49.8 → 1:57.2: "Because once people feel this thing, they don't go back. So if you want to be part of that, come check it out." The "because" now points at the 30 nights, which is why it works.
5. From 1:41 to the end, never use the original picture (it has "FIRST 1,000 GET $110 OFF" burned into it). The picture there is the dog: the phone, the paw on Add to Cart, then the payoff.
6. New end card: Try it for 30 nights. Don't love it? Send it back. + logo, over the dog asleep with the Spin360. No price, no discount, no "first 1,000".
