[4] Script




Imagine you're walking through the coastal dunes, late in the afternoon. Nobody around for miles.


The sand is soft under your feet. The dune grass is tall around your legs. Beyond it, the ocean keeps breathing.


Out across the grass, you see the breeze before you feel it — rolling in from the water in one slow wave, bending everything on its way to you.


Then it reaches you. Salt in the air. Against your hands. Your shirt. Your hair.


It’s not coming from one side. It’s all around you — your arms, the back of your neck, everywhere at once.


So you stop. You close your eyes, and you turn your face into it.


Your shoulders come down — and you realize you’d been holding them up there all day.


Your breathing slows on its own. You don’t even decide to. It just happens.


Out here, nothing wants anything from you. The air just moves, and you let it move you.


You know this feeling. You just can’t remember the last time you actually had it.


A trip, maybe. Years ago. Or one of those evenings when the whole world finally got quiet.


That’s not a dream.


That’s Spin360.


Here’s the part that’s hard to believe until you feel it: Spin360 was built to recreate that kind of air. Not just air moving — air moving naturally.


Real coastal air doesn’t come at you in one straight line. It moves in layers. It folds around you. It touches your skin, disappears, then comes back from somewhere else.


That’s what the blade was built to recreate. It pulls air in from above and below, then guides it outward in a slow, smooth 360-degree current — circling through the whole room.


So what reaches your skin doesn’t feel like a draft aimed at you. It moves around you — across the sheets, around your arms, behind your neck, through the corners. The way it moved around you out there.


And your body settles, because your skin recognizes the pattern.


The whole room comes alive with it — the curtains, the sheets, the air on your skin — until a closed room starts to feel open.


Spin360 wasn’t built just to cool a room. It was built to give it a feeling.


Atmosphere — not just airflow.


It doesn’t tell you to relax. It reminds your body how.


Below speed five, it’s silent. Above speed five, it turns into a soft hush — that gentle shhh that pushes the rest of the world a little farther away.


Drop in a scent pod — Sunlit Citrus, like bright citrus on a breeze. Soft Florals, like fresh linen and warm vanilla. Or Citronella, for that outside feeling without the bugs.


Now it can feel like those dunes at golden hour. Like fresh air moving through an open room. Like the one evening your body actually let go.


Spin360 came from David Patrick — the inventor behind Shark Wheel, the invention that brought him to Shark Tank and put him across the table from Mark Cuban and Nick Woodman, founder of GoPro.


After reinventing the wheel, David spent years chasing a quieter question: what if you could bring the feeling of outside into your home?


This is the answer.
