The feeling we're selling
We're not selling a fan, and we're not selling a scent. We're selling that moment when you walk into a really nice hotel and just breathe — and then getting that same moment when you open your own front door. Every shot should make the viewer think: "I want to come home to that."
This is inspiration, not rules. If you find a better shot that sells the same feeling, use it.
The one visual idea that runs through the whole ad
The bubble vs. the flow. Bubble = a warm, golden grade only in a small circle around the candle; the rest of the room is slightly cooler and still (sheer curtain, plant leaves, open book pages — dead still). Flow = the warm grade fills the whole frame and soft things move gently (sheer curtain, plant leaves, a flame leaning). Visible haze only in hotel light beams, and very subtle. Never visible haze or dust inside her home — it reads as smoke or dirt.
The ad moves from bubble to flow. Hotel = flow. Her home before = bubble. Her home with Spin360 = the bubble breaks open and flows through the room, just like the hotel.
Rules of thumb
* Format: 9:16 vertical. Captions always on, big and simple.
* Pace: slow and warm in the hotel and feeling parts. Tighter and quicker in the "it doesn't work at home" parts.
* Look: warm, soft, premium. Luxury hotel film, not a tech demo. No cold blue gadget light.
* Music: soft piano and strings. It thins out at "Here's what nobody tells you," lifts at "With this," and drops to almost nothing at "You take one breath..." before the last line.
* Sound design: quiet lobby ambience in hotel shots. A soft air hush every time air moves. Dead-quiet room tone when her home air is still.
* One main character: a woman who loves home fragrance — relaxed, stylish, real, 30s to 50s. Same woman in every AI shot so it feels like one story.
* Airflow must look gentle. Never hair blasting, never things flying. It moves around her, not at her.
* Scent placement: the scent pod goes ON TOP of Spin360. Anything else (diffuser) goes NEXT TO it, never on top. Never show a lit candle right next to the fan.
* No real hotel names or logos anywhere. Hotels and hotel-scent products stay unbranded.
* Text on screen: only a few key words, marked in the Note column. Don't clutter. Never place it over the caption area.
* Product accuracy: AI-generated product shots are fine, but the unit must always look like the real Spin360 (same shape, same color, pod on top), and it must never be shown doing more than the real demo shows (no smoke filling a whole room, no strong wind).
* Lived-in home: her home must feel real and personal, not like a catalog. Always show a few of her own things: a framed photo, an open book, her favorite mug, a throw on the couch. Keep the same details in every AI shot of her home.
* Continuity: if any shot mixes real and AI footage, the hands, wardrobe and product must match across them.
* No lit candle in the same frame as Spin360 — including the breath shot and the end card.
* Real footage, not AI, for: Shark Wheel and the Shark Tank clip. Never AI-generate Shark Tank. David Patrick can be AI-generated, but he must look like the real David. For the smoke demo, real footage is strongly preferred because it is the proof. Never show or suggest Spin360 on Shark Tank — the Shark Tank deal was for Shark Wheel.
Hook: 4 voiceover variations
This test is different from a normal visual test: each hook has its own voiceover, and the body after it is exactly the same for all four. So we can test which opening line wins. Each hook gets its own visual idea below. The first frame has to be striking at 0.0 seconds: no logo, no slow fade-in. Every hook ends by handing off cleanly into the first body line.

1. Desire
Surface Desire / Why Train Starting Point:
 “I want my home to smell expensive, like a five-star hotel.”
Why Train:
 → Why? Because I want my home to feel luxurious and elevated.
→ Why? Because I want being at home to feel more special and pleasurable, like the experience of staying at a five-star hotel.
→ Why? Because I want to genuinely enjoy the atmosphere of the space I live in.
Deeper Desired State:
 A home that feels luxurious, elevated, and pleasurable to live in.
Social Reinforcement:
 “I want people who enter my home to notice how good it smells.”
2. Avatar
Primary Avatar:
 The luxury home-fragrance buyer.
Experiences:
 Already uses or has used candles, room sprays, plug-ins, diffusers, or fragrance oils to make their home smell better.
Behaviors:
 Spends money on products that improve how the home smells and feels, experiments with scents, cares about the vibe of the space, and is attracted to luxury-hotel-inspired products.
What They’ve Already Tried:
 Candles, sprays, plug-ins, traditional diffusers, fragrance oils.
What They Want:
 A home that smells expensive, luxurious, and like a five-star hotel.
Frustrations:
 Current scent solutions may only scent one area, fade too quickly, require constant replacement, or fail to fill the space.
Emotional Context:
 They want their home to feel elevated, polished, and luxurious — and they like when other people notice how good it smells.
3. Angle
Primary Angle:
 Many traditional scent products make one part of the room smell good — but they don’t change how the whole room smells and feels.
Problem Being Solved:
 The customer wants the luxurious, five-star-hotel atmosphere at home, but many traditional scenting solutions are strongest around the source and mainly address fragrance. They can improve how part of the space smells without changing the overall atmosphere of the room.
Why Someone Pays Attention:
 They may already be spending money on candles, sprays, plug-ins, or diffusers, yet still aren’t getting the complete luxury-hotel atmosphere they actually want.
Spin360 Opportunity:
 Spin360 can address both limitations: it helps move scented air throughout the room while also creating a soft, natural-feeling breeze.
Angle in One Sentence:
Many traditional scent products only improve the smell around one area. Spin360 helps turn that scent into a whole-room experience — so the room smells like a five-star hotel and feels amazing too.
 4. Mechanism
Primary Mechanism:
 Spin360 pulls air from above and below, mixes the scented air with the room air, and redistributes it 360° throughout the room — while creating a soft, natural-feeling breeze.
Former Limitation:
Many traditional scent products release fragrance from a fixed source, so the scent can remain stronger around that area, and they do not change how the room physically feels.
How Spin360 Overcomes It:
 Spin360 adds scent distribution + natural-feeling air movement. By pulling air from above and below, mixing the scented air with the room air, and redistributing it 360°, Spin360 helps carry fragrance beyond the immediate source while also creating a soft breeze.
Mechanism → Promise:
The same 360° airflow helps distribute the scent through the room while making the room feel better too.
Mechanism in One Sentence:
Spin360 pulls, mixes, and redistributes scented air 360° throughout the room while creating a soft, natural-feeling breeze.






5. Authority
Primary Authority:
 Years spent pursuing the desired atmosphere.
David Patrick, the inventor behind Spin360, spent years trying to create the best possible atmosphere indoors. To achieve that, he developed a system designed to recreate natural-feeling airflow — and use that airflow to help distribute scent throughout the room.
Why He’s Credible:
 He didn’t simply create another fan or scenting device. The airflow and scent-distribution system came out of years spent pursuing the same end result the customer wants: a room that smells great, feels great, and is genuinely pleasurable to be in.
Proven Inventor Track Record:
 David also has an established history as an inventor and entrepreneur. He invented Shark Wheel, has developed other inventions, and previously appeared on Shark Tank, where Shark Wheel secured an on-air deal with three Sharks. His background reinforces that Spin360 comes from someone with a demonstrated history of developing unconventional product mechanisms.
Authority in One Sentence:
David spent years pursuing the ideal indoor atmosphere — backed by a proven track record as an inventor — leading him to develop Spin360’s natural-feeling airflow and a new way to distribute scent through the room.


6. Awareness Level
Primary Awareness Level:
 Solution-Aware
The prospect already wants their home to smell luxurious and already knows traditional scenting solutions such as candles, sprays, plug-ins, and diffusers.
They do not yet know that air movement and scent distribution can offer a different way to satisfy that desire — while also improving how the room physically feels.
Awareness in One Sentence:
They already know scenting solutions exist. They don’t yet know Spin360 offers a different mechanism: scent distribution + natural-feeling airflow.




7. Emotion / Valence & Intensity
Primary Emotional Direction:
Positive Valence + Medium Intensity
The ad should create desire, sensory pleasure, aspiration, and comfort — making the prospect vividly imagine how luxurious their home could smell and how good the atmosphere could feel.
Some creatives can begin with mild frustration around existing scenting solutions, but the dominant emotional movement should be toward the desired experience.
Emotion in One Sentence:
Make them vividly want the atmosphere Spin360 creates.




8. Differentiation / Positioning

 Not just a scenting product — a system designed to change the entire atmosphere of the room.

 Many traditional scenting products primarily add fragrance from a fixed source.
Former Limitation:
 The scent can remain concentrated around that source, and the product itself does not change how the room physically feels.
Spin360 Difference:
 Spin360 helps distribute scent throughout the room while simultaneously creating natural-feeling airflow. Instead of only changing how the room smells, it also changes how the room physically feels.
Positioning → Desire:
Spin360 helps make the entire room smell luxurious while creating the natural-feeling airflow that makes the atmosphere feel better too.
Positioning in One Sentence:
Scent distribution + natural-feeling airflow — designed to change the entire atmosphere of the room.
