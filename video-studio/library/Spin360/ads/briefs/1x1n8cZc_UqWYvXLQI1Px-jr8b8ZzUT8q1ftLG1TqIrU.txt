[a]Hook 1 — “You and your wife are about to sleep better…”
Start with a husband and wife sleeping or relaxing together for about 0.8–1 second, then immediately cut to a Swedish baby sleeping outside in a stroller, with snow/cold clearly visible. This is the most literal and easiest to understand. The viewer sees: couple → baby in the snow. The spoken hook does the rest.
Hook 2 — “Your wife is missing something Swedish babies get every night.”
Open with a close-up of the wife sleeping or getting into bed, ideally with the husband slightly visible in frame, then make a fast cut to a row of strollers with babies sleeping outside. The visual should create the comparison: your wife ↔ Swedish babies. This one probably has the strongest WTF factor.
Hook 3 — “Your wife could feel more relaxed at night…”
Open with the wife looking deeply relaxed in a beautiful nighttime setting, maybe with her husband beside her and a slight breeze moving her hair or the sheets. Then cut to a Swedish baby sleeping outside. This is the most desire-led version: first show the outcome he wants for her, then open the curiosity gap.
