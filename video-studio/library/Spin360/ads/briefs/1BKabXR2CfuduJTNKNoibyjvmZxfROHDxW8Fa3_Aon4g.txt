SPIN360 EDITING BRIEF
Ad ID: Spin360_RealComment_AC74
Use the provided final audio as-is and build the visual edit around it.
________________


Ad Concept
Real Customer Comment → Product Proof
David reads a real customer comment from his phone.
The customer’s husband did not like the setup and thought they would return Spin360. But after using it, he changed his mind and loved it.
The customer also says they can keep the AC at 74°F because the room feels like 67–68°F.
The comment gives David a natural reason to explain why Spin360 makes the room feel more comfortable.
The story is:
He doubted it. He tried it. He felt the difference. Now they are keeping it.
________________


Visual Hook Variations
Create three visual hook versions.
Use the same opening audio and the same main edit in all three versions.
Only change the opening visuals, captions, framing, punch-ins, and sound design.
________________


Hook 1 — Raw Founder
Start directly on David looking at his phone.
Keep the framing simple and natural.
Use normal captions.
When David says the comment is real, briefly highlight:
THIS IS A REAL COMMENT.
Use a small punch-in on David.
The opening should feel honest and unplanned.
________________


Hook 2 — Phone Detail
Start with the closest real shot available of:
* David holding the phone.
* David tapping or scrolling.
* David looking down at the screen.
* A close shot of his hand and phone.
Then cut to David’s face.
Use normal captions and briefly highlight:
REAL CUSTOMER COMMENT
Do not create a fake review screenshot.
The goal is to make the viewer curious about what the customer wrote.
________________


Hook 3 — Result Teaser
While David begins reading the comment, briefly show the result that comes later:
AC SET TO 74°F
 “FEELS LIKE 67–68°F”
Add a small label:
Customer experience
Use a real thermostat shot or a simple temperature graphic.
Then return to David holding his phone.
Do not show the thermostat changing from 74°F to 67°F.
The goal is to make the viewer wonder:
How did Spin360 make the room feel that much cooler?
________________


Hook Editing Rule
Each version should have a different feeling:
* Hook 1: raw and honest.
* Hook 2: personal and real.
* Hook 3: result and curiosity.
After the first few seconds, continue into the same main edit.
________________


Editing Style
Use a raw founder-video style mixed with a simple product explainer.
The beginning should feel honest and real.
The middle should become more visual so the viewer can easily understand the temperature and airflow idea.
The ending should feel calm and premium.
Do not make the whole ad fast, loud, or flashy.
David should stay on screen when his face, gestures, or reaction are the strongest visual.
Use B-roll when it helps explain the idea.
Do not cover every sentence with B-roll.
________________


Story Flow
Part 1 — The Real Comment
Start with David holding his phone and reading the comment.
Use normal captions.
When he says the comments are real, briefly highlight:
REAL CUSTOMER COMMENT
When he talks about the small screw and says:
“But God, did that suck.”
Use a small punch-in.
Keep this moment funny and honest.
A real close-up of the screw or setup part can be used if the correct footage is available.
Do not show the wrong product part.
The viewer should understand:
David admits the old setup problem and says he fixed it.
________________


Part 2 — The Customer Changes Her Mind
When David reads:
“It’s a win, and I love it.”
Highlight the caption:
IT’S A WIN, AND I LOVE IT.
When David reads:
“We’ve been able to keep our AC on 74 degrees because it makes it feel like 67, 68.”
Make this the first major proof moment.
Show:
AC SET TO 74°F
 “FEELS LIKE 67–68°F”
Add:
Customer experience
Possible visuals:
* A thermostat set to 74°F.
* Spin360 working in a bedroom or living room.
* A person looking cool and comfortable.
* An AC vent.
* Curtains, clothes, hair, or bedding moving softly.
Do not show the thermostat dropping from 74°F to 67°F.
The customer is describing how the room feels.
________________


Part 3 — Explain Why It Feels Cooler
When David explains the temperature difference between the floor and ceiling, use a simple room graphic.
Show:
80°F — NEAR THE CEILING
 74°F — NEAR THE FLOOR
Then show Spin360 mixing the air.
Use simple, smooth arrows.
The graphic should be easy to understand in one second.
Do not make it look like a science class.
When David says:
“We make it all one temperature.”
Show the warm and cool air layers becoming more even.
The viewer should understand:
The AC already made cold air. Spin360 helps move that air through more of the room.
________________


Part 4 — He Expected to Return It
When David reads:
“My husband kept the box in pristine condition… because he was sure we were sending it back.”
Stay on David or use a real packaging shot if one is available.
Do not use generic return footage.
Do not create fake Spin360 packaging.
When David says:
“Nope! It’s here to stay.”
Use a stronger punch-in.
Let the music lift slightly.
Show:
NOPE.
 IT’S HERE TO STAY.
This is the emotional payoff.
The viewer should understand:
He expected to return it. Then he used it and changed his mind.
________________


Part 5 — Spin360 Is Not a Normal Fan
When David begins explaining why Spin360 is different, show the real product clearly.
When he says:
“Every other fan in the world is whap, whap, whap.”
Use three quick cuts that match each “whap.”
Possible visuals:
* A regular fan blade spinning.
* A narrow stream of air.
* Hair, paper, or fabric being hit by a hard blast.
* A ribbon twisting in front of a regular fan.
The regular fan should feel mechanical, narrow, and harsh.
Then slow the edit down when David says:
“This fan was created to create an outdoor feeling indoors.”
Use softer visuals:
* A curtain moving gently.
* Hair moving lightly.
* Clothing moving in a soft breeze.
* A wide room shot with Spin360.
* Leaves moving outside.
* Soft movement inside the room.
The contrast should be clear:
Regular fan: hard, narrow blast.
 Spin360: soft, wide breeze.
________________


Shot Selection
Use the real shot that explains each line most clearly.
Good options include:
* David speaking.
* David holding the phone.
* David looking at the phone.
* Real Spin360 close-ups.
* Full product shots.
* Spin360 inside a bedroom or living room.
* Thermostat footage.
* AC vents.
* Curtains, clothes, hair, or fabric moving.
* Simple airflow graphics.
* Real lifestyle footage.
Do not force a new shot for every sentence.
Stay on David when his face or delivery is stronger than the B-roll.
Avoid generic footage that makes the ad feel fake.
________________
Captions and Text
Use normal captions through the ad.
Do not show the full customer review.
Highlight only the strongest moments:
THIS IS A REAL COMMENT.
IT’S A WIN, AND I LOVE IT.
AC SET TO 74°F
“FEELS LIKE 67–68°F”
Customer experience
NOPE. IT’S HERE TO STAY.
Keep the text large, clean, and easy to read on a phone.
Do not fill the screen with long paragraphs.
________________


Sound and Music
Start with light music.
Keep some natural outdoor sound around David.
Use small sound details only when they help:
* A phone tap.
* A light click during the screw section.
* Three short impacts for “whap, whap, whap.”
* A soft breeze sound when the outdoor-breeze idea begins.
Do not use loud whooshes on every transition.
Let the music grow slightly when the husband changes his mind and when the product difference becomes clear.
________________


Pacing
Beginning
Simple, natural, and honest.
Do not over-edit the opening.
Middle
Use more product shots and simple graphics to explain the AC and temperature difference.
Ending
Slow the visual pace down.
Make Spin360 feel calm, comfortable, and different from a normal fan.
________________


Ending
Use the strongest complete moment near the end for:
* A clean Spin360 product shot.
* Spin360 inside a room.
* The current offer.
* The logo.
* A clear CTA.
Possible final text:
BRING THE OUTDOOR BREEZE INDOORS.
________________


Final Editing Goal
The finished ad should feel like:
A real customer story that naturally becomes a simple product demonstration.
Keep the customer story believable.
Make the 74°F versus 67–68°F result easy to understand.
Show why the husband changed his mind.
Then show that Spin360 is not just another fan. It creates a softer, wider, outdoor-like breeze inside the room.
