	Hook v1
	If you're lying there at midnight going over tomorrow's list again, this is what finally turned my brain off.
	

	

	It wasn't a new routine. It was a room where the air moves, like a porch on a summer night.
	

	

	Every night it was the dentist on Thursday, the email I forgot to send, whether I'd paid the water bill. My body was tired and my brain was clocking in.
	

	

	I tried writing the list down before bed. I tried the 4-7-8 breathing exercise. I tried a podcast and just lay there listening.
	

	

	Then my daughter-in-law said their bedroom has a fan running all year, and I said I'm sixty, I don't sleep with a fan blowing on me.
	

	

	But she said this one doesn't blow on you, and the first night I got as far as the water bill and I don't remember what came after.
	

	

	And by the end of the week the list just didn't start.
	

	

	It's a fan, and I know how that sounds, but let me explain.
	

	

	It doesn't blow at you like a normal fan, it moves the air in the whole room.
	

	

	So it feels like the window's open. Like the porch on a summer night.
	

	

	And when the whole room moves like that, my body finally gets that it's night, and it lets go of the day.
	

	

	And the sound helps too. It's silent on the low speeds, and on the higher ones it makes this soft steady shhh that never changes.
	

	

	So there's nothing to listen for, and my brain can finally shut up.
	

	

	And the best part? You get 30 days to send it back, and they don't ask why.
