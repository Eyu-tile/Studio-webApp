More than half of Americans sleep with their windows closed at night. But the number one reason isn’t the weather.


During the day, a lot of us love having the windows open. The air moves through the house, and the whole place just feels more open, more alive.  
But then bedtime comes. You start thinking about safety, street noise, bugs, dust… so you close the windows, and suddenly the air goes still. The room just doesn’t feel the same.




That exact problem is what one inventor spent four years trying to solve: how do you keep your windows closed at night, but still make your bedroom feel like they’re open?
And this is what he came up with.
It’s called Spin360, and it’s basically an open-window simulator for your bedroom. It recreates that soft, natural movement of air you normally feel with an open window, spreading it around the whole room. So even with the windows closed, you still get that open, airy feeling.
The reason is simple: instead of chopping air straight at you like a normal fan, Spin360 constantly pulls air from above and below, mixes it together, and its unique blade pushes it back out in a full 360 degrees. 
Think about someone gently fanning you with a magazine. You feel that soft, natural breeze across your skin. It creates that same kind of feeling, except it spreads it around your whole bedroom. 
So you can close the windows, lock them, keep the noise, bugs, dust, and everything else outside… while keeping that natural movement of air inside.
