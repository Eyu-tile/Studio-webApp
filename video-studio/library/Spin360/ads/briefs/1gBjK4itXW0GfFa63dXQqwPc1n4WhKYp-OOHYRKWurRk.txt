	Strategy
# Element
	Answer
	Desire
	"I want to sleep like I used to." He wants his bedroom to finally feel right at night: fresh air, no stuffiness, no fan blasting his face, so he and his wife sleep through and both wake up rested. Underneath: being the guy who found the smart fix for the house, not another gadget.
	Avatar
	Men 45–65, homeowners, buying for the bedroom they share with their wife (real buyers: "Got my wife one. She loves it", "I have two… so perfect in the bedroom"). They like knowing how things work and are skeptical of gadgets and price ("pricey, but… worth it"). Behavior: they keep throwing fans at the problem (a box fan pointed at the bed, the ceiling fan, the AC, even "four fans surrounding our bed") and the room still feels stuffy or uneven. Belief to respect: "a fan is a fan, it just blows air at you."
	Angle
	Proof it works: three thermometers, top 78° and bottom 71°; with Spin360 on, all three read 73°. One temperature with a real breeze, so the bedroom finally feels like outside, at the temperature of your own home.
	Mechanism
	New mechanism (patented with all claims): you don't point it at you, you point it up. It pulls air in from the top and the bottom and blows it out 360°, so ceiling air and floor air mix into one temperature with a gentle breeze. Transparency: the first prototype "was garbage" until they found this.
	Proof
	Falsifiable proof (can be checked): David's real temperature test, 78° at the top and 71° at the bottom, then 73° on all three gauges, plus the patent granted with all claims.
	Authority
	Title + transparency: the founder who reinvented the wheel on Shark Tank, the patent with all claims, and honesty about the prototype that failed. Shown before the pitch.
	Awareness
	Hook: Unaware (pattern interrupt, no product). Body takes him from "my bedroom just feels off" (Problem Aware) to "a fan built differently fixes it" (Solution Aware). The fan market is sophisticated (stage 3–4), so his objection is "how is yours different?" and the mechanism and the 3-gauge proof carry the ad.
	Emotion
	Curiosity (what is this guy doing?) → "that's clever" (he feels like the smart buyer who gets the engineering) → conviction (the numbers) → calm (sleeping outside, at home temperature).
	Format
	Voice-over string-out of David from the Comments/QA shoot, like our "Founder reacts to comments" ad (reference below), but we do NOT show or read comments. Mostly full-screen B-roll with his voice; David on screen when it helps.
	Hooks
	3 drill hooks in realistic public places (another city street, a stadium tailgate, a street fair), filmed like a stranger caught it on their phone.
	Offer
	Risk reversal: 30 nights to try it. If you don't love it, send it back. No fake scarcity.
