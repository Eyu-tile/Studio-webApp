Demographic: US moms, 25 to 38, baby 0 to 12 months.
	3 Angle
	Sealed nursery air keeps the baby waking every 2 hours
	4 Mechanism
	Problem: with the door and the window closed, the air in the nursery gets stuck; every breath out (used-up air, CO₂) hangs around the baby's face and gets breathed back in, so the baby keeps waking up.
Solution: Spin360 pulls air in from the top and the bottom and sends it out all around (360°), so the air around the crib keeps moving like outside; no harsh wind, just a soft, gentle breeze, and silent.
	5 Authority
	1) The Nordic tradition: babies nap outside, even in the snow, and sleep twice as long; 2) simple breathing science: every breath out is used-up air.
Authority comes from the explanation inside the song: no expert or doctor on screen.
	6 Awareness
	Problem-aware: the first line calls out the 2 a.m. wake-up; she has no idea the cause is the stuck air in the nursery.
Strange fact: 'It's not your baby. It's the air.' + Nordic babies napping in the snow.
	7 Emotion
	  

Opens: recognition and laughter (the exhausted 2 a.m. mom, crying with her baby).
Mid: realization and worry: the baby breathing his own used-up air.
Ends: relief, joy and tenderness: 'Mama does too', the healthy 10-year-old, 'Mama's back to being Mama'.
	8 Positioning
	Enemy: the stuck air in the nursery, and the gadgets that never touch it (swaddle, sound machine, blackout curtains, a regular fan that blasts the baby), and the AC and the purifier, which cool and clean the air but never move it around the crib.
First and only: an air circulator made for babies' rooms that keeps the air around the crib gently moving, like outside, without ever blowing on the baby.
