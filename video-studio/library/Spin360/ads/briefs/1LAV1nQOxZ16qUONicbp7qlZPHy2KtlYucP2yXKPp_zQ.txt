[3] Art Direction — Read This Before Generating A Single Frame 
This ad lives or dies on whether the footage feels real. Generic AI imagery will kill the credibility of the narrator story. The goal is every frame passes as archival or documentary footage — never generated. 
GLOBAL RULES (apply to every scene): 

* NO AI glow. No plastic skin. No clean perfect edges. No HDR look. If it looks like AI, regenerate it. 
* NO ONE looks at the camera. Ever. The camera observes life. It does not stage it. 
* Scenes feel FOUND, not produced. Like a documentary crew happened to be there. 
* Real textures everywhere — worn fabric, imperfect surfaces, natural imperfections. 
* Natural light only in historical scenes. No studio lighting. No fill light. 
* Quality standard: every frame must pass as a still pulled from a real BBC or PBS documentary.

WORLD 1 — Victorian Era / Open Air Schools Hook + Unaware Section 


● Film stock: 35mm, faded and slightly desaturated. Think archival photographs from 1900–1920.     


	● Color palette: Muted sepia tones. Warm but faded — like an old photograph left in the sun.
	● Camera movement: Ken Burns style — slow, gentle pans and zooms across still or near-still images.
	● Subjects: Women teachers in long dresses and high collars. Children in period clothing — wool coats, buttoned shirts. No modern elements anywhere in frame.
  



	● Environment: Outdoor classroom settings. Simple wooden desks outside. Open fields or forests in background. Simple wooden schoolhouse buildings.
  

	● Children napping: Outdoors on simple cots or blankets. Bundled but outside. Peaceful.
  



	● Textures: Worn wool, rough wood, soft grass. Everything has age and texture.
	● Light: Soft, overcast, diffused natural light. Never harsh or dramatic.
	● Grain: Visible film grain throughout. Slightly faded edges.
	● What NOT to generate: Modern anything. AI smoothness. Perfect skin. Staged poses. Anyone looking at camera.
	



WORLD 2 — Sweden / Swedish Babies Swedish Babies Section 


Use footage provided from Gabriel’s VSL 
	● Film stock: 35mm, Kodak Portra 400. This is the IDENTICAL art direction to the Swedish Babies ad that is already running.
	● Color palette: Cool blue-gray exterior. 6500K color temperature. Desaturated Scandinavian winter palette.
	● Snow: Dirty packed snow — NOT fresh powder. Real, lived-in snow.
	● Strollers: Vintage Scandinavian style prams. Seen from outside or above. No baby faces visible — non-negotiable for both creative and Meta moderation.
	● Environment: Quiet Scandinavian streets. Stone buildings. Café exteriors. Nordic architecture.
	● People: Bundled in practical winter clothing. No parents hovering near the strollers — the scene should feel like parents have gone inside.
	● Textures: Worn wool with pills. Smudged glass with condensation. Frost on metal. Real cold.
	● Light: Flat overcast Nordic winter sky. No dramatic lighting. Everything feels cold and grey and beautiful.
	● What NOT to generate: Baby faces. Warm sunny scenes. Modern clothing. Anything that looks staged or posed.
	

WORLD 3 — Modern Sealed Bedroom Bridge + Behavior Section 


● Hard contrast to Worlds 1 and 2. This should feel uncomfortable after the beauty of the previous scenes.
	● Color palette: Warm but stale. Slightly overexposed tungsten light. Yellowish and heavy.
	● Camera style: Observational. Almost surveillance-like. Static shots. No movement.
	● Subject: Woman, mid 40s to 50s. Real looking — not model perfect. Actually tired.
	● Scenes: Lying awake staring at ceiling. Alarm going off, heavy-eyed. Sitting on edge of bed in the morning, head in hands. AC unit humming on wall. Ceiling fan barely moving. Air that looks still.
  
  

	● The stillness of the air should be VISIBLE — nothing moves in frame. No curtains. No fabric movement. Dead air.
	● Textures: Generic modern bedroom. Nothing special or aspirational about it. This is the problem.
	● Light: Too warm. Uncomfortable. Not cozy — stuffy.
	● What NOT to generate: Dramatic or cinematic lighting. Beautiful or aspirational bedrooms. Anyone who looks comfortable.

[7] Editing Rules 
● DO NOT rush. This ad earns its watch-through with pacing and atmosphere.

● Music transitions with the emotional arc — slightly melancholic in historical sections, resolving to warmth at product reveal.
