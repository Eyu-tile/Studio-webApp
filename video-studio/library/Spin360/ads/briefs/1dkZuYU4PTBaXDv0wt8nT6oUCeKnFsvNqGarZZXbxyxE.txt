[2] Script




This is one of the easiest ways to make your home feel expensive.
Forget decor for a second.
This matters more.
People don’t just notice what your home looks like when they walk in.
They notice how it feels.
And scent is a huge part of that.
A lot of people think luxury home scenting has to be complicated — candles everywhere, sprays, or another diffuser sitting in the corner.
It doesn’t.
This air circulator has a scent pod system built right in.
Just pop in a scent pod, turn it on, and as it creates a soft, steady breeze around the room, that moving air helps carry the fragrance throughout the space.
So instead of one corner smelling good, the whole room gets that clean, elevated, five-star hotel feeling — with a gentle breeze moving through the room at the same time.
It’s one of those things people notice without even knowing why your home feels so put together.
If you want your home to smell more luxurious and feel better the moment someone walks in, try Spin360.














 
















HOOK VARIATIONS
Format: Voiceover
Spoken hook stays the same across all versions.
Spoken Hook:
 “This is one of the easiest ways to make your home feel expensive.”
________________


V1 — Desired Outcome
Text Hook:
 MAKE YOUR HOME
 FEEL EXPENSIVE
Visual Hook:
 Open on a sophisticated, elevated room with immediate camera or subject motion → quick cut to the front door opening and someone entering the home → soft premium curtains moving subtly from the airflow.
Goal:
 Sell the desired outcome immediately: a home that feels elevated, polished, and expensive.
________________


V2 — Contrarian / More Than Decor
Text Hook:
 LUXURY HOME FEEL
 MORE THAN DECOR
Visual Hook:
 Open on an extreme close-up of an expensive-looking decor detail — ceramic vase, coffee-table book, marble, or another premium object → hard cut away from the decor → scent pod inserted into Spin360 → wide reveal of the elevated room.
Goal:
 Create a knowledge gap around the idea that the luxury-home feeling comes from more than decor, setting up: “Forget decor for a second. This matters more.”
________________


V3 — Sensory Mechanism
Text Hook:
 SCENT CHANGES HOW
 YOUR HOME FEELS
Visual Hook:
 Show a guest entering the home → subtle pause and positive reaction to the space → hard cut to Spin360 being turned on → curtains or fabric moving softly from the airflow.
Goal:
 Communicate the invisible sensory mechanism behind why a home can feel more elevated the moment someone walks in.
