VO line
	Source
	“New York, 1997”
	AP: happened May 10, 1997, at a restaurant in Manhattan’s East Village (Seattle Times/AP, 1998)
	“A mom from Denmark leaves her baby asleep in a stroller. Right outside a restaurant window.”
	AP: Danish actress, 14-month-old asleep in a stroller “in front of the restaurant window” (Seattle Times/AP, 1998; HeraldNet/AP, 2017)
	“Someone inside calls 911.”
	AP: “a 911 call” (Deseret/AP, 1999) and “a patron summoned police” (HeraldNet/AP, 2017)
	“She spends two nights in jail. Her baby spends four days in foster care.”
	AP, 1998: two nights in jail and four days in foster care (Seattle Times/AP)
	“The charges? Dropped.”
	AP: “charges that were eventually dropped” (HeraldNet/AP, 2017)
	“Back home in Denmark, that’s just how babies nap.”
	Snopes: “It’s common to leave your baby napping outside while you go shopping or sit in a cafe” (Snopes)
