Spin360_NatureBreeze_Winter
Brief - Spin360_NatureBreeze_Winter.mp4
One ad, vertical 9:16. No voiceover: music and short on-screen text only. Three sequences: (1) nature, generated, no product, to bring back the feeling of a real breeze; (2) the closed winter room, built by you; (3) the Spin360 in nature, then a fade to people sleeping with it in the bedroom. The images are inspiration, not a storyboard. The on-screen text is final. 3 hooks: the video is the same, only the first text changes (see Hooks).
	

The idea: take people back to a real outdoor breeze (goosebumps), show winter closing every window, then turn the room back into outside with the Spin360.
	The emotion curve
  
Music follows the curve: it builds in Act 1, almost disappears in Act 2, and comes back bigger in Act 3.

	Answer
	Why this ad
	"It feels like my bed is outside" is a proven message for us (BedOutside_Hook_1: $53k, ROAS 3.21; BedOutside_Hook_4: ROAS 4.34; NatureBreeze_DavidVO: $47k). This ad tells it with no voice, only images, music and short text, like the perfume ad in the reference: real nature, and the product placed inside it.
	Desire
	To feel a real outdoor breeze while you sleep, even in winter with every window shut.
	Sub-avatar
	People who love sleeping with the windows open and lose that in winter (they crack the window "just for air", then the cold or their partner closes it).
	Angle
	The feeling of an open window, with the windows closed.
	Awareness
	Unaware / problem aware. No product talk until Act 3.
	Emotion (the curve)
	Up (the breeze, nostalgia) → down (winter shuts the windows, still air) → up higher (the room becomes outside). Valence and intensity drive this ad, not words.
	Proof / authority
	David's real line as on-screen text: "You'd swear the walls were removed and your bed is outside."
	End result
	You fall asleep without noticing and wake up actually rested. This is the deep benefit; we sell the feeling, never cooling.
	Offer
	30 nights to try it. No discount.
	Production brief
Format: 9:16, no voice. Cuts of 1–2 s like the reference. The 7 Act 1 clips are ready: 5 s each, 1080p, no product. Each one is linked in the shot list; use the best 1–2 s of each. Act 1 has no product at all: it only brings back the feeling.
The product moment: the first time we see the Spin360 it is inside nature (shot 11), like the perfume in the reference. Then a soft fade into the bedroom, and we see people sleeping with it.
Look: premium, real, slow, shallow depth of field, natural light. Act 1 warm and green; Act 2 cold, blue, dark, nothing moves; Act 3 warm again, and the air visibly moves (curtain, hair, grass).
On-screen text: white, clean, centered, few words, on screen long enough to read once (about 2–3 s each). Exactly as in the table, in this order. It must match what is on screen at that moment.
Music: emotional, cinematic. Build in Act 1, drop to almost nothing in Act 2, return bigger in Act 3.
Rules: the Spin360 looks exactly like the real product (generate it from our product reference videos); it never heats or glows red; no Christmas decor; breeze is always positive.
