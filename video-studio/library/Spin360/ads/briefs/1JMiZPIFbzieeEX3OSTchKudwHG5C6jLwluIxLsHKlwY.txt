4.  Strategic breakdown
Why each section exists and what it is meant to make her feel.
HOOK  (0:00–0:08)  ·  Surprised — positive, high intensity
The strange fact with no product in sight. The second line — “and the babies slept better than they ever did inside” — is what turns the valence: it stops being horror and becomes curiosity within three seconds. This is the fix for the original, which stayed in shock and got clicks instead of buyers.
STORY  (0:08–0:45)  ·  curiosity holds
Third-person documentary. The story is never contradicted: no “the method was insane,” no “they had the reason half wrong.” The YouTube comments on this same footage show the audience defends the practice — the original was arguing with its own viewers. Here it escalates instead: companies, cities, Eleanor Roosevelt, doctors. Five micro loops open and close along the way; the big one holds.
THE REVEAL  (0:45–0:58)  ·  Recognition — positive, medium intensity
She is not accused of anything. “You know how good a breeze feels when you sleep with a window open” — she recognises it in herself, and cannot argue with her own experience. Then “every night of your life, you've been sleeping in that same dead air” arrives as a consequence of the baby waking up, not as a warning. No knife: the middle of this ad is recognition, not fear.
BRIDGE  (0:58–1:02)  ·  the turn
“Now here's the part that has nothing to do with babies” lands immediately after the payoff, before she can think “nice story, anyway.” The engineer arrives as the person who has the answer she just realised she needs.
PRODUCT  (1:02–2:40)  ·  climbing to Relaxed
Sensation before name: the breeze, the double take, the walls disappearing. The name and the patent land after she has felt it. Every benefit opens the next one, so the section never becomes a list. The babies come back to kill the objection: they weren't sleeping in front of a fan, they were sleeping in moving air.
CLOSE  (2:40–end)  ·  Relaxed — positive, low intensity
Authority, then social proof, then the price — which arrives after Shark Tank and 35,000 units, so $189 sounds small. Soft CTA, no urgency spike. The ad ends in the state the product sells.
