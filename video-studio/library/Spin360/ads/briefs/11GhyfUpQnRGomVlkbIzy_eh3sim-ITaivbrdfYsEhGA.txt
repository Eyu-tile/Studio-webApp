[4] Creative Direction 


Hook Format (TEXT ON SCREEN ONLY — 0 to 5 sec): 
Raw, authentic TikTok-style text. White text, black background or overlaid on footage. No voiceover during hook. The text IS the hook. Should feel like a real person posting about their experience, not an ad. 
Emotional Arc:
Start with heat misery / AC problem → moment of discovery → relief and disbelief → mechanism explanation (why it works) → 'this is basically AC' payoff → CTA. 
Key Creative Insight from Account Data: 
Older women (45–65+) respond strongly to feeling-first, calm, sensory-driven ads. This angle hits both: the problem is visceral (sweating, can't sleep) and the solution is equally sensory (waking up shivering, room feels alive). Don't over-explain — let the feeling sell it. 
Competitive Positioning: 
Don't compare to other fans. Compare to AC. Frame it as: 'I thought I needed AC. Turns out I just needed the right airflow.'
