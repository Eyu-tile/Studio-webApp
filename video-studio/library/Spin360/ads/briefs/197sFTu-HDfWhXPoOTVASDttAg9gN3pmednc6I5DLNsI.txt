	Strategy
# Element
	Answer
	Why this ad
	People who already know the Spin360 need a push, not an explanation. Same format as our creator ad (watch it) and Spin360_CrazySaleWinter: one person, the Spin360 site scrolling behind, one line of text, music. New line and new scenes so it tests as a new ad.
	What it's about
	She found the one fan you keep on all winter, and it's on sale. The text says it and the picture shows it: she points at the Spin360 running next to her in a cozy winter room. It answers the biggest winter doubt (a fan in winter?) and gives the reason to buy now.
	How we make it
	Green screen style like the reference: the person in front, a screen recording of the Spin360 site scrolling behind, the text on top. About 10 s, vertical 9:16, no voice.
	Sub-avatar
	People who have seen our ads or visited the site and haven't bought yet (bottom of funnel).
	Awareness
	Most aware. They know the product. The job is a reason to buy now.
	Angle
	The fan you can use all winter, and it's on sale right now.
	Emotion
	Fun and a little FOMO. Light.
	Offer
	The current sale, shown on the site in the background. No end date on screen unless the sale really has one.
	Production brief
Format: vertical 9:16, about 10 s, no voice. Like the reference: the person in front, a screen recording of the Spin360 site scrolling slowly behind (product page, the sale banner visible), the text in big white letters with a black outline over the middle.
Music: one upbeat track, the same in all 3 (different from CrazySaleWinter and LeavingEverythingSale, so it does not feel like the same ad).
The scenes: generated or real, your call. Phone selfie, candid, like the reference. Not an ad. Winter in every scene, but no Christmas or holiday decor (it is not the holidays yet), and the Spin360 running and visible in every scene. It is the only fan on screen.
