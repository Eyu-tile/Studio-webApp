[4] Body of the ad


It is such a nice breeze in the entire room. The entire — it's like somebody lifted my house up and I'm outside with the most beautiful breeze, but it's the temperature of the inside of my house. It can be raining outside, I got a wonderful breeze inside with the right 72-degree temperature.
When you feel it while you're working, you don't notice there's a fan hitting you. You just feel like there's a breeze in the room.
And the way we created our fan: it sucks really hard from the top and the bottom, and then blows out three 360s in all directions — kind of like Saturn's rings. What we've made is something that is very analogous to a breeze. It doesn't feel like a fan.
When you sleep with this, you're mixing the air in the room better than if the room is stagnant. And it is virtually dead silent.
We also have a cotton pad there that you can put essential oils on, or you could go and spray something above it, and in 10 seconds the whole room smells like it. It is incredible how well it does scenting. This is a scent distribution system as much as it's a fan.
I've launched products before, such as the Shark Wheel, but this is my absolute biggest home run. We've done something that — it's simple, it's never been done before, and it solves more problems than we can count.
