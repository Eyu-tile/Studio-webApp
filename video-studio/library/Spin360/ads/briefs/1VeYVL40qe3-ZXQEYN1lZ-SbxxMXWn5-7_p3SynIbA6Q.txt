[1] Strategy


1 · The eight elements
1
Desire
	Surface desire: “I want my bedroom to feel better / more comfortable.”
Core Desire: Comfort. 


	2
Avatar
	Primary: U.S. women 45+, strongest signal in 55–65+.
Demographic evidence: Swedish Babies skewed heavily female, especially 45–64, with meaningful 65+. Sleeping Porch reinforced this even harder: 87% of spend went to women, concentrated primarily in 65+, 55–64 and 45–54; all 4 recorded results were women.
Experiences / behaviors:
* Strong affinity for open windows, porches, outdoor sleeping, breezes and moving air.
* Many already sleep with fans, open/cracked windows, or both, including in winter. Swedish comments repeatedly describe needing air movement to sleep.
* Often likes the contrast of being cozy/warm under blankets while cool air moves around the room.
* Some explicitly distinguish air movement from cooling — they want to feel the air move, not simply lower the temperature.
* Strong nostalgia / generational memory: grandparents’ houses, sleeping porches, childhood summers, open windows, attic fans, rain on roofs, nighttime sounds and outdoor smells.
* Sleeping Porch comments repeatedly describe these experiences as “best sleep ever,” “magical,” “wonderful,” and among their favorite childhood memories.
* She may want open-air sleeping but modern life gets in the way: crime/safety, humidity, bugs, noise and air quality.
* She may want moving air while disliking a concentrated fan blast directly on her face/body.
Psychographic: She cares a lot about how a room feels. She associates moving air with comfort, home, nature, nostalgia and better-feeling sleep environments. She isn't necessarily looking for “a stronger fan”; she wants to recreate the feeling of sleeping with the window open or on a porch, while still being comfortable and protected indoors.
Core Avatar Insight:
She doesn't just miss cooler air. She misses the feeling of sleeping with the world slightly open around her.




	3
Angle
	Still air makes the bedroom feel less comfortable.
	4
Mechanism
	Spin360 pulls air in from above and below and sends it outward in 360°, and the unique blade shape gives the airflow a softer, more natural feel — a gentle breeze moving around the whole room instead of a stream hitting one spot.
	5
Awareness
	Solution aware
	

6
Positioning
	ENEMY
The sealed, still room — and the regular fan that answers it with a hard stream hitting the same spot.
FIRST AND ONLY
Not a stronger fan: a different way to move air through a room — softer, broader, more like a natural breeze.
