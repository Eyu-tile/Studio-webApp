	Strategy
# Element
	Answer
	Desire
	Fall asleep earlier, in a cool, comfortable room, with a steady sound.
	Avatar
	Women around 55–65 whose bedtime keeps sliding past midnight. The fan was a gift from their daughter.
	Angle
	The fan that has her asleep before midnight again.
	Mechanism
	A 360 fan that moves cool air all around the room as a gentle breeze, not a blast in your face. Levels 6 to 9 give a steady white noise for sleeping.
	Authority
	A 58-year-old woman talking about her own nights, plus concrete specs (9 speeds, 6 to 9 for sleep).
	Awareness
	Hook: Problem Aware (she knows she goes to bed too late). Body: Product Aware.
	Emotion
	Recognition ("that's me") in the hook, then calm and relief.
	Positioning
	The bedtime fan: a cool room and a steady sound.
	Format
	Silent POV text-on-screen opening (Reference A), then a voiceover over simple product shots (Reference B). Why: Reference A has our best hook rate (27%) and Reference B our best ROAS, so we keep the hook that stops the scroll and the body that sells.
	Hooks
	3 visual hooks. Same on-screen text and same body in all three; only the picture of the first 7 seconds changes (room and camera angle), so we learn which picture stops the scroll.
	Offer
	30 days to try it. If she doesn't love it, she sends it back.
	Production brief
Format: vertical 9:16, about 36 s. It combines two of our ads: the silent POV opening of T3_Before_Midnight_Fan_Sound (Reference A) and the voiceover product demo of DontHaveAC (Reference B).
Look: night, dark bedroom, the Spin360's blue ring as the main light. Real, simple, filmed-at-home feel, like both references.
Big idea: a 58-year-old woman who finally falls asleep before midnight, thanks to the fan her daughter sent her.
Cast & set: voiceover by a woman who sounds 55–65, like the 58-year-old in the hook, at a natural pace (not rushed). Bedrooms that look like the home of a woman that age. The Spin360 always stands on the floor.
