The idea
She can't sleep without a fan on, and it is for the sound, not the air. In the hook the fan dies, and the silence wakes her up. When it gets cold she doesn't turn it off: she turns it to the wall and keeps it on just for the sound. Then it starts to rattle. So she buys another one, and another. All of them were built to cool a room, not to put her to sleep. Then we cut to Spin360 in a warm, calm bedroom: it sits on the floor next to her bed and makes a deep, steady hum night after night. There is no wind in her face, just a soft breeze moving around the room. We close on the 30-night trial.
The main moment is the sound switch in the middle (around 0:28). The rattling fan cuts off and the steady Spin360 hum comes in. Everything before it feels cold and annoying, and everything after it feels warm and calm. Build the edit around that moment.
