Spin360 — Racing Thoughts / “Something for Your Mind to Settle Into”
1. Desire
Surface Desire: Fall asleep easily at night, even when your mind won’t stop thinking.
Why Train: Fall asleep without lying awake thinking → get more continuous sleep → wake up actually rested → have more energy, patience, and mental clarity during the day.
Core Desire: Rest / Relief / Control
________________


2. Avatar
People who are physically tired at night, but whose brain seems to switch back on the moment they get into bed.
Experiences: replaying conversations, thinking about tomorrow, running through to-do lists, work, money, family, unfinished tasks, random memories, or thoughts that become much more noticeable once the bedroom gets quiet.
At the same time, small external sounds can suddenly stand out more — a car passing, a creak in the house, a partner moving, footsteps, a dog, or another little nighttime sound that pulls their attention back awake.
Core experience:
“My body is tired, but my brain won’t shut off.”
Behaviors: lies in bed trying to stop thinking, distracts themselves, scrolls, reads, watches something, or looks for ways to quiet their mind before sleep.
Some already use fans, white/brown noise, rain sounds, podcasts, audiobooks, music, TV, or sleep apps because having something steady in the background feels easier than complete silence.
Using background sound is not a requirement of the avatar. The avatar is defined by the problem: their mind stays active when they are trying to sleep.
________________


3. Angle
When your bedroom gets completely quiet, everything else feels louder. Even your own thoughts.
Complete silence can make two things more noticeable:
Internal noise: thoughts, worries, tomorrow’s tasks, memories, mental chatter.
External noise: every little sound in the room or outside it suddenly has more contrast and can grab attention.
Instead of trying to force the brain to stop thinking:
Give it something simple and steady to settle into.
________________


4. Mechanism
A steady sound for your mind. A soft breeze for your body.
Spin360 creates a consistent sensory environment around the sleeper.
Sound: a steady, predictable fan-generated background gives the mind something simple and non-demanding to settle into instead of complete silence.
That steady background can also reduce the contrast of small, changing nighttime sounds, making them less likely to stand out.
Airflow: Spin360 pulls air from above and below, mixes it, and distributes a soft breeze around the room rather than blasting one part of the body with a direct stream.
Together, sound and airflow create an environment that feels:
steady → gentle → predictable → easy to settle into
The mechanism is not:
“Spin360 shuts your brain off.”
The mechanism is:
“Spin360 creates a steady environment your mind and body can settle into.”
________________


5. Authority
David Patrick — inventor of Spin360, who spent years developing the blade and airflow system behind the product.
Secondary Authority: real consumers who independently describe fan noise and other steady background sounds as helping them calm an overactive mind, focus on something neutral, or sleep when silence makes thoughts and small sounds more noticeable.
Relevant consumer language includes:
“It keeps my mind busy.”
 “It calms my mind.”
 “I can focus on the sound.”
 “Without it, the thoughts come rushing back.”
 “It drowns out the mental chatter.”
 “Silence at night is deafening to me.”
________________


6. Awareness Level
Problem Aware → Solution Aware
Problem Aware: they know what happens when they go to bed.
Their body is tired, but their mind keeps going.
They might say:
“My brain won’t shut off.”
 “I start overthinking the second I get into bed.”
 “I’m exhausted, but I can’t stop thinking.”
 “The quieter it gets, the more I notice everything.”
They may not yet realize that a steady sensory background can create a more comfortable transition into sleep.
Solution Aware: they already know that having something consistent in the background — fan noise, white/brown noise, rain, podcasts, audiobooks, music, TV, etc. — can make sleep easier than complete silence.
The opportunity is to connect both groups to a new product experience:
Not just something you listen to — an entire bedroom environment to settle into.
________________


7. Emotion — Valence + Intensity
Negative Valence / Medium Intensity
Primary emotions: mental overload, restlessness, frustration, tension, irritation, and inability to let the day end.
Core emotional contradiction:
“I’m exhausted. Why am I still thinking?”
Desired emotional state:
relief, calm, comfort, surrender, mental quiet, and ease.
Transformation:
Body in bed, brain still running → thoughts and little noises keep grabbing attention → the room becomes steady and predictable → attention has less to chase → drift off without noticing.
________________


8. Differentiation / Positioning
Enemy: A bedroom that becomes completely quiet and completely still while your brain is still running.
In complete silence:
your thoughts can feel louder
 and
every little external sound can stand out more.
Secondary enemy: sleep solutions that are too stimulating, obviously looped, artificial-feeling, uncomfortable, or require active attention.
Positioning:
Spin360 creates a steady sleep environment for both mind and body: consistent fan-generated sound for the mind to settle into and soft 360° airflow for the body to settle into.
Instead of trying to force yourself to:
“Stop thinking.”
The reframe is:
Give your brain somewhere else to settle.
