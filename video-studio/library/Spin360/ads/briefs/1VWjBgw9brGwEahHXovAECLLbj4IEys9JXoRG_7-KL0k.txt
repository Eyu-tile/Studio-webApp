Her bedroom is shut, the air sits still around her face, and she wakes up at three for no reason. Spin360 keeps the air moving, and she sleeps through till the alarm.

	Strategy
# Element
	Answer
	1 · Desire
	Sleep the whole night through without waking up, and wake up actually rested. Core desire: comfort (rest) + feeling like yourself again.
	2 · Avatar
	Adults (mostly women 40–65) who wake up in the middle of the night, often around 3 a.m., wide awake for no reason. About 1 in 3 American adults wake up at least 3 nights a week (Ohayon, 2008, 8,937 adults), and for 9 in 10 of them it has lasted more than 6 months. They have tried a new mattress, pillows, sleep apps; nothing touched it.
	3 · Angle
	A sealed bedroom keeps you waking up at night. Same angle as Baby_NordicBabies_2to5Hours, sold to the adult.
	4 · Mechanism
	Problem: door and window shut, the air stops moving; every breath out stays around your face and you breathe it back in all night, so you toss, turn and wake up. Solution: Spin360 pulls air in from the top and the bottom and sends it out all the way around, so the air around the bed keeps moving like outside. No wind in your face.
	5 · Authority
	The Nordic tradition (babies nap outside in the snow and sleep almost twice as long), simple breathing science, and one real customer review read word for word.
	6 · Awareness
	Problem-aware: she knows she wakes up at 3 a.m.; she has no idea it could be the still air in her room. Strange fact: babies sleeping outside in the snow.
	7 · Emotion
	Opens with surprise and curiosity (snow), drops into recognition ("that's me at 3 a.m."), turns to relief: she sleeps through and opens her eyes to morning light.
	8 · Positioning
	Enemy: the sealed, still bedroom, and what doesn't touch it (AC cools, purifier cleans, a regular fan blasts your face so you turn it off, a new mattress). Spin360 is the air circulator made for bedrooms.
	9 · Offer
	30 nights in your bedroom; if you don't love how you sleep, send it back. Price reframed, never shown.
	Production brief
Voice: female VO, warm and natural, like the baby ad, around 200 words per minute. Look: cinematic but real, like a premium commercial: shallow depth of field, warm practical light, slow camera moves, film grain. The snow story (lines 1–3) is real snow footage, the same as the baby ad. Everything from line 4 on is cinematic AI in the same realistic look, with one woman (about 47, the one in the images) the whole way through.
Visual language (same as the baby ad): used-up air = a translucent grey haze that sits still; fresh air = soft light-blue ribbons; a regular fan = one harsh straight stream; Spin360 = a gentle 360° ring. Problem scenes in cool blue, solution scenes in warm amber.
Rules: the Spin360 is always the white floor model, on the floor, away from the bed, exactly like the product reference. Never show any fan blowing in her face except the regular fan in line 10. The customer review is shown as text, never as an actor. No price on screen, no discount. Images are visual inspiration only, not keyframes.
Music: soft cinematic piano. Story: curious, with wind and snow ambience. Problem: sparse and tense. From the product reveal (line 11): warm and hopeful. Payoff: calm, morning light.
