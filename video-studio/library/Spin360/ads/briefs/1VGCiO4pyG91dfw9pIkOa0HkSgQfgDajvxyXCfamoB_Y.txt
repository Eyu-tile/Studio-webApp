The idea
It's cold outside and the fan is on anyway. That only makes sense if you sleep with a fan for the noise, and that's who this ad is for. The problem we name is their own: sooner or later every cheap fan starts to tick, rattle or whine, and a new noise wakes them up. The Spin360 is built for the sound, so the sound never changes.
