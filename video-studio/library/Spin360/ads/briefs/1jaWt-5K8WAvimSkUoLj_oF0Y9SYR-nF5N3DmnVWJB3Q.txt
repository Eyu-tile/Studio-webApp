The idea: we talk to people who sleep with a fan all winter. Every line shows the problem with their fan on the left and the Spin360 solving it on the right, at the same time.
	Strategy
# Element
	Answer
	Why this ad
	Fan sleepers keep their fan on in winter for the air and the sound, but they point it at the wall so it doesn't freeze them out. That fix costs them the air. Putting their fan next to ours, side by side, shows the switch in one look. The format comes from the Fabletics "3 reasons to switch" ad (watch it).
	What it's about
	Three reasons to switch from the fan they already own. Each one shows the problem on the left and the Spin360 fixing it on the right: no wind on you, a room that isn't stuffy, and the warm air from the ceiling brought down to the bed.
	How we make it
	Split screen the whole video, casual voice + captions, about 55 s, vertical 9:16. Erin's real footage on the right, a different AI woman on the left.
	Desire
	"I want the air and the sound all night, without cold wind on me." (our wording of what we found, not a customer quote). Deeper: a winter bedroom that feels fresh and cozy, with the fan back next to the bed.
	Sub-avatar
	Fan sleepers 30–65 who can't sleep without a fan, keep it on all winter, and point it at the wall so it doesn't freeze them out.
	Angle
	One reason to buy: keep sleeping with a fan in winter, without the chill. The three reasons all prove that one promise.
	Awareness
	Hook: solution aware. They already use a fan as their fix and live with the winter trade-off. Body: product aware. Each reason shows something the Spin360 does that their fan can't.
	Belief
	They believe a fan in winter makes you colder. We don't fight it, we agree: their fan does, that's why it faces the wall. Then we show why this one is different. Second belief: a new fan could take away the sound they need, so we answer it ("you still get that steady sound").
	Mechanism
	It pulls air in from the top and the bottom and sends it out all around the room, not in one stream at you. That's why it can sit by the bed, keep the whole room fresh, and bring the ceiling's warm air down.
	Authority / proof
	Real customer footage (Erin) on the right side the whole time. The proof is visual: the same kind of scene with two different fans. No founder or Shark Tank line in this cut.
	Emotion
	Negative to positive, low to medium: the small nightly annoyances (cold wind, turning the fan to the wall, stuffy air) turn into relief and a cozy bed.
	Positioning
	Different from "your old fan", never "better". The contrast is already in their head: the fan pointed at the wall. The Spin360 is the fan you don't have to turn away.
	Objections
	"Will I lose the sound?" → you still get it. "What if I don't like it?" → 30 nights to try it.
	Hooks
	3 spoken hooks that call out people who sleep with a fan in winter. Same body and CTA in all three; only the hook and the first shot change.
	Offer
	30 nights to try it. No discount, no price.
	Production brief
Format: vertical 9:16, about 55 s. Split screen in every shot, a thin line in the middle. A red ❌ under the left half and a green ✅ under the right half, like the reference.

Real footage of a real person using it builds more trust. Left side: an AI-generated woman who is clearly a different person: different face, different hair. Never make an AI copy of Erin, and never use the same AI woman on both sides. Keep the same AI woman on the whole left side and Erin on the whole right side, so people follow two people. This is where we started, not a rule. Go through Erin's footage, and if something else works better, go with it.
