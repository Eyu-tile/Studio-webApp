[2] Strategy


1 · DESIRE
	comfort — being comfortable inside your own home. The electric bill is the justification, not the desire.
Surface: keep the house cool without the bill going up.
	2 · AVATAR
	Owns AC, 35–64, hot climate, pays attention to the bill.
Experiences: a room the cool air never reaches · cold feet, hot middle of the room · a bill that makes running the AC impossible.
Behaviors tried before buying: managing the thermostat by activity · turning the AC off and toughing it out · eating the bill · ceiling fans and recirculators.
Emotions: financial strain · relief · skepticism about the price.
	3 · ANGLE
	Your AC is expensive and the room still isn't comfortable.
Season-locked: conditional on owning AC, and on it being hot.
	4 · MECHANISM
	Pulls air in from top and bottom at once, pushes it out in every direction — one single temperature in the room.
Proof: three gauges in one bedroom, 78 up / 71 down → all three read 73 with the fan on.
	5 · AUTHORITY
	The founder. Word: cold air sinks + the gauge numbers. Title: founder. Environment: outdoors, no authority.
Third party: three real customer comments on screen.
	6 · AWARENESS
	problem aware. Amplifies a problem the viewer already feels and names its cause.

Script:
Hey guys, do not fall for this video. This is a scam.
So this thing's been going around. It's got, I don't know, a lot of views — and it's me. That's me in the video. I made this fan, and people are believing everything it says. So let me just go through it, claim by claim.
Okay. First. It says people keep their AC on seventy-four, and the room feels like sixty-seven, sixty-eight — so their electric bills dropped. Yeah — those are real comments. People send me those all the time.
Next. It says the cold air from your AC sinks straight down to your feet, and this thing pulls it back up and mixes it around the whole room. That one sounds like marketing. It's physics. Cold air sinks. I mix it. That's the whole design.
Then it says the breeze on your skin makes you feel cooler. I mean... yeah. Moving air just feels cooler than still air. You've felt that your whole life. So you get the cold air spread around the room, plus the breeze on your skin. That's the combo. That's why seventy-four feels like sixty-seven.
Look — if it's a hundred and five in your room and this thing starts blowing a hundred-and-five-degree air, we're not cooling it down. That's not what's happening. But we are giving you a mix. Plus that wind effect on your skin.
So look — you want to know what the actual scam is? I'll tell you.
The scam is calling this thing a fan. That's the lie. It's right there in the name. Because a fan blows air at your face. That's it. That's the whole job.
This thing helps you feel more of the AC you're already paying for. People turn their thermostat up — and their bills drop. The blade gives you that natural breeze around the whole room. And somewhere in there, it also happens to be a fan.
So we call it a fan, and you find out the rest at home. That's the scam. I'm basically confessing here.
Don't fall for it. Please do not buy this.
...I'm kidding. Buy it.
