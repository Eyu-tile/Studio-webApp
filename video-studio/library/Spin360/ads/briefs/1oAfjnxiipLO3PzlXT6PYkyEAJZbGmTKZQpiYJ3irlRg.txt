Spin360_Baby_BlackSmoke - Script & Visual Brief




Brief - Spin360_Baby_BlackSmoke




Desire: "I want my baby to sleep safe and sound through the night, so I can finally sleep too."




Awareness: Problem-aware (she knows the baby wakes up all night). Unaware of the cause.


Angle: Her baby is breathing bad air all night, a pool of carbon dioxide around his face. It's why he wakes up every night, and why she can't sleep either. 


Mechanism: Still nursery air lets the carbon dioxide the baby breathes out pool around his face. Spin360 pulls air in from the top and bottom and mixes the whole room, so the pool is gone.


Proof: One study linked having a fan in the room to a 72% lower risk of SIDS.


Format: Pixar-style 3D animation (cute, warm, family-movie look) + female VO, warm but urgent, mom-to-mom. No dark dramatic music. 3 hook variations, about 1 minute.




Look: Bright, colorful and vibrant, never dark. Black smoke ONLY in the hook, hovering in a believable way just above the baby; it turns into a glowing blue cloud (carbon dioxide) in about 1 second and stays blue for the rest of the ad. Under the cloud the baby looks sad and unwell (never choking); once the air moves, the baby is visibly happy and healthy. The mixing shot must be a big, powerful visual of the whole room's air swirling. Same Pixar baby and mom in every shot.
