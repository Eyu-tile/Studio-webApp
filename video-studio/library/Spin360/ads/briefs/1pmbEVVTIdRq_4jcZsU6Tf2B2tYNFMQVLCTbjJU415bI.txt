This is a storytelling/VSL-style ad centered around the idea that the sleep industry has focused on the wrong solution for bad sleep.
Core message:
* Human biology has not adapted to modern technology/lifestyles
* Since the industrial revolution, technology has advanced faster than our bodies can adapt
* Deep sleep requires the body to cool down 1–2 degrees
* This happens naturally through air moving across the skin
* Most sleep products solve the wrong problem
* Spin360 is positioned as a more natural airflow solution
The ad should feel:
* Thoughtful
* Elevated
* Calm
* Educational
* Slightly cinematic
Keep the focus on the viewer and their experience, not on us/the brand.

[7] Editing Rules
* First 3 seconds must be FAST (no pauses)
* No dead air in video—unless it's intentional
* No clip longer than 2 seconds in first 10 seconds
* Add captions for entire video
* Each visual clip MUST complement the audio to help the audience understand what is happening
* Editor must add sound design
* Use Speed Ramps when needed and Add Blur
* The mixing demo should be HIGH QUALITY and clearly visible—this is the hero visual
* Nature B-roll should feel peaceful and aspirational, not stock-footage generic
