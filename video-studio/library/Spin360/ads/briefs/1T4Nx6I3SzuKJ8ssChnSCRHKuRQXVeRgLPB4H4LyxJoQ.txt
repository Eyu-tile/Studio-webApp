Body
My room was shut all night. Door closed, window closed. The air just sat there, and every breath I let out came right back at my face. So I'd wake up. And Mom would wake up. Same deal in her room, by the way. Door shut, window shut. By seven she's on her third coffee and the car keys are in the fridge.
Then Grandpa walks in with this fan. Spin360. Mom goes, "Dad. It's not even hot." He goes, "It's not for the heat. It's for the air." And he turns it on.
Okay. First thing: it doesn't blow at you. At all. The air just moves around the whole room. Soft. Like somebody cracked a window, except the window's shut.
Second thing: that air I was breathing back in? Gone. All night the air around my face keeps moving, so it's fresh air every breath, not my own.
And that's when it happened. I didn't make it to the end of my bottle. I didn't fight it, I didn't notice it. I was just… out. And I stayed out.
Next morning, I woke up like a guy coming back from a cruise. No crying, no rubbing my eyes. Just up.
And it's not only me. Mom stole it. Put it next to her bed. Now she turns the light off and she's gone. Yesterday she finished a whole sentence.
So yeah. The air moves. You sleep. Try it for 30 nights. If you don't love how you wake up, send it back. Okay. Mom's waiting.
Strategy
# Element
	Answer
	1 · Desire
	Fall asleep without noticing and wake up actually rested.
	2 · Avatar
	Adults 30–60 who shut the bedroom door and windows to sleep and wake up tired anyway. Moms are in it, but this is not a baby product: the baby is the narrator, the sale is to the adult.
	3 · Angle
	In a closed room you breathe your own air all night. The Spin360 keeps the air around you moving, with nothing blowing in your face, so you drift off without noticing.
	4 · Mechanism
	It doesn't blow at you; it moves the air around the whole room, so every breath is fresh air, not your own. Said in the baby's words, never the spec sheet.
	5 · Authority
	None claimed. Grandpa brings the fan, the baby reports what happened. No studies, no testimonials.
	6 · Awareness
	Hook: problem unaware (the shut door is the problem). Body: product aware. The belief "a fan is for heat" is said out loud by Mom and answered by Grandpa.
	7 · Emotion
	Amusement first (a baby giving a car review), then relief: he falls asleep without noticing, Mom finishes a sentence. Calm, dry, warm. No hype.
	8 · Positioning
	Enemy: the closed bedroom and the regular fan that blows in your face. Spin360 moves the air around the whole room, soft, like a cracked window with the window shut.
