Spin360_Baby_SIDSBiggestFear
Desire: "I want to bring my baby home and know they're safe every night."


Avatar: Expecting moms (no baby yet) getting the nursery ready. The baby scenes show what WILL happen once the baby arrives.


Awareness: Problem-aware of SIDS (every parent's biggest fear), unaware that CO₂ builds up around a baby's face in still air.


Angle: SIDS takes healthy babies → the invisible cloud of CO₂ in a closed nursery → the Kaiser Permanente study (fan in the room, 72% lower risk) → why a window or a regular fan can't fix it → Spin360 → what fresh air does for a newborn (lungs, deep sleep, a happy baby).


Mechanism: Spin360 pulls air in from the top and the bottom and sends it out 360°, so the air in the whole room keeps moving all night. A soft, silent breeze, never wind in the face.


Proof: Coleman-Phox, Odouli & Li, Kaiser Permanente, Archives of Pediatrics & Adolescent Medicine, 2008: fan use linked to a 72% lower SIDS risk (185 SIDS cases vs. 312 controls). SIDS is the leading cause of death for babies 1 month to 1 year (NICHD).

Production notes
Format: Female VO, ~2:45, vertical 9:16. Three hooks with the SAME VO; only the opening visual changes. The body is the same for all three.
Style: Cinematic Pixar-style 3D animation with realistic, moody lighting: warm golden interiors for the safe moments, cold moonlit blue for the problem scenes. Darker and more intense than the pastel version, but never graphic.
Visual language: CO₂ = a smoky grey-violet mist that sits still and pools around the baby's face. Fresh air = translucent white-blue ribbons of breeze.
Fixed rules: The baby is always on its back in a bare crib. Spin360 is always the big white floor model. No text inside the images; all text is added in the edit.
