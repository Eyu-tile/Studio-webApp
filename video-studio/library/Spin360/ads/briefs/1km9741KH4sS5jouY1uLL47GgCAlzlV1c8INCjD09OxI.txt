The plan exists so you can depart from it. Find the happy accidents. The storyboard is your map (not a cage) — if you see a better road, take it. 
Format — THE WINNING LAYOUT (copy the running ScamConfession): The B-ROLL fills the entire vertical frame — real bedrooms, the couple, the product, natural light. DAVID is a cutout picture-in-picture anchored bottom-left: chest-up, background removed, reacting and pointing at what the scene shows. BIG red ALL-CAPS anchors drop in the upper-middle on the key lines. Continuous spoken captions run throughout (account caption style) — a sound-off viewer must follow 100% of the argument. David goes FULL FRAME on exactly three beats — the confession, the wave gesture, the close. Full frame is punctuation, not default.


How to read the storyboard: timings are approximate — the voice-over recording sets the real clock. The VISUAL column describes the IDEA each stretch needs to communicate, with example scenes; the editor picks or generates footage that carries that idea. Only the items marked ⚓ NON-NEGOTIABLE are fixed: hit those exactly. Red anchors and music changes are tied to SPOKEN LINES, not to timestamps.


Who the avatar is: The fan-dependent husband. He has slept with a fan every night of his adult life — he needs the moving air on his body AND the real fan sound, both, or he doesn't sleep. Next to him: a wife who hates the fan — the stream hitting her face, and the wop-wop-wop of the blades. He has tried every position in the room. Both concluded it's unsolvable. This ad tells him he was right — and hands him the one exception.


Every b-roll scene is the COUPLE's world — their bed, their room, their war, their peace. The scene identifies the buyer before David says a word.


The idea: David opens by accusing his own ad of being a scam, recites the claims like charges, confesses they're all true, and then reveals the real scam: the word "fan" — because with a fan, somebody in that bed always had to lose. The mechanism (blades that push instead of chop) resolves both of her objections — the wind on her skin AND the sound — with one physical fact.


The structure (5 movements): Accusation → Claims-as-charges → Confession ("that's all true") → The real scam + the physics → Reverse-CTA close. Two open loops carry retention: the scam loop (opened at the hook, paid on "the scam is the word fan") and the sound loop ("I'll get to that" → paid on "Told you I'd get to it").


EMOTIONAL ARC & PACING MAP: Total ≈ 1:45–2:00, set by the VO. The ad breathes in waves — fast where he's listing, slow where he's landing.


1. HOOK — CONSPIRATORIAL. Serious, near-whisper warning. Red anchor within the first second.
2. CLAIMS — PROSECUTOR RHYTHM. Faster, list cadence, slightly skeptical. B-roll switches per claim.
3. CONFESSION — THE BREAK. Full stop, FULL FRAME, half-smile, quiet pride. The music turns with him.
4. THE SCAM + THE WAR — BAR PROFESSOR → FRUSTRATION SPRINT. Slow thesis, then the fastest cutting in the ad. Hard stop on "somebody."
5. THE MECHANISM — INVENTOR CALM. Slowest stretch. The wave gesture FULL FRAME. "Window's open" = soft.
6. THE SOUND — PLAYFUL → REVERENT. He performs the wop-wop; the real audio takes over.
7. CLOSE — DEADPAN → TENDERNESS. Joke straight-faced, smile on "I'm kidding", last line soft over the final wide.


MUSIC PLAN :


* From the first word: tense-curious minimal pulse (subtle plucks/tick), low under voice. (Suno/Lyria: "minimal tense curious pulse, subtle plucks, sparse, modern, low intensity, ad-safe, no drop".)
* ⚓ On "Which — yeah. That's all true." — THE MUSIC TURNS: tension resolves into a warm minimal acoustic/ambient bed. The score flips from "warning" to "truth" with him.
* Under the war sprint ("You point it at yourself..." → "...somebody"): light tick percussion joins, quickening. Cuts dead on "somebody."
* On "Mine doesn't point": the bed opens up — warmest stretch, relief arriving.
* ⚓ During the sound beat ("Oh — and the sound" → "...taking the wind for it"): MUSIC DUCKS TO NEAR-ZERO. The real audio owns it: wop-wop → silence → shhh. The duck is what makes the real audio hit.
* From "So yeah" to end: bed resolves on the final wide. No tail over the logo.
* Variation: continuous warm bed from the start.


⚓ SOUND DESIGN (non-negotiable assets): Real conventional-fan wop-wop 2s (on "That wop... wop... wop") · real Spin360 shhh at speed 6+ 2s (on "that clean, steady shhh") · one dial click (on "Below speed five"). Close-mic'd, clean.
