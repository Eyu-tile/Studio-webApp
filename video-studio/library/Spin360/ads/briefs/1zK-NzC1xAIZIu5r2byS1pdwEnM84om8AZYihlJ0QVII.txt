Hooks:

Body:
1. It moves air without blasting your face.
Regular fans blast air straight at you. Spin360 uses a unique blade shape to move air gently around the room — more like a natural breeze, not air being thrown at your face all night. 
2. No cold AC blast.
A lot of people can’t sleep with the AC too cold. This fan doesn’t chill the room like AC can. It keeps the air moving softly, so the room feels more balanced and comfortable.
3. It keeps the air from feeling still.
A closed bedroom can start to feel still and stuffy overnight. Spin360 keeps the air gently circulating, so the room feels fresh instead of stale.
4. It has a true quiet mode.
On the lowest settings, it’s barely noticeable. The air keeps moving, but there’s no harsh fan noise taking over the room.
5. Or use it as soft white noise.
Turn it up, and it becomes a gentle, even shhh — not that rough fan drone, just a smooth background sound that helps the room feel calm.
6. Add a scent pod.
Drop in a scent pod, and the breeze doesn’t just feel fresh — it smells fresh too. A soft scent moves with the airflow, giving the room that calm, spa-like feeling.
For people who want the bedroom to feel fresh, calm, and comfortable — without cold AC or fan air blasting at their face.
It comes from David Patrick — the inventor behind Shark Wheel, seen on Shark Tank. Now, he’s reinventing the way air moves through your bedroom.
