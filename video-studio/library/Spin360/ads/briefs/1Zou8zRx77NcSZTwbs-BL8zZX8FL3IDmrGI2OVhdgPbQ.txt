Brief - Spin360_David_HeatMapSplit.mp4
3 ads, one edit, vertical 9:16, split screen. Top half: animation. Bottom half: David. Only the top visual of the hook changes between the 3 ads; the audio and the rest are the same. The images are inspiration for each shot, not a storyboard.
	

The idea: David tells his real test (78 at the top, 71 at the bottom, fan on: all three gauges at 73) while the top half shows it as the heat map he asked for. Then: the heat from your heater goes to the ceiling, Spin360 moves it back down to you, no cold blast, and real reviews.
	Strategy
# Element
	Answer
	Why this ad
	In winter the heat goes to the ceiling and people still feel cold where they sit and sleep. David already ran the test that proves what Spin360 does about it (78 at the top, 71 at the bottom, fan on, all three gauges at 73), and in the Q&A he even asks for it as a heat map: "That would be cool as heck to see a heat map… the red and the blue and the whole thing just becomes purple… we're totally gonna do that." This ad makes that heat map.
	Desire
	"I want the warm air near the ceiling to reach where I actually am." (from our Reddit research on heating in winter)
	Sub-avatar
	People who run the heat all winter and still feel cold at the bed or the couch. Behavior: they crank the thermostat, wear socks, pile on blankets.
	Angle
	It makes the heater you already have work better: Spin360 moves the heat from your heater around the room, down to where you are.
	Awareness
	Hook: problem aware (heat on, still cold), opened with the proof. Body: product aware.
	Belief
	"A fan in winter makes me colder" and "fans don't change anything" (people who reversed a ceiling fan and felt no difference). We answer with a measured test, no cold blast, and real reviews.
	Mechanism
	Spin360 pulls air in from the top and the bottom and sends it out all around the room, so the warm air stuck at the ceiling mixes back down. Proof: the 78 / 71 / 73 test David tells.
	Authority / proof
	David, the inventor, on camera (real footage). Lower third the first time he shows up. Two real 5-star reviews and a real comment, read by David.
	Positioning
	Not a heater. It doesn't make heat; it moves the heat you already pay for. Enemy: the heat stuck at the ceiling.
	Emotion
	Curiosity (78, 71… then 73?) → recognition (heat on, cold feet) → relief (the heat reaches you) → trust (real reviews).
	Offer
	30 nights to try it. No discount.
