SPIN360 — "The Breeze"
Video Prompts · Kling 3.0
Cinematic mother-daughter conversation, golden hour, oceanfront balcony in Hawaii. 10 clips (~10s each) · product B-roll layered on top in editing · breeze always SOFT.
How to use: the Global Look + Characters block below applies to every clip and is what defines the characters and the world. Prepend it to each clip when generating — Kling has no memory between generations.
________________


🎨 GLOBAL LOOK + CHARACTERS
(applies to every clip)
Cinematic luxury commercial, shot on ARRI Alexa with vintage cinema lenses, shallow depth of field, creamy bokeh. Warm dreamy aspirational aesthetic — the visual language of a high-end fragrance ad or a Nancy Meyers film. Golden hour on an oceanfront balcony in Hawaii, late afternoon.


	

	LIGHTING
	soft glowing golden-hour sunlight, warm amber key wrapping the subjects, gentle rim light on their hair, subtle controlled lens flare from the low sun, soft halation on highlights — designed, intentional, expensive light.
	COLOR GRADE
	warm and filmic, golden highlights, soft teal shadows, gentle contrast, warm healthy skin tones, slightly desaturated and elegant.
	CAMERA
	subtle organic movement, slow and deliberate as if on a high-end gimbal; composed framing with layered depth — soft foreground, sharp subject, dreamy ocean background.
	TEXTURE
	fine 35mm film grain, gentle motion blur on moving hair and fabric; cinematic and filmic, never plastic or AI-smooth.
	BREEZE
	keep it SOFT and GENTLE: only a light, lazy breeze barely moving a few loose strands of hair, a sheer linen curtain swaying softly and slightly, a potted plant with leaves gently trembling, a calm ocean. Subtle, serene late-afternoon air — NOT windy, NOT strong, no hair flying, no flapping or billowing fabric.
	CHARACTERS
* MOM (about 62) — elegant warm natural woman, soft silver-grey hair with a few fine strands gently drifting, graceful light wrinkles, calm content face, cream linen wrap, wellness elegance. Beautiful at her real age, not de-aged, not a model.
* EMMA (about 35) — her daughter, same fair skin tone, subtle family resemblance, same eyes and smile, loose hair barely moving in the soft breeze, minimal natural makeup, soft neutral linen resort wear, easy and warm.
They sit close on elegant balcony loungers facing the golden ocean, turning to each other as they talk.
SOUND (every clip) — gentle ocean swell, soft wind, distant birds throughout, intimate and warm. No intrusive music.
