	[2] Concept


Who it's for (Persona)
	P1 Quiet Sleep Seeker, male pocket: married man 35-55 sleeping hot in summer. Secondary: any couple drifting into separate rooms when it gets hot.
	Angle
	Sleep divorce. The hot, loud, still bedroom is quietly costing couples the same bed. The fan is the thing that brings her back.
	Awareness stage
	Unaware / Problem aware (story-led, product deferred to 2:10)
	Concept description
	Quasi-style AI animated sung narrative, 3:25. Private-wound hook (her text), drift into the guest room, wise neighbor explains it is the air (Sweden callback), cute blob physics scene, demo night, full-circle chorus flip: she sleeps better when I'm GONE becomes when I'm HOME.
	Key USPs
	Cools the whole room to one temperature (approved claim). Freakishly quiet. Nothing to point, nothing to fight over. Whole-room breeze.
	Hypothesis
	The long-form sung-story format (proven by Quasi in skincare) transplanted to fans holds 3+ minute watch time and reaches men through the marriage stakes, where the Sweden ad reaches mothers.
	Format
	AI Music Video. Song via AI music tool. Visuals: character sheets first, then start+end keyframes per scene, animated frame-to-frame.
