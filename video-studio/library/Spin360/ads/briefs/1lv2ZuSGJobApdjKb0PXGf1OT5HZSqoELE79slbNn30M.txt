Strategy
# Element
	Answer
	1 · Desire
	"I want to sleep through the night without waking up hot." What she's after is fresh air moving over her bed all night, the way it feels when the window is open.
	2 · Avatar
	Hot sleepers, more often women, whose bedroom gets warm and stuffy at night. Their fix is to open the window, but they can't keep it open all night because it's cold outside or the street is loud. So they try the AC or the heat settings and still wake up hot. In a customer's words: "My bedroom gets hot and stuffy."
	3 · Angle
	Get the open-window feeling in your bedroom all night, with the window shut. All 3 hooks show this one idea.
	4 · Mechanism
	One single temperature. Claim: the whole room feels like one fresh, even temperature. Reason: hot air goes up and cool air sinks, and Spin360 pulls the hot air down and the cool air up, so the room becomes one temperature with a soft breeze moving all around it. Show it on screen: ribbons and curtain moving while the window stays shut.
	5 · Authority
	David, the inventor, talks from the first line. He explains how it works and says how he sleeps with it. You see the proof (ribbons, curtain, hair moving with the window shut). He only says how it feels and never says it cools the room, so nothing clashes with what she already believes about fans.
	6 · Awareness
	Solution aware. She already knows the fix she wants, an open window, and the hook starts right there. The ad then climbs two steps: why Spin360 gives that feeling (one single temperature), then the offer (try it for 30 nights).
	7 · Emotion
	Positive, medium energy. Happy surprise first ("wait, the window is shut?"), then calm. Music and pace should feel light and airy, never scary and never hyped.
	8 · Positioning
	Different, not better. Enemy: the split room the heater or AC leaves behind, with hot air stuck up top and cool air down low. Spin360 owns "the open-window feeling with the window shut," and you can see it.
	Production brief
Voice: David from start to finish. Three lines are made in Seedance 2.5 (David's voice, and David on camera where the shot list says so):
(1) Hook: "Is it an open window? No. Does it feel like one? Yes."
(2) "We are freakishly fresh."
(3) "So if you want amazing sleep all year, try the Spin360 for 30 nights."
All other lines: David's audio from the stringout.
David's look: every David shot is in front of an open window: tall white sheer curtains blowing in, soft daylight, green hills outside. Use the image in the 0:03 row as the start frame in Seedance. For the David shots from the edit project, key the blue backdrop and put the same window behind him, so every David shot is in one place.
The visual idea: the air moves, but the window is shut. Every hook shows the air moving (ribbons, curtain, her hair). The ad ends on the same picture: a bedroom at night, window shut, curtains moving gently.
The air is a breeze, not wind: curtains and hair move softly, nothing flies around (except the ribbons in Hook 1).
Rules: no discount and no season words, on screen or in audio. Never say "silent". Spin360 is the only fan on screen. Captions: white words, center, word by word.

	Why each part is there (for the cut)
Part
	What it does
	Hooks (0:00–0:03)
	Open the big question: if it's not a window, what is it? Each one shows the air moving with the window shut.
	"Freakishly fresh" (0:03)
	Answers fast with a person: the inventor. Keeps the question open: how?
	"Sleep / AC or heater / separation" (0:06–0:20)
	Opens a second question she feels: why does my room feel stuffy? Hot on top, cold on the bottom.
	"One single temperature" (0:20–0:27)
	Pays the "how". Keep the arrows animation tight: this is the reason to believe.
	"Walls removed / bed outside" (0:32–0:37)
	Pays the hook: it feels like the window is open.
	Ending (0:45)
	Same picture as the hooks: window shut, air moving. Then the 30-night trial.
