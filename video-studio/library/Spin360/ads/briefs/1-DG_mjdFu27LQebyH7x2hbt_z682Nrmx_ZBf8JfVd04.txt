Spin360_WhyWinter


Brief - Spin360_WhyWinter.mp4
3 ads, one edit, ~30 s. Narrator voiceover. Same body in all three; only the hook (VO + first shot) changes. Mix our real footage (UGC library) with generated shots. The images below are inspiration, not a storyboard.
	

The idea: a fan in winter sounds strange. The ad answers that in three short reasons: the air moves without a cold blast, the sound helps you sleep, and the warm air comes down from the ceiling to where you sleep.
	Strategy
# Element
	Answer
	Desire
	Sleep well in winter: air moving in the room, a sound that helps you fall asleep, and warm where you sleep. No cold air in your face.
	Avatar
	Adults 35–65 who want a cozy, comfortable bedroom in winter: heat on, windows shut. Not only people who already sleep with a fan.
	Angle
	Why people use this fan even in winter, and it's not to cool down. Three reasons, all about sleeping better in winter.
	Awareness
	Unaware of a "winter fan". The hook is the surprise ("a fan, in winter?"); the body answers it with three facts.
	Format
	3 Reasons with a narrator VO. ~30 s, vertical 9:16. One scene per reason. Real footage where we have it, generated shots for the rest.
	Hooks
	3 spoken hooks, same body. Each hook ends on "people use / buy / run this fan in winter", so the body can start with "And it's not to cool down." The visual shows what the hook says.
	Offer
	30 nights to try it. No discount, no price.
	Production brief
Format: vertical 9:16, about 30 s. Narrator VO: warm and calm, like someone explaining something they like. Not an announcer.
Look: winter outside (snow, frost on the window), cozy inside (warm lamps, thick blankets). The Spin360 in a real bedroom.
Captions: white, a few words at a time. Put "One / Two / Three" big on screen as each reason starts.
Sound: soft, warm music. Under reason 2, drop the music for a second and let the real sound of the unit (speed 6+) come through.
Real footage: product shots and bedroom scenes from the UGC library; Shark Tank in LiveLonger_HOOK_6 01:24–01:32; prototypes in this folder.
