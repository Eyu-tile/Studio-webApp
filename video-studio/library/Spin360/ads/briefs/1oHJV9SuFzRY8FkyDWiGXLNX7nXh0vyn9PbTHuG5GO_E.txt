Who it's for (Persona)
	Anyone who sleeps badly in a sealed, modern bedroom (broad, sleep-led). Doesn't know still air is the cause.
	Angle
	We were built for moving air.
	Awareness stage
	Problem unaware - they don't yet know that still, sealed air is why they sleep badly.
	Concept description
	A calm narrated history VSL. Opens on the forgotten 'sleeping porch' - why old houses had them - reveals people always slept in moving air, and that a sealed, still bedroom is the new experiment. Then the Spin360 brings that moving air back indoors.

	[3] Script / B-Roll


Beat
	Script
	B-Roll
	HOOK (pick one - max 3 tested)
	Hook 1: A hundred years ago, American houses had a room that doesn't exist anymore.
Hook 2: Your grandparents slept in moving air. On purpose.
Hook 3: The best sleep of your life wasn't in a bedroom.
	

	THE SLEEPING PORCH
	It was called a sleeping porch. A screened-in room, up on the second floor, with nothing in it but beds. From spring to fall, whole families slept out there - kids, parents, grandparents. Builders put them on houses the way we put in walk-in closets today, because buyers wouldn't take a house without one.
	

	WHY THEY DID IT
	And the reason was simple. People back then knew something about sleep that we forgot. They knew we sleep deeper when the air around us is moving. The way night air moves on a porch.
	

	WHAT CHANGED
	Then air conditioning showed up, and we sealed everything. Windows shut, doors closed, vents off at night because they're loud.
	

	YOU ALREADY KNOW THIS
	But here's the thing. You already know this about yourself. Think about the best sleep of your life. A cabin with the window cracked. A beach house. Your grandmother's porch. It's never a sealed room. It's always somewhere the air is alive.
	

	THE EXPERIMENT
	For pretty much all of human history, people slept in moving air. A sealed bedroom where the air sits perfectly still all night? That's brand new. That's the experiment. And your body is not a fan of it.
	

	THE REVEAL
	So a few years ago, one guy set out to bring that moving air back indoors. He's the guy who reinvented the wheel - the Shark Wheel - went on Shark Tank, did a deal with Mark Cuban, then spent four years building the Spin360. It doesn't do what fans do. Nothing blasts at you. It just sits in the corner and keeps the air in the whole room slowly turning, all night, in every direction at once.
	

	THE FEELING
	And the first time you turn it on, it's honestly hard to describe. It doesn't feel mechanical at all. It feels like a fresh breeze - a real one, the kind you get outside on a perfect night that just wraps around you from every direction. Like the walls of the room aren't even there. And you can't hear it. You just wake up different.
	

	CTA
	Once you feel it, you'll get why they built whole rooms for this. The first thousand people get a hundred bucks off, and you've got thirty days to sleep with it before you decide, or send it right back. And if you can still see the link, you're not too late.
