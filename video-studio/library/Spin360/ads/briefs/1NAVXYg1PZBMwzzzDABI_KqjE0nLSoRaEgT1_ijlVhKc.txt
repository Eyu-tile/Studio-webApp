[2] Script


Here’s why your house doesn’t smell good all day.
Most people use candles or sprays, but they only scent one area and fade pretty fast.
Spin360 works differently.
Just pop in one of its scent pods, turn it on, and as it moves air around the room, it carries the fragrance with it.
So instead of the scent staying in one corner, it moves throughout the space.
That’s how you get that five-star hotel smell at home.
And while it’s spreading the fragrance, it’s also keeping the air moving and giving you a soft, steady breeze around the room.
So you’re not adding another device that only makes the room smell good. You’re making the whole space feel better at the same time.
If you want that five-star hotel smell at home, try Spin360.

 MAKE YOUR WHOLE HOME SMELL EXPENSIVE

V3 — Curiosity / Luxury
Hook text:
 THE 5 STAR HOTEL SCENT TRICK

 Open on a room that looks like a luxury hotel. Then quickly reveal that it’s actually a home. Cut to someone inserting the scent pod into Spin360.

 Create curiosity around the “trick” behind getting that five-star hotel scent at home.
