1. Desire
 Surface Desire: Create a bedroom environment that makes it easier to relax, fall asleep, and stay asleep.
 Why Train: Fewer disturbances → more continuous sleep → wake up feeling more rested → feel better during the day.
Core Desire: Comfort / Control / Health
2. Avatar
 People whose sleep is disrupted by nighttime noise and who want a more consistent, comfortable sleep environment.
Experiences: traffic, neighbors, dogs, snoring, footsteps, household sounds, or other nighttime disturbances.
Behaviors: use fans, AC, air purifiers, digital white-noise machines, apps, speakers, or earplugs to mask nighttime noise; often keep searching when digital sound feels fake, looped, harsh, or distracting, when fans create unwanted airflow or inconsistent noise, or when the solution simply doesn’t mask enough disturbance.
3. Angle
 Nighttime noise makes it harder to relax, fall asleep, and stay asleep.
4. Mechanism
 Purpose-built blade-generated white noise.
Spin360’s blade is designed to move air smoothly instead of repeatedly chopping through it like a traditional fan. That produces a smoother, steadier mechanical white noise specifically suited for sleep — while the same blade creates a soft breeze that moves around the room rather than a harsh stream of air aimed at one spot.
5. Authority
 David Patrick — inventor of Spin360, who spent years developing the blade and airflow system behind the product.
Secondary Authority: real customers with firsthand experience using Spin360 for their sleep environment.
6. Awareness Level
 Problem Aware → Solution Aware
Problem Aware: they know traffic, neighbors, snoring, barking, or other nighttime sounds are disturbing their sleep, but may not yet see white noise as the solution.
Solution Aware: they already know about or use background sound, fans, white-noise machines, apps, AC, or similar solutions to mask nighttime noise.
7. Emotion — Valence + Intensity
 Negative Valence / Low-to-Medium Intensity
Primary emotions: annoyance, restlessness, frustration, and lack of control.
 Desired emotional state: calm, relief, comfort, and ease.
8. Differentiation / Positioning
 Enemy: Digital white-noise machines and apps that play artificial sound through a speaker, and traditional fans that chop the air, creating harsher fan noise and direct airflow.
Positioning: Spin360 is built to create a better kind of fan-generated white noise. Its purpose-built blade moves air smoothly instead of chopping it, producing a steadier, more soothing sleep sound while circulating a soft breeze around the room.
