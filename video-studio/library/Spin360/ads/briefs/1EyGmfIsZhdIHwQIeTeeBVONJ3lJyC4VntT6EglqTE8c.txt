[4] Script

Imagine the walls of your room were removed and your bed is just sitting there outside. And there's this perfect breeze coming from everywhere — not blowing at you, just moving around you, across your skin, through your hair. Like you're on a beach in Hawaii and the air just knows exactly what to do.
And it's quiet.   There's this soft sound, barely there, like the world just turned the volume all the way down. And you breathe in and it smells like pine and cedar. Like a cabin in the mountains with the windows open.
You don't try to sleep. Your eyes just close. And when you wake up you feel like a completely different person.
That's not a dream. That's the Spin360.
It pulls air from above and below at the same time and sends it out 360 degrees. And because of how the blade is shaped the air doesn't feel mechanical — it feels like a real breeze. It wraps around you, shifts direction, moves with the room. That's why it feels so good. That's why people say it feels like being outside. 
And the sound — below speed five you won't even know it's on. Above five it makes this soft shhhh and honestly that alone just makes your whole brain shut off. It's kind of hard to explain until you hear it but it just works.
Oh and the scent pods — you drop one in and like ten seconds later your whole room is pine and cedar and eucalyptus. Smells like you just opened a window to the mountains. Throw citronella in during summer and bugs are just gone. That part might be my favorite honestly.
It was made by the guy who invented the Shark Wheel — yeah he literally reinvented the wheel, went on Shark Tank, got a deal with Cuban, O'Leary, and Woodman. They just started selling it and the first thousand people get a hundred off. Go feel it. You're gonna love it.
