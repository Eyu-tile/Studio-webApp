Brief - Spin360_AIStreetInterview.mp4
	

The idea: everyone sleeps best where the air moves. The video never says it straight: the interviewees show it, the man realizes it, and David explains it. Inspiration, not a rulebook. Use your judgment.
	Strategy
# Element
	Answer
	Desire
	"I want to sleep like I used to." Wake up rested instead of tired, with fresh moving air like the best nights of their life (camping, the beach, a window open), without a fan blasting their face.
	Avatar
	Homeowners 45–65 in a closed-up house who haven't slept well in years: they go to bed tired and wake up tired and can't say why. They already tried a box fan, the ceiling fan, the AC. Belief to respect: "a fan is a fan, it just blows air at you."
	Angle
	One reason to buy: everyone sleeps best where the air moves (outside). The Spin360 brings that moving air into a closed bedroom: it pulls the warm air from the ceiling and the cool air from the floor and mixes them into one perfect atmosphere, so the room feels open and outdoors, at the temperature of your own home.
	Mechanism
	Regular fans blow one way, or swing side to side to spread it. This one pulls air in from both directions and sends it out all around at once (Saturn's rings), mixing the floor air and the ceiling air.
	Proof
	David's own first night ("I thought I was outside"), the nights after ("waking up more refreshed"), a real customer ("I haven't slept this good in years"), and "I can't live without this thing."
	Authority
	David Patrick, the inventor, introduced by the on-screen text "The inventor who brings outdoor air into your bedroom." He is the only real person in the video, and it shows.
	Awareness
	Hook: Unaware (a funny, obviously-AI street interview). The man in the robe turns it Problem Aware ("I'm stuck in a closed-up bedroom where nothing moves"). David takes it to Product Aware.
	Emotion
	Amusement (the baby, the bear), recognition ("that's me"), curiosity, wonder ("the walls were gone"), then calm and want.
	Format
	AI street interview, and we don't hide that it's AI. Absurd interviewees in the snow, then a hard cut to the real David from the Dropbox interview. The cold/snow is only in the visuals: nobody ever mentions the season.
	Hook
	1 hook (the interview is one continuous sequence).
	Offer
	No discount and no sales line in the voice. End card only: "Try it for 30 nights."
