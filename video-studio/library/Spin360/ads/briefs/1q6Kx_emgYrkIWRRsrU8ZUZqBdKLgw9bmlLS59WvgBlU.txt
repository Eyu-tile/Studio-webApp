Brief - Spin360_Baby_Timeline_FirstNight
Strategy
# Element
	Answer
	1 · Desire
	"I want my baby safe and healthy in the nursery, and I want to sleep with peace of mind." Core desires: the baby breathing easy and sleeping through, and mom getting her nights back.
	2 · Avatar
	Experiences: mom of a baby 0–12 months who wakes every two hours. Behaviors: has tried white noise, swaddles, blackout curtains, feeding/rocking, a monitor; paces the hallway at 2 a.m. Emotions: exhausted, quietly guilty ("am I doing something wrong?"), worried about the baby's health. Demographic: US women 25–38.
	3 · Angle
	It was never you or your baby. It was the air. What happens inside the nursery, hour by hour, after you close the door.
	4 · Mechanism
	Every breath out is carbon dioxide. With the door and window shut nothing moves the air, so it hangs around the baby's face and the baby breathes it back in → lighter sleep → wake-ups. AC cools, purifier cleans, a regular fan blasts one way: none moves the room's air. Spin360 is an air circulator: pulls air from the top and the bottom and keeps the whole room moving, 360°, softly.
	5 · Authority
	(1) Finland: moms put babies outside to nap, even in the snow, and those babies sleep longer; (2) the mechanism shown visibly in 3D; (3) two-hour stretches turning into five; (4) 30-night guarantee.
	6 · Awareness
	Problem aware, cause unaware: she knows the baby wakes every two hours, not why. The hooks bring her straight to the cause. Strange fact: "your baby breathes their own breath back in."
	7 · Emotion
	  

Opens: negative, high (alarm). Mid: dread → frustration → curiosity (Finland) → the turn at the product. Ends: positive, high (peace, relief, "feeling like yourself again").
	8 · Positioning
	Enemy: still air in a closed nursery, and the gadgets that don't move it (AC, purifier, regular fan). "It's not a fan, it's an air circulator" — the one thing in the room that moves all of the air, gently and quietly.
