	1 Desire
	A baby who finally sleeps five hours straight instead of two, and a mom who gets her life back: she sleeps, drinks her coffee while it's still hot, has the energy to play with her baby and gets to enjoy him instead of just surviving him. And a happier baby who wakes up smiling.

	2 Avatar
	Who: a mom, specifically a new mom (first or second baby, 0 to 12 months) whose baby wakes up every two hours. The baby on the talk show speaks for her baby, but every line is aimed at her.
Experiences: up every two hours for weeks or months; tried the swaddle, the sound machine, the blackout curtains and the bedtime routine, and nothing changed.
Behaviors: googles baby sleep at 2 a.m., buys sleep gadgets, reheats the same coffee three times, scrolls funny mom content at night.
Emotions: exhausted, frustrated, quietly guilty, loves her baby but is running on empty; responds to humor that 'gets' her.
Demographic: US moms, 25 to 38, baby 0 to 12 months.
	3 Angle
	Sealed nursery air keeps the baby waking every 2 hours
	4 Mechanism
	Problem: with the door and the window closed, the air in the nursery stops moving; every exhale (used-up air, CO₂) hangs around the baby's face and gets breathed back in, so the baby can't settle and wakes up.
Solution: Spin360 pulls air in from the top and the bottom and sends it out all the way around (360°), so the air around the crib keeps moving like outside; the used-up air drifts away, every breath is fresh air, with a soft, gentle breeze and no noise.
	5 Authority
	In order of appearance: 1) simple breathing science: every breath out is CO₂; 2) the Nordic tradition: babies nap outside, even in the snow, and sleep almost twice as long; 3) proof inside the family: the big brother never slept more than two hours, this baby slept five the first night with Spin360; 4) social proof as a joke: Grandma says he's the easiest baby in the family.
Authority comes from the explanation and the story, told in the baby's voice: no expert or doctor on screen.
	6 Awareness
	Problem-aware: she knows her baby wakes up every two hours (the hook calls it out), but she has no idea the cause is the still air in the nursery, and she has never heard of a solution like this.
Strange fact: 'I stopped breathing my own air': a baby breathes his own exhaled air all night in a closed room.

	Enemy: the still, sealed air in the nursery, and the gadgets that never touch it (the AC only cools, the purifier only cleans, a regular fan blasts the baby).
First and only: an air circulator made for babies' rooms that keeps the air around the crib gently moving, like outside, without ever blowing on the baby.
