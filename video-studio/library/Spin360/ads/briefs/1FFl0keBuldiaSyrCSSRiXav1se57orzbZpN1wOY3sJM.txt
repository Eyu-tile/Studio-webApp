Watch:Brief - Spin360_Baby_SIDSNursery_Song
Strategy
# Element
	Answer
	1 · Desire
	"I want my baby safe and healthy in the nursery, and I want to sleep with peace of mind." Core desires: health/safety of my child + peace of mind ("the last box is checked").
	2 · Avatar
	Experiences: pregnant (2nd–3rd trimester), building or finishing the nursery right now. Behaviors: reads every book and list, buys the crib, monitor, blackout curtains, sound machine; ticks boxes; nesting. Emotions: excitement with a quiet fear of SIDS; needs to know she did everything right. Demographic: US women 25–38, mostly first-time moms. "moms with newborns on the way… I want my newborn child that's about to come out to not die of SIDS" 
	3 · Angle
	The missing box on the nursery checklist: the air. "You checked every box. Nobody checked the air." Same angle and order as the SIDSNursery VSL David approved, now as a song.
	4 · Mechanism
	Every breath out is CO₂. In a closed, still nursery it doesn't rise or leave; it lies on the baby's face like an invisible blanket and the baby breathes it back in all night. AC cools, purifier cleans, a regular fan blows one way: none of them moves the room's air. Spin360 pulls air from the top and the bottom and circulates the whole room 360°, so the blanket lifts.
	5 · Authority
	In order of appearance: (1) researchers believe still air is one of the reasons behind SIDS; (2) the Kaiser Permanente study, hundreds of babies, fan in the room = 72% lower risk; (3) what parents tell us: 2-hour stretches → 5. The ad earns authority by educating; no expert on screen.
	6 · Awareness
	Unaware of the problem (she thinks her nursery is safe) → the hook makes her problem-aware → the body takes her to product-aware. Strange fact: "Your baby breathes its own breath back in, all night long."
	7 · Emotion
	  

Opens: negative, very high (shock, fear). Mid: the turn at the product, negative → positive (relief). Ends: positive, high (tearful pride, peace, triumph).
	8 · Positioning
	Enemy: still air in a sealed nursery, and the gadgets that don't move it (AC, purifier, a regular fan). First and only: the air circulator made for nurseries — moves the whole room's air, 360°, gently, quietly.
	

Production brief




Style: 3D animation with Pixar-quality characters, grounded in reality: real light, real textures, real room scale. Warm terracotta nursery with a painted moon mural.


Captions: big word-by-word lyric captions (bold white, black outline), like the viral AI songs. Spoken parts also captioned.


Visual language: CO₂ = a sheer, dark, smoky veil that sinks and lies on the baby's face (the "invisible blanket"). Fresh air = a warm golden-white glowing breeze full of tiny light motes. The golden air never appears before the Spin360 does (Verse 4). Problem scenes cold and moonlit; solution scenes golden and warm.
