[4] Script
Imagine you're in Hawaii.
Really — picture it. You're in a hammock. Two palm trees. Three in the afternoon. The wood's warm from the sun, your back kind of sinks in, and you just… let your arms fall.
And then the wind shows up.
Not strong. Not weak. It's just there. It moves around you — touches your arm, lifts the hair on your forehead, goes away, comes back. Like it's breathing with you.
You hear the ocean somewhere behind you. Not loud. Just enough to remind you it's there.
And the smell — salt, wood, those flowers you don't even know the name of. It's the kind of air that makes you take a deeper breath. You're not breathing it. You feel it in your chest.
And your eyes get heavy. Your shoulders drop. Your jaw unclenches — you didn't even know it was clenched.
And you fall asleep. Not because you're tired. Because your body just… lets go.
You know that feeling, right? That feeling that only happens on vacation. Once a year, maybe. If you're lucky.
That's not a dream.








That's the Spin360. 
It pulls air in from two sides and pushes it out everywhere — 360 degrees, like Saturn's rings.
The whole room moves. Every corner of it. Every inch of your skin.
And because of how the blades are cut, the air doesn't feel mechanical. It feels like a real breeze.
Like the one that was on your skin in that hammock.
And then there's the sound.
Below speed five — absolute silence. You'll forget it's even on.
Above speed five — the best white noise there is. That soft shhhhhh that makes the whole world disappear in minutes.
Finally, something that sounds like it was made for sleep.
Because it was.
You sleep deeper — the kind of sleep where you wake up and don't even remember closing your eyes. You stop sweating in the middle of the night, stop kicking off the sheets, and the temperature just… stays right. Your skin breathes. Your body cools down the way it should.
And the air feels alive. Not blowing at you from one side — surrounding you. Like a window is open somewhere even when it's not.
You drop in a scent pod — eucalyptus, plumeria, ocean — and the whole room starts to feel different. Today it's Hawaii. Tomorrow it's a forest after the rain. Next week it's the Mediterranean.
Your bedroom stops being a bedroom. It becomes wherever you want to be.


David Patrick made this. 
He's the inventor of the Shark Wheel — you probably saw him on Shark Tank, where he closed a deal with Mark Cuban and Nick Woodman, the founder of GoPro. 
His idea was simple — bring nature indoors. To launch this, he's giving the first 1,000 people $100 off. Tap below. 
Come build your sanctuary.
