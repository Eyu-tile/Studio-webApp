Spin360_Baby_StudyFirst - Script & Visual Brief
Desire: "I want my baby safe and healthy in the nursery, and I want peace of mind."


Avatar: Expecting moms and parents setting up the nursery.


Awareness: SIDS fear is fully aware. The fan study and the still-air / rebreathing mechanism are unaware. She believes her AC or air purifier is enough.


Angle: Open with the study: one study linked a fan in the baby's room to a 72% lower SIDS risk. Then why (still air around the face), why AC, purifiers and regular fans don't solve it, and then Spin360, made for nurseries.


Mechanism: Spin360 pulls air in from the top and the bottom and gently mixes the whole room, 360 degrees. Soft, natural breeze, never a blast in the face.


Proof: Coleman-Phox, Odouli & Li, Archives of Pediatrics & Adolescent Medicine, 2008 (PubMed 18838649): fan use linked to a 72% lower SIDS risk. Made for babies, built to last 10+ years.
