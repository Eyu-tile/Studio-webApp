[4] Script


Body:
1. It doesn’t blast air at the crib.
Because of its unique blade shape, Spin360 doesn’t push air like a regular fan. It pulls air from above and below, then moves it gently around the room — more like a natural breeze outside, not a hard stream of air thrown at your baby.
2. No cold AC blast.
A lot of parents don’t want cold air blowing through the nursery all night. This fan doesn’t chill the room like AC can. It keeps the air moving softly, so the room feels more balanced and comfortable.
3. It keeps the nursery from feeling stale.
A closed room can get stuffy fast, especially overnight. Spin360 keeps the air softly circulating, so the nursery feels fresher without needing to crack a window or make the AC too cold.
4. It has a true quiet mode.
On the lowest settings, it’s barely noticeable. The air keeps moving, the room stays calm, and there’s no harsh fan noise taking over the nursery.
5. Or use it as soft white noise.
Turn it up, and it becomes a gentle, even shhh — not that rough fan drone, just a smooth background sound that helps the nursery feel calm.
For parents who want the baby’s room to feel calm, fresh, and comfortable — without fan air blasting at the crib.
It comes from David Patrick — the inventor behind Shark Wheel, seen on Shark Tank. Now, he’s reinventing the way air moves through your baby’s room.
