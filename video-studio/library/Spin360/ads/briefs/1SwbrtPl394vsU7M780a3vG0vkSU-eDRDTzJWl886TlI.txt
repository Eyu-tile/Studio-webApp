Strategy
# Element
	Answer
	1 · Desire
	"I want to sleep deep and feel calm at night."
	2 · Avatar
	Women who love air moving in the room but can't stand a fan blowing on them. In a customer's words: "I have ALWAYS found fans to be wonderful to hear and terrible to be near! […] My eyes dry out, my hair blows all over." David hears it all the time: "I hate it when a fan is pointed right at me."
	3 · Angle
	It feels like a real breeze, not a fan. The calm and sleep lines are its payoff.
	4 · Mechanism
	New mechanism (the world's first 360-degree fan): the air goes out all around the room instead of one stream straight at you, so it touches your whole body like a breeze: "it's hitting my toes. It's hitting my knees. It's hitting my chest. […] That's what a breeze does." Customer proof: "you can feel the air movement in the room, but it's not directly blowing on you."
	5 · Authority
	David Patrick, the inventor: he made it, the strongest level of authority. Put his name and "Inventor of Spin360" on screen the first time he appears (0:05), because the script never says it. He repeats, word for word, what women tell him about fans, so she feels understood. Real customers on camera add to it. All of this comes before the product is named.
	6 · Awareness
	Product aware. She knows fans and hates the feel of one blowing on her. The hook hides the product to open a question (she feels calm and can't say why): that is a hidden product, not an unaware ad. The body says why fans fail her (pointed right at you) and why this one is different (air all around, like a breeze). The product is named only near the end.
	7 · Emotion
	Positive, low intensity: calm and relief. Curiosity first ("what is running?"), then relief when David explains it. Soft music, slow pace, no hype. If it feels flat, the first iteration is to raise the intensity.
	8 · Positioning
	Enemy: the fan that pushes one stream of air straight at you. Spin360 is different, not "better": the air goes all around the room, not at you. That's the part we own.
	Production brief
Audio: all lines are the real audio from the two files, cut at the times in the table. A "[…]" in a line means: cut that piece out and join the rest. Leave short natural pauses, don't rush the customers.
CTA: Seedance 2.5, David on camera (use the 0:05 row image as the start frame, same blue shirt and chair as the interview).
Rules: no discount and no season words. Never say "silent". Spin360 is the only fan on screen. Captions: white words, center, word by word.
