	Strategy
# Element
	Answer
	Desire
	"I want my bedroom to feel just right." All winter the house is shut and the bedroom never feels right. They want one even, fresh, perfect atmosphere in the room and to wake up in a better mood.
	Avatar
	Homeowners 45–65 (buyers are mostly women 45–65) sharing a closed bedroom in winter: windows and doors shut, the air just sits there. Curious about how things work (they stop for the guy with the drill). Belief to respect: "a fan is a summer thing". We never say "fan in winter"; we sell the atmosphere.
	Angle
	One reason to buy: in a shut winter bedroom nothing moves the air, so the warm air stays at the ceiling and the cold air stays at the floor. The Spin360 pulls the warm air down and the cool air up and makes one single temperature: a perfect atmosphere all the time.
	Mechanism
	A regular fan blows one way. This shape (the same shape as the Shark Wheel) sucks air in from the top and the bottom at the same time and sends it out like Saturn's rings, so it pulls the warm air down from the ceiling and the cool air up from the floor and mixes the whole room.
	Proof
	The thermal camera (red ceiling + blue floor turns into one even color), the smoke test (in from top and bottom, out like Saturn's rings), and David's own experience.
	Authority
	David Patrick, the inventor who reinvented the wheel (Shark Wheel). He says it in the first line.
	Awareness
	Hook: Unaware (what is this guy doing?). Body: from "my room never feels right in winter" (Problem Aware) to "this one makes the whole room one temperature" (Product Aware).
	Emotion
	Curiosity (the drill), a laugh (the worst propeller), "oh, that's how it works", recognition (the shut winter house), calm (one perfect atmosphere), then want.
	Format
	Drill hook, then a real interview string-out of David (green sweatshirt interview, 2 cameras) + 2 short AI lines of David. Visual idea for this one: the temperature you can see (thermal camera) and a winter room behind David.
	Hooks
	3 drill hooks in real winter public places with steam or smoke already in the scene, filmed like a stranger caught it on their phone: a Chicago Christmas market, a ski lodge fire pit, a New York bus stop on a freezing morning. Same on-screen text on all three.
	Offer
	No discount and no sales line in the voice. End card only: "Try it for 30 nights."

Rules
* This ad is about one perfect temperature, not about heating or cooling. No heater, no "warms the room", no "cools you down".
* Never say or write "fan in winter". We sell the atmosphere.
* No discounts and no sales line in the voice. The offer is only on the end card.
* New shots: the Spin360 is already on from the first frame. Air moves as a gentle breeze, never strong wind.
* Thermal camera: no real brand logos on the screen (no FLIR etc.).
* Hooks must look real. If a new hook doesn't look real, don't use it.
