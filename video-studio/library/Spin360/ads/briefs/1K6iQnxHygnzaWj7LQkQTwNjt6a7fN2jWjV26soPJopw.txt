Spin360 is a fan, but not the one you grew up with. It's the world's first true 360 fan: it pulls air in from two sides and pushes it out in every direction at once, so the whole room gets the breeze instead of one stripe of it. Invented by the same guy who invented the Shark Wheel.

Why people buy it:
- It cools the whole room, not one spot. No oscillating, no waiting for the sweep to come back around.
- It's quiet on the lower speeds. You can sleep next to it and you can film next to it.
- Scent pods click into it, so the room also smells amazing while it runs.
- It costs pennies a night to run, so people just leave it on.
- It makes the room feel different (Like a gentle breeze from the outdoors)
