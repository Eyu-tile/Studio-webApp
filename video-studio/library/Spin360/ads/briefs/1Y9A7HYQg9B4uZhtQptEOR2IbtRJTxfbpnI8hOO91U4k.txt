	Strategy
# Element
	Answer
	Why this ad
	People who already know the Spin360 need a push, not an explanation. A simple, funny "I'm rushing to get it" video does that in 10 seconds. It comes from our own creator ad (watch it): she drives, the Spin360 site scrolls behind her, one line of text, music.
	What it's about
	The sale is on, so people are rushing to get one. Same music in all 4 and almost the same text (V2 has its own line). Only the scene changes, and every scene is winter.
	How we make it
	Green screen style like the reference: the person in front, a screen recording of the Spin360 site scrolling behind, the text on top. About 10 s, vertical 9:16, no voice.
	Sub-avatar
	People who have seen our ads or visited the site and haven't bought yet (bottom of funnel).
	Awareness
	Most aware. They know the product. The only job is urgency.
	Angle
	The sale is happening right now, so don't miss it.
	Emotion
	Fun and a little FOMO. Low intensity, light.
	Offer
	The current sale, shown on the site in the background.
	Production brief
Format: vertical 9:16, about 10 s, no voice. Like the reference: the person in front, a screen recording of the Spin360 site scrolling slowly behind (product page, the sale banner visible), the text in big white letters with a black outline over the middle.
Music: the same upbeat track in all 4.
The scenes: generated or real, your call. Keep them candid, like a phone video, not an ad. Winter in every scene.
