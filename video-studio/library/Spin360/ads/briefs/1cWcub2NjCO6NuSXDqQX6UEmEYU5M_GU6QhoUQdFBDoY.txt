THIS IS NOT A FAN
Don't expect this to blow wind on your skin to cool you down, it won't do that. It keeps your room from feeling stale, and makes the room temperature feel like it's 3-5 degrees colder. You’ll constantly get a small breeze across your skin like when you’re at the beach or maybe on a slightly breezy day outside but it's just a slight feeling of that wind. If you want to feel wind hitting you constantly, then get a normal fan. This fan makes the room FEEL cooler, and more fresh. I sleep with mine in my room and I used to keep my room at 68 degrees at night. Now I only need it at 72 degrees and I feel the same chilliness under my covers. It also takes like 15 minutes to even start cooling a room down. I'd suggest leaving it on full blast, leaving the room for a while and then coming back in. Youll feel a really noticeable, fresh difference between the rooms without one.
