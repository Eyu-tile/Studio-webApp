3 ads, one edit, about 43 s. Only the hook changes; the body and CTA are the same in all 3. The images are inspiration for the idea of each shot, not a storyboard.
	

The idea: "Why would I buy a fan for the winter?" David answers it himself: you still want the breeze, you just don't want a cold blast in your face. The Spin360 points up, not at you, so the air keeps moving and nothing blows cold on you.
	Strategy
# Element
	Answer
	Why this ad
	People who already know the Spin360 still ask the same thing: why would I buy a fan for the winter? David answers it himself, on the podcast, in one line: you still want the breeze, you just don't want a cold blast in your face. This ad turns that real answer into a short bottom-of-funnel video.
	What it's about
	In winter you still want the air moving around you, just not a cold blast in your face. The Spin360 gives you the breeze without the blast.
	How we make it
	About 43 s, vertical 9:16. One setting for David: the podcast set. His real answer from the podcast carries the ad. Anything David says that isn't in the podcast footage is generated with AI in his voice and look, on the same podcast set. The customer is real footage.
	Desire
	"I want to feel like I have a fan on but without the wind commitment." (real fan sleeper on Reddit, r/no)
	Sub-avatar
	Fan sleepers who keep the fan on all year, and in winter turn it to the wall so it doesn't freeze them out. That is their most repeated behavior ("I point it at the wall"), and cold is the number one reason they turn it away.
	Angle
	Winter breeze without the cold blast.
	Awareness
	They know the product or the category. The doubt is the season. The ad answers the objection and ends on the offer.
	Belief
	"A fan in winter = cold air blowing on me." We keep their belief that a breeze feels good and remove the cold blast: you point it up, not at you.
	Mechanism
	You don't point the Spin360 at you. You point it up in the air, and it pulls in and blows out a different way, so the air moves around the whole room and nothing blows cold on you.
	Authority / proof
	David Patrick, the inventor, answering on camera. People who don't know him need to see who he is the first time he shows up (lower third, see the production brief). Plus a real customer who runs it all year round.
	Emotion
	Doubt (a fan, in winter?) → recognition (yeah, I do like a breeze) → relief (no cold blast) → trust (a real customer uses it all year).
	Positioning
	Not a fan you point at yourself. A breeze that moves around the room.
	Objections
	"It will make me cold" → nothing blows cold on you. "Why in winter?" → you still like the breeze. "What if I don't like it?" → 30 nights to try it.
	Hooks
	3 real hooks, all ending on the same question: "Why would I buy a fan for the winter?" H1 the question alone, H2 David first, H3 a customer first. Same body and CTA in all 3.
	Offer
	30 nights to try it. No discount, no price.
	Production brief
Format: vertical 9:16, about 43 s. Captions on.
One setting for David: the podcast set (outdoors by the pool, two chairs, the interviewer on the left, David on the right, two Spin360s behind them). His real lines come from the podcast stringout (Stringout_Spin360_Podcast-QA_Vertical.mp4, Frame.io; the session was filmed 3 times, take 2 starts at +00:21:07 and take 3 at +00:43:22, so pick the best read). Every David line marked AI is generated in his voice and look, on that same set.
Who David is: the first time David shows up, put a lower third on screen: "David Patrick · Inventor of the Spin360 · Reinvented the wheel on Shark Tank". The script never says who he is, so the authority has to come from the screen.
Customer: always the real footage (TESTIMONIALS.mp4, Dropbox SPIN360 2026). Never generated.
Captions: white, a few words at a time. Each hook has its own on-screen line (in the table). The hook text has to make sense with the sound off.
The podcast set looks like summer (outdoors, sunny, by the pool). That's fine for David talking, but every winter moment in the ad (the hook question, the blanket, the night) comes from winter B-roll, not from the set.
Don't show a regular fan anywhere. Show the Spin360, David, or a cozy winter vibe shot.
