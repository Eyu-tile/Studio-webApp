Spin360_Baby_BabyProofYourAir - Script & Visual Brief




Brief - Spin360_Baby_BabyProofYourAir


Desire: "I want to know I've baby-proofed everything, so nothing in that nursery can hurt my baby."




Awareness: Unaware of the problem. She thinks the nursery is already safe.


Angle: She baby-proofed the whole house but not the air. The nursery air sits still all night, and the carbon dioxide her baby breathes out pools around his face. 


Mechanism: Still nursery air lets the carbon dioxide the baby breathes out pool around his face. 
Spin360 pulls air in from the top and bottom and mixes it all around the room, so there's no pool of carbon dioxide by the crib.


Proof: A study of almost 500 babies: a fan running in the room was associated with a 72% lower risk of SIDS.


Format: AI visual story with a female VO talking mom-to-mom ("you"), no on-camera narrator. Realistic AI,  Warm, calm and firm voice, no dark dramatic music. 4 hook variations, about 1:35 of VO.
Look: Bright, colorful and vibrant, never dark or gloomy AI realism. Day scenes warm and bright; the problem (carbon dioxide) always the same glowing blue. The mom is pregnant in the hook montage and appears with her newborn in every scene after the hook.
