Spin360_3Reasons_SealedRoom
3 ads, one edit, about 65 s. Split screen the whole video: the sealed bedroom on the left (❌), the same bedroom with the Spin360 on the right (✅), exactly like Spin360_3ReasonsToSwitch (watch Hook 1). Casual female voiceover with captions. Same body in all three; only the hook (VO + first shot) changes.
	

The idea: you shut the door and the windows to sleep, and the air stops. Every line shows what still air does on the left, and the Spin360 fixing it on the right, at the same time.
	Strategy
Element
	Answer
	Why this ad
	Same format as Spin360_3ReasonsToSwitch (split screen, three reasons, each one problem on the left and Spin360 on the right). The contrast is no longer "your old fan": it is the sealed bedroom, the problem behind our biggest winner (Swedish Babies: still air in a closed room), now with the why (your own breath sits around your face).
	Desire
	Fall asleep without noticing and wake up actually rested.
	Sub-avatar
	People who shut the bedroom door and windows to sleep and still wake up tired. Any season.
	Angle
	One reason to buy: in a closed room the air does not move, so you breathe your own air all night; the Spin360 keeps the air around you moving, in a full circle, with nothing blowing in your face.
	Awareness
	Hook: problem unaware (they shut the door and never connected it to waking up tired). Body: product aware (each reason ends on what the Spin360 does).
	Belief
	"A fan blows on me and I hate that." We agree: a regular fan blows one line of air right at you. This one moves the air around you.
	Mechanism
	Pulls air in from the top and the bottom and sends it out all around the room, in a full circle. That is why the air around your face keeps moving and nothing blows at you.
	Authority / proof
	Swedish parents put their babies outside to nap and they sleep longer out there (told as a fact, not as a cause). Real UGC footage on the right side the whole time.
	Emotion
	Low and real: the small nightly annoyance (stuffy, awake at 1:40) turns into relief (lamp off, out, up before the alarm).
	Offer
	30 nights to try it. No discount, no price.
	Production brief
Format: vertical 9:16, Split screen in every shot, a thin line in the middle. A red ❌ under the left half and a green ✅ under the right half, like 3ReasonsToSwitch.
Who is on each side: Right side (✅): real UGC footage. Erin, our real creator (her folder, full library: UGC folder). The right-side frames in the tables below are inspiration only: they show the kind of shot we mean (her in bed, the curtains, the lamp, the Spin360 by the bed). Pick whatever real clip works best for each line; you are not limited to these frames or to Erin. Left side (❌): generated. An AI woman who is clearly a different person from the one on the right (different face, different hair). Never an AI copy of Erin. Keep the same two people the whole video.
Matching halves: same kind of shot on both sides at the same time (both in bed, both windows, both mornings), so the only difference you see is the air and the fan.
The breath (reason 1): a faint, translucent grey haze in front of her face, like visible breath that does not drift away. Subtle and realistic, not a cartoon cloud. On the right it breaks apart and drifts off.
Voice: casual, like someone telling a friend. Not an announcer. Captions: white, a few words at a time, at the top.
Sound: soft music under the voice.
Rules: never say or show "silent/quiet"; no discount; no Shark Tank; no "one temperature". Breeze is always positive; the negative is a regular fan blowing in your face.
