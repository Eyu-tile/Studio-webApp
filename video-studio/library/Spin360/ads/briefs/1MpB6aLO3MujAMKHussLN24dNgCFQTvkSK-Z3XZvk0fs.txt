Hooks
Hook1:
You think you're gonna make jet engines, you end up making chocolate chip cookies. But they're the best chocolate chip cookies. So that's kind of my life. I started out in such a unique way. I was never an inventor. I stumbled into a shape that was based on nature. And then that shape kept finding different uses — it became a wheel, it became a Frisbee, it became a centrifuge. There were all these amazing things. But it was a precursor to me being able to make this product. 




Hook 2: You can put your pants on your head, and you can be different. You will definitely be different if you walk around with your pants on your head. But you're not better. Body
  
We have a product that is utterly unique. It sucks from the top of the bottom, and blows out like Saturn's rings. Nobody has ever created a product like that before. Something that simulated a natural breeze.
And we've had people literally come up to us and go — what is that thing? What is that fan? Cause it doesn't feel like a fan. It feels like a breeze. And that's what we're selling.
I've been using this in my bedroom. When I have my fan running, it is constantly blowing a perfect breeze over my entire body. Not localized in one place. I'm getting it 100% on me. It's something that I don't want to sleep without it. Again, I'm never waking up hot. I'm completely refreshed.
And I know the reason for that is — I'm not sitting in stagnant air. When you sit in stagnant air, you know it. It stinks. There's something about it. But when air is moving, especially in a natural way, there's something about it that is just refreshing. And when you get that for eight hours while you're sleeping, you end up coming out with a different mood.
And it is life changing. It's something that's different and unique, and it's exciting, and that's what you want in life. You want something that really changes your mood and elevates things. And I believe we have that.
