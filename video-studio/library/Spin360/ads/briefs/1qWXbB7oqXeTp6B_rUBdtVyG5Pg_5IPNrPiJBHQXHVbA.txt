The idea: A quiz, not an ad. David asks three yes/no questions straight to camera — the fan every night (even winter), the wind she hates, the wop-wop she hates even more. The husband counts his yeses and by question three he's been SEEN. Then David flips the guilt: "It was never you two" — the machine made somebody lose. The rest is the fix: a fan that doesn't point, blades that push instead of chop, and the payoff — same night for both of them, with everything she hated simply gone.


The core logic (keep this intact in the cut): he keeps his two things (air + sound). She loses her two things (blast + wop-wop). Nobody converts to anything — what she hated just doesn't exist in this machine. "Nobody hates an open window."


Format: the winning family layout — b-roll fills the frame, David cutout PiP bottom-left reacting/pointing, continuous captions. David goes FULL FRAME on exactly two beats: the hand gesture and the close. Full frame is punctuation, not default. ~65s, 9:16.


Tone: a friend who already knows the answers. Playful through the questions, warm at "It was never you two", inventor-calm in the mechanism, half-smile at the close. Never salesy, never defensive.


How to read the storyboard: timings are approximate — the VO sets the clock. The VISUAL IDEA column describes what each stretch must communicate, with example scenes; you pick or generate the footage that carries that idea. You know the account's shot style: real bedrooms, natural light, couple's world, realism first — imperfect framing welcome. Only items marked NON-NEGOTIABLE are fixed. This storyboard is a battle plan, not a cage — if the footage hands you something better than the written idea, take it and keep it consistent.


MUSIC (tied to lines, not timestamps): light quiz-pulse from the first word, one soft tick per check · the pulse CUTS on "Listen —" and a warm minimal bed takes over · bed opens on "So here's what I built" · ducks to near-zero for the real shhh (non-negotiable) · resolves on the final wide. No tail over the logo.


SOUND DESIGN (non-negotiable): three soft "check" ticks (one per question) · real Spin360 shhh at speed 6+, 2s, on "clean, steady shhh" · room tone under the beats. The old fan appears on screen but its audio never plays — David SAYS the wop-wop; the only product sound the viewer hears is ours.
