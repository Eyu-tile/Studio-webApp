People walk into the house and they all ask the same thing: "is there a window open in here?" There isn't. It's just this thing spinning slow in the corner.
And it barely uses any power, so you do what everyone who owns one does: turn it on and never turn it off. Next to your bed at night, next to your desk while you work — it just becomes how your house feels.
It was made by the guy who invented the Shark Wheel — the square wheel that actually rolls. He took it on Shark Tank and walked away with a deal from Mark Cuban, Kevin O'Leary, and Nick Woodman. This is his next one. So go check it out. Sleep like a husky. Just in your own bed.
