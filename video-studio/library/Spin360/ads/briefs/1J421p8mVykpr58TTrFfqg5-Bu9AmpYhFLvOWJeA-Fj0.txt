[4] Script
At first, women were like…
“Wait… why does this fan actually make me sleep better?”
And everyone thought they knew why.
But that still wasn’t the real reason women were loving it.
It wasn’t just because the air feels like the walls disappeared and your bed landed on a beach in Hawaii.
The air moves like it does outside. Soft across your skin. Calm enough to make your eyes close before you notice. Fresh enough that you wake up rested.
It wasn’t just the 360 airflow either.
It pulls air from above and below, mixes everything, and sends it around the room. Same temperature in every corner. Fresh air right where you breathe.
And it wasn’t just the sound.
Below speed five, it’s basically silent. Above that, it gives you the perfect white noise. That relaxing shhhhh that makes the world disappear in minutes.
And it wasn’t even the scent pods.
Drop one in, and ten seconds later, your room feels like a spa. Use citronella, and summer nights feel even better.
And it wasn’t just because the guy who made it reinvented the wheel and landed a Shark Tank deal with Mark Cuban and Nick Woodman.
Now he’s reinvented the fan.
The part people almost missed?
It’s not one feature. It’s how everything works together.
The breeze, the quiet, the fresh air, the scent.
It’s like your bedroom gets its own little weather system. 
That’s why women are loving this thing.
The first thousand people get $100 off.
Come feel it.
You’re gonna love this thing.
