Spin360_Baby_2to5Hours - Script & Visual Brief


Brief - Spin360_Baby_2to5Hours
Desire: "I want my baby to sleep more hours in a row, so I can finally sleep through the night too."
Awareness: Problem-aware. She knows her baby wakes up all night. She doesn't know the still air in the nursery could be part of why.
Mechanism: Still nursery air lets the CO₂ the baby breathes out pool around his face. Spin360 pulls air in from the top and bottom and sends it out all around the room, so the whole nursery has soft, moving air, like sleeping with the window open.
Angle: Her baby wakes up every 2 hours, so she's up all night and living on half the sleep she used to get. 
Proof: A study of almost 500 babies: a fan running in the room was associated with a 72% lower risk of SIDS.
Format: AI-generated visual story, narrated by the mom in first person (her voice over every scene). Aspirational mom, beautiful home. Realistic AI. Engaging pace that's easy to follow.


Look: Bright, colorful and vibrant, never dark or gloomy AI realism. Night scenes use soft blue light, still bright and readable. The present and the "after" use warm golden light. The Spin360 always comes from real product photos, and the unit stays still: it doesn't rotate or oscillate. Only the air moves.


Script


My baby went from waking up every two hours to sleeping five hours straight. From one simple change.
Before this, the newborn stage was the part of becoming a mom I dreaded most.
I was getting out of bed over and over, every single night.
Then waking up exhausted, going to work tired, and trying to live my normal life on half the sleep I used to get.
Then I put this in his nursery. It's called Spin360.
And I kid you not, he went from waking up every two hours to consistently sleeping four, sometimes five hours without waking up.
And not only that.
He falls asleep faster.
His naps got better.
And the biggest difference? I stopped getting dragged out of bed all night.
So I went down a rabbit hole to figure out why.
Every breath your baby breathes out has carbon dioxide in it. And when the air in the nursery isn't moving, it just hangs right around their face.
They're literally sleeping in a pool of carbon dioxide.
That picture completely freaked me out.
And there's research on this. One study of almost 500 babies found that having a fan running in the room was associated with a 72% lower risk of SIDS.
And it made total sense. It's why we all sleep so well outside, or with the window open. We need moving air. And babies need it even more than we do.
But you don't want a regular fan blasting one stream of air straight at the crib.
Spin360 pulls air in from the top and the bottom and sends it out all around the room. It mixes the whole nursery. No harsh blast. No stale air sitting by the crib. Just soft, moving air everywhere, like sleeping with the window open.
Now he sleeps longer. I wake up less. And for the first time since he was born, I feel like myself again.
Every night you wait is another night you're up at 2, 4, and 6.
If your baby is still waking up every two hours, tap below and get Spin360 for their nursery.
