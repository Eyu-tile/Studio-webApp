[4] Script
We couldn’t find a fan that felt right for the nursery.
So we found the first fan that actually feels made for sleep.
After learning how important moving air can be for a baby’s lung and brain development.
We wanted something that made the room feel fresh, without cold AC or harsh air blowing at the crib.
This doesn’t just move air around the nursery.
It creates this surprisingly soft 360° airflow that moves through the whole room.
So instead of one strong stream of air, it feels more like a gentle, natural breeze.
On low, it’s incredibly quiet for naps and bedtime.
And when you turn it up, it makes this soft white noise that feels perfect for sleep.
You can even add a scent pod, so the nursery feels fresher too.
All of that in one fan means less clutter in the room.
Honestly, it’s become one of my favorite things in the nursery.
And if you’re setting up a nursery, this is one thing I’d add right away.
