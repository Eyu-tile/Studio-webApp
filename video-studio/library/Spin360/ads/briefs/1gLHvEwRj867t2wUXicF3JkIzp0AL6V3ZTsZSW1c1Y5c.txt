Strategy
# Element
	Answer
	1 · Desire
	"I want to wake up and actually feel rested." In the song: "Then you wake up like, wait… I actually feel good."
	2 · Avatar
	Adults who sleep in a closed bedroom (windows shut, AC or heat on) and wake up tired without knowing why. The same people who stopped for the original ad.
	3 · Angle
	It's not the cold, it's the air. Babies in Finland sleep better outside because the air keeps moving. Your sealed bedroom has air that doesn't move. One reason, the same as the winner.
	4 · Mechanism
	It pulls air in from two sides at once and sends it out everywhere, like Saturn's rings. Because of how the blade is shaped, it never feels mechanical: it feels like a real breeze.
	5 · Authority
	The babies (researchers measured that they sleep almost twice as long outside), plus David, the guy who reinvented the wheel and got a deal on Shark Tank with Mark Cuban, Kevin O'Leary and Nick Woodman.
	6 · Awareness
	Hook and first minute: unaware. A story about babies sleeping in the snow, no product. Verse 3 on: product aware. The engineer and the Spin360 come in only after the problem ("a sealed room where the air won't move").
	7 · Emotion
	Wonder ("on purpose!") → "that's me" (the sealed room) → relief (walls gone, wake up rested). Upbeat country-pop, sing-along chorus. The music gets bigger when the product comes in.
	8 · Positioning
	Enemy: the sealed bedroom with dead-still air (not the cold, not the temperature). Spin360 brings the moving air of outside into your room.
	Production brief
Audio: the song is the whole ad: no voiceover, no music bed. If there is instrumental before her first word ("In Finland…"), cut it so her voice starts at 0:00 (we measured her voice coming in around 0:08–0:09). The times in the table are from the uncut song file, so move everything back by what you cut.
Picture: the same shots of the original ad, in the same order. Use the clean footage from the original project (no burned-in captions). Stretch or repeat shots to fit the song: the song is longer than the voiceover (2:58 vs 2:21). Cut on the beat.
The band stop: on "On purpose!" (~0:13) the band stops for a moment. Make it a hard cut to the baby's face at that exact moment.
Captions: the sung lyrics, word by word, same caption style as the original. Never show "Sweden" or "Swedish" anywhere: the song now says Finland / Finnish.
Changes from the original: no "$100 off" and no "First thousand people". The offer is now a 30-day trial (end text: Try it for 30 days). No scent names on screen (no pine, cedar, eucalyptus or citronella): only "scent pod". Skip the shot with the 7-Eleven sign (0:09.8 in the original): use another stroller shot there.
Ending: use the whole song. If the last chorus feels long, you can end after the second "It's the air" (~2:45).
Visual hooks (3 ads, same song)
The first sung line is slow, so the picture and the on-screen text carry the hook from the very first frame. On-screen text for all 3 (0:00 until "On purpose!"): In Finland, they put babies outside to sleep in the snow. Then on the band stop: On purpose.

	Bridge + Verse 4 (1:49–2:24)

	Final chorus (2:24–end)
	Closes the loop with the hook: it's the air, Spin360. Last image: the baby asleep outside.
