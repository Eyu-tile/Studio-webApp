Spin360_HearNotNear
3 ads: same voiceover and same body, 3 different visual hooks. About 33 seconds, 9:16.
The idea
A real customer wrote this in her review: "I have ALWAYS found fans to be wonderful to hear and terrible to be near!" That is how a lot of people feel. They like the sound of a fan to sleep, but they hate the blast: dry eyes, hair blowing around, air in the face.
Spin360 keeps the sound and drops the blast. Nothing is aimed at you. The air in the whole room just keeps moving, soft, like a window left open. The result we sell: you sleep right through the night and wake up actually rested.
The ad opens with her sentence and closes by answering it: "Wonderful to hear. And finally, good to be near."

The 3 visual hooks
The voiceover is the same in all three. Only the picture over the first two lines changes (about the first 6 seconds).
Hook
	What we see
	Hook 1: the review
	The review itself on screen, like a real 5-star review card, with her sentence appearing word by word as the voice reads it: "I have ALWAYS found fans to be wonderful to hear and terrible to be near!" Behind it, soft and out of focus, a bedroom with the Spin360.
	Hook 2: hear / near
	Two quick moments. "Wonderful to hear": someone falling asleep peacefully to the sound of a fan. "Terrible to be near": the same person with an ordinary fan blowing right in the face, hair flying, eyes squinting. The contrast is the hook.
	Hook 3: the blast
	Start directly on the problem: an ordinary fan blasting someone in bed, hair and sheets moving, papers flying off the nightstand. Then cut to the Spin360 in the same kind of room, where nothing is flying and a curtain just moves gently.
