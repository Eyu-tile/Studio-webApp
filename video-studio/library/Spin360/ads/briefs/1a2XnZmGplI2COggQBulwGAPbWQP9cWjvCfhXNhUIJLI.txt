Spin360 — Husband / Wife Relaxation Master Doc
Summary
Mass Desire: “I want the woman I love to feel relaxed and good at the end of the day.”
Situation: She carries the day into the bedroom.
Angle: Her day doesn’t always end when she gets into bed.
Desired State: She finally unwinds.
Mechanism: Natural-feeling 360° airflow that changes how the whole room feels.
Identity Payoff: I created that comfort for her.
Relationship Payoff: We get the quiet part of the night back.
________________


1. Desire
Surface Desire: I want the woman I love to feel relaxed, comfortable, and good at the end of the day.
Why Train:
 She feels more comfortable → she lets the day go → she becomes more relaxed and present → the end of the night feels better for both of us → they have more peaceful, affectionate time together.
Core Desire: Comfort
Secondary Desire: Belonging / Connection
Hidden Downstream Desire: Closeness, affection, intimacy.
The dominant desire should remain her feeling relaxed and good. Closeness and intimacy are consequences, not competing promises. This follows Schwartz’s principle of choosing one dominant desire and channeling it through the product’s performance.
________________


2. Avatar
Husbands and long-term male partners who notice when the woman they love has difficulty transitioning from the demands of the day into real relaxation at night.
Experiences: She may come to bed still thinking about work, responsibilities, tomorrow, family, her phone, or simply carrying the momentum of the day.
What he notices: She is physically in bed before she is fully settled. It takes time before she feels calm, comfortable, and present.
What matters to him: He likes seeing her relaxed and wants the end of the night to feel good for both of them.
Identity: He values being the man who notices what makes her feel better and quietly creates that comfort for her.
Not required: He does not need to already use a fan, like fans, understand airflow, or be actively searching for a sleep product.
________________


3. Angle
Her day doesn’t always end when she gets into bed.
The tension is the gap between:
Being in bed → actually feeling like the day is over.
Core Expression:
“You can’t switch her mind off for her. But you can change what the room feels like.”
The angle is the transition from day into relaxation, not insomnia, ADHD, fan ownership, or racing thoughts as a medical problem.
________________


4. Mechanism
Natural-feeling 360° airflow that changes the sensory experience of the whole room.
Spin360 pulls air from above and below and pushes it naturally outward around the room, instead of mechanically blasting air directly at one person. 
Mechanical Mechanism
Dual-direction intake → unique blade geometry → 360° distributed airflow → soft, surrounding breeze.
Sensory Performance
The winning scripts consistently translate that engineering into the feeling of:
“that soft breeze across your skin”
 “it feels like the walls were removed”
 “your bed is just outside”
 “the whole room breathes”
 “wind through trees”
They also repeatedly connect the experience to:
falling asleep before you notice it → waking up actually rested.
Sound
At lower settings, the winners position Spin360 as extremely quiet. At higher settings, they describe a steady natural “shhh”, more like wind through trees than conventional fan noise.
Supporting Sensory Layer
The scent pod can add another environmental layer and is described in winning ads as making the bedroom feel closer to a spa or natural environment. It is secondary to the breeze.
Mechanism Chain
360° airflow → soft surrounding breeze + natural sound → room feels more open, natural and calm → easier transition into relaxation.
The fan is the mechanism.
Changing how the room feels is the payoff of the mechanism.
________________


5. Authority
Primary Authority: David Patrick, inventor behind Spin360 and the Shark Wheel, whose story gives credibility to the unusual blade geometry and airflow system.
The existing winners use his Shark Tank / Shark Wheel background specifically to make the unconventional design more believable.
Secondary Authority: Real Spin360 customers describing the breeze, bedroom experience, relaxation, and sleep in their own words.
Authority should answer:
“Why should I believe this fan actually feels different?”
It should not replace the desire as the opening of the ad.
________________


6. Awareness Level
Unaware → Problem Aware
He does not start with:
“I need a better fan.”
He starts by recognizing:
“She gets into bed, but she’s still carrying the day with her.”
The ad then introduces the reframe:
Instead of giving her one more thing to do to relax, change the environment she steps into.
Only then do we reveal Spin360 as the mechanism.
The product enters after recognition of the situation, which is consistent with Schwartz’s awareness principle.
________________


7. Emotion — Valence + Intensity
Opening: Negative Valence / Low Intensity
Primary emotions:
Recognition / concern / mild frustration / tenderness
The prospect should think:
“Yeah. I know exactly what that looks like.”
Transition: Positive Valence / Low-to-Medium Intensity
Primary emotions:
Relief / calm / warmth / comfort / affection
The ad should feel:
intimate, peaceful, observational, premium
Not:
fearful, clinical, aggressive, sexual, or relationship-crisis driven.
________________


8. Differentiation / Positioning
Primary Enemy: A bedroom that still feels like an extension of the day.
Secondary Enemy: Relaxation solutions that ask her to do one more thing:
another app, routine, ritual, supplement, or task.
Product-Level Enemy: Traditional fans that create a direct, mechanical stream of air instead of changing the feeling of the entire room.
Positioning
Spin360 is not introduced as a better fan. It is introduced as a way to transform how the bedroom feels.
Its 360° airflow creates a soft, natural-feeling breeze around the room — the same perfect breeze / open window / outdoors experience repeatedly used in winning Spin360 advertising.
Positioning Statement
Spin360 transforms the bedroom into the place where the day finally ends — using a soft, natural-feeling 360° breeze to create an environment where she can unwind and the two of you can enjoy a calmer end to the night.
