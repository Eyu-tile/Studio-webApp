	Strategy
# Element
	Answer
	Inspiration
	Iteration of our winner Gabriel 11.2 "This 360 fan sucks. Number four will make you yell." (video · post). Same sarcastic 4-reasons structure, same tone, same Shark Tank close. What changes: winter, and the reasons are about the fan people turn to the wall.
	Desire
	Keep the fan on all winter (the moving air and the sound) without the cold blast in your face. In their words: "I point the fan at the wall so the air bounces off and I get small residual breezes that don't freeze me out and I'll still get the white noise", "circulation without the cold blast".
	Avatar
	Adults 30–65 who sleep with a fan all year and turn it to face the wall in winter, because the blast makes them cold. They keep it on for the moving air and the sound.
	Angle
	The winter fan you don't have to turn around: the feeling of a fan pointed at the wall, without pointing it anywhere.
	Mechanism
	Not explained on purpose. We sell the feeling (it was made to feel like your fan is pointed at the wall). The visuals show it: air moving around the room, nothing in the face.
	Proof
	Shown: curtain moving, hair still on the pillow, tissue test. Authority: the guy who reinvented the wheel on Shark Tank.
	Awareness
	Product aware. They know fans and already have their own fix (the wall).
	Emotion
	Curiosity (a winter fan that sucks?) → recognition (that's my fan at the wall) → coziness (blanket, sleeping like you're outside) → trust (Shark Tank + 30 nights). The sarcasm keeps it light.
	Format
	Voiceover with b-roll, ~41 s, vertical 9:16, same pacing and energy as Gabriel 11.2. Real footage first (UGC library + product). Captions on every line.
	Hooks
	One hook (the winner's copy), 3 visual first frames. Only the Spin360 in the hook, never a regular fan.
	Offer
	30 nights to try it (said with sarcasm, like the original). No discount, no price on screen.
