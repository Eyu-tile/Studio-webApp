Spin360_Baby_SIDSNursery — VSL


Desire: "I want my baby safe and healthy in the nursery, and I want to sleep with peace of mind."


Avatar: Expecting moms (no baby yet) setting up the nursery. That's why the problem scenes show what WILL happen once the baby arrives.


Awareness: Unaware. She already fears SIDS, but she has never heard that CO₂ builds up around a baby's face in still air. She thinks the crib, the baby monitor and the AC already have it covered.


Angle: The invisible problem (CO₂ sitting around the baby's face) → the Kaiser Permanente study (a fan in the room, 72% lower SIDS risk) → why the AC, purifiers and regular fans don't fix it → Spin360.


Mechanism: Spin360 pulls air in from the top and the bottom and moves the air of the whole room, 360°. A soft breeze, like an open window, never a blast in the face.


Proof: Coleman-Phox, Odouli & Li, Kaiser Permanente, Archives of Pediatrics & Adolescent Medicine, 2008: fan use linked to a 72% lower SIDS risk.


Script (VO)
Hook:


 If you're building a nursery right now, I can almost guarantee you're missing the most important thing. And if you don't have it, I'm not exaggerating, your baby could die.
You've got the crib. The baby monitor. The blackout curtains. Everything's perfect. So you think your baby's safe.
But there's something that's going to happen in that crib every single night, and nobody tells you about it.
Every time your baby breathes out, it's carbon dioxide.  And in a stuffy room, with no air moving, it doesn't go anywhere. It just sits there. Right around their little face.
So all night long, your baby will be breathing that same air back in. Over and over.
And they won't be able to tell you. They can't say, "Mom, something's wrong." They'll just squirm, toss and turn, and cry, and you'll have no idea why.
Researchers believe this is one of the reasons babies die from Sudden Infant Death Syndrome.
So they asked a simple question: what if we just kept the air moving?
A team at Kaiser Permanente looked at hundreds of babies. And the ones who slept with a fan in the room had a 72% lower risk of SIDS.
Seventy-two percent. From one simple change in the nursery.
But here's the problem. Your AC just cools the air. Your purifier just cleans it. Neither one actually moves the air around your baby's face.
And a regular fan only blows in one direction. You'd need four of them, pointed every which way, to really get the air in a room moving.


That's why Spin360 was created. It's a 360-degree air circulator, made for nurseries.
It pulls air in from the top and the bottom and gets the air in the whole room moving. All that carbon dioxide sitting around your baby's face just drifts away, so your baby breathes easier, with fresh air all night long.
And you'll see it on their little face. They wake up calmer, happier, and they stay that way all day.
Don't worry, it's gentle. No harsh blast of air in their face. Just a soft breeze all through the room. It's made to feel like an open window.
And that healthier air makes a difference fast. The number one thing parents tell us? Their babies went from sleeping two hours to five. And it's whisper quiet, so it won't wake them.


But in the end, here's what really matters: in those first nights, when you get up at 3 a.m. to check on your baby, you'll open the door, peek in, and there they'll be, sound asleep, breathing easy. And you'll go back to bed and actually sleep knowing they're okay.


It's built to last over ten years, so it grows with your child. From the nursery all the way to the big-kid room.
Is it cheap? No. But you just spent hundreds on a crib and a monitor so you could watch your baby breathe. This makes sure the air they're breathing is healthy.
Now you know. Don't let your baby spend even one night in still air. Finish the nursery before your baby comes home, because the first nights matter the most. Tap the link and get yours.
