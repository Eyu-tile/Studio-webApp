	SETUP

	THE INSIGHT
	Here's what the hotel has that your bedroom doesn't. Moving air, all night. Hotel rooms run quiet, constant circulation, so the air around you never goes still, never gets stale, and never piles up warm on one side of the bed while you sleep.
	

	YOUR BEDROOM
	Your bedroom does the opposite. The vent goes quiet once the thermostat's happy, the door's closed, and the air just stops. You spend all night in a puddle of your own warmth and wonder why you wake up foggy.
	

	THE REVEAL
	That's the whole reason this thing exists. It's called Spin360, and it gives your bedroom the hotel treatment. It pulls air from above and below at the same time and keeps the whole room gently turning, both sides of the bed, all night. And it's quiet enough that you'll hold your hand over it to check it's actually on.
	  
  

	PAYOFF
