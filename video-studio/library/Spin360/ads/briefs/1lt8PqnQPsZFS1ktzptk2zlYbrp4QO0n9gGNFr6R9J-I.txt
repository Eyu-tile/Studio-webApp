[2] Strategy




1 · DESIRE
	comfort — being comfortable inside your own home. The electric bill is the justification, not the desire.
Surface: keep the house cool without the bill going up.
	2 · AVATAR
	Owns AC, 35–64, hot climate, pays attention to the bill.
Experiences: a room the cool air never reaches · cold feet, hot middle of the room · a bill that makes running the AC impossible.
Behaviors tried before buying: managing the thermostat by activity · turning the AC off and toughing it out · eating the bill · ceiling fans and recirculators.
Emotions: financial strain · relief · skepticism about the price.
	3 · ANGLE
	Your AC is expensive and the room still isn't comfortable.
Season-locked: conditional on owning AC, and on it being hot.
	4 · MECHANISM
	Pulls air in from top and bottom at once, pushes it out in every direction — one single temperature in the room.
Proof: three gauges in one bedroom, 78 up / 71 down → all three read 73 with the fan on.
	5 · AUTHORITY
	The founder. Word: cold air sinks + the gauge numbers. Title: founder. Environment: outdoors, no authority.
Third party: three real customer comments on screen.
	6 · AWARENESS
	problem aware. Amplifies a problem the viewer already feels and names its cause.
	7 · VALENCE
	Positive, low intensity. Calm, satisfied. Copy and visuals in the same quadrant, no shift across the video.
