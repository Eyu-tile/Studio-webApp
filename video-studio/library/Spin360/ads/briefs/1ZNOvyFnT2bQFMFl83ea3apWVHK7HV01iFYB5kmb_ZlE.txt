Spin360_QuietOrNoise
3 ads: same voiceover and same body, 3 different visual hooks. About 36 seconds, 9:16.
The idea
People who sleep with a fan split in two groups. Some need the sound of a fan to fall asleep. Others can't stand fan noise. Spin360 works for both: settings 1 to 5 are ultra quiet, and settings 6 to 9 make a soft "shhh", like white noise. On any setting the air is not a blast, it is a soft breeze that moves through the whole room. The result we sell: you sleep right through the night and wake up actually rested.
The viewer should finish the ad thinking: "whichever kind of sleeper I am, this fan is for me."
Voiceover script
Some people can't sleep without the sound of a fan.
Some people can't sleep with it.
This fan is for both.
If you need silence, settings one to five are ultra quiet. You forget it's on.
If you need the sound, six through nine give you that soft shhh, like white noise.
Either way, it never blasts you. The air in the whole room just keeps moving, soft, like a breeze through an open window.
So you sleep right through the night. And you wake up actually rested.
David Patrick, the man who built it, calls it the best sleep of his life.
And those people who can't sleep with a fan? They keep telling him: "I hate fans… but I love yours."
Sleep on it for 30 nights. If you don't love it, just send it back.
The 3 visual hooks
The voiceover is the same in all three. Only the first 5 to 6 seconds of picture change (the first three lines of the script).
Hook
	What we see
	Hook 1: two sleepers
	Split screen. On one side, someone sleeping deeply with a fan running next to the bed. On the other side, someone awake and annoyed by a noisy fan (pillow over the head, looking at the fan). On "This fan is for both", both sides become the Spin360 next to a bed.
	Hook 2: the dial
	Close-up of a hand on the Spin360 control. We see the speed number go up from the low settings and pass 5, with the blades in the frame. Simple labels on screen: "1-5 quiet" and "6-9 shhh". On "This fan is for both", cut wide to the Spin360 in a bedroom.
	Hook 3: the swap
	Someone takes an ordinary fan out of the bedroom and puts the Spin360 in its place. On "This fan is for both", the Spin360 is running next to the bed.
	How the edit could go (open, your call)
* Material: use our UGC library first (bedroom and sleep shots, Spin360 on the nightstand, the dial, blades up close). Use David and the customer comments from Reinvent_Wheel_SplitScreen_David_V2_Hook_2.mp4 for the last part.
* "Settings one to five": someone asleep with the Spin360 on beside the bed. Bring the music almost to zero here, so we feel the quiet.
* "Six through nine": the dial passing 5 and the blades. Let us hear the real fan sound softly under the voice, so the viewer hears the "shhh".
* "It never blasts you... breeze through an open window": a curtain, a plant or hair moving gently. No strong wind.
* "Sleep right through... rested": sleeping, then waking up in morning light.
* David line: David from the winner. He is not speaking here, so avoid shots where his lips clearly move.
* "I hate fans... but I love yours": the real customer comments from the winner.
* Last line: end card with "Sleep on it for 30 nights."
