# Spin360 brand guide (from Spin360 Style Guide v1, August 2025)

Source: `brand/Spin360_Style_Guide_v1.pdf` (16 pages). This file is the working summary; the PDF wins on any conflict.

**Product:** Spin360, a premium pedestal fan with a 360° cage, a gold halo ring, a blue LED ring on the pole, a round
digital control (shows "F3") and a diffuser tray that holds scented pads (several scents).
**Brand words:** **Luxury. Quiet. Refreshing.** (always in this order; used as a three-line stack.)
**Feeling:** "Feels like a pleasant breeze."

## Type
- **Inter** for every brand execution. Weights: Light, Regular, Medium, Bold.
  The guide itself uses Light/Regular at large sizes (calm, airy), not heavy bold. Prefer Light/Regular for titles,
  Medium for captions, Bold sparingly.
- Fallback only if Inter is impossible: Helvetica, then Arial ("should not occur frequently").
- The logo wordmark is a serif: never set "spin360" in Inter as a substitute for the logo; use the logo file.

## Photography / footage rules (the look every shot must hit)
Tone: Luxury = wind blowing through doors from a private beach and pristine water, light wispy clouds, gentle wavy
air. Quiet = gentle wind and a view of a private beach. Refreshing = scented flowers and ocean breeze.

**Required in lifestyle scenes:**
1. Breeze moving fabric curtains (sheer white/cream).
2. An open window or door.
3. Sea / sky / clouds in view through it.
4. Modern luxury decor (white/cream sofas, light wood, minimal, airy, sunlit).

**Optional:** 5. custom scene built with AI (furniture + view added to an empty curtain room). AI-made rooms are allowed.

Reference images: `brand/images/` (`inspo_*`, `photo_*`).

## Elements
- **Soft Wave**: fine flowing gray lines, "the motion of a pleasant breeze". Pair with the **all-gold logo**.
  Good for motion backgrounds and transitions (slow drift, never fast). See DO_NOT_USE for the reference file.

## What this means for video (working defaults, confirm per brief)
- Pace: slow and calm. Long takes, soft dissolves, gentle push-ins; no whip pans, glitches, flashes or hard bass hits.
- Sound: airy, quiet. Soft ambient/piano/strings, wind and curtain rustle, distant waves. The fan is **quiet**: never
  add loud motor or whoosh SFX to it.
- Grade: bright, sunlit, high-key whites, soft cyan sea and sky; product shots on Midnight Blue or neutral gray.
- Captions/supers: Inter Light/Regular, Cloud White on footage (Midnight Blue on light frames), Halo Gold for emphasis.
- End card: Midnight Blue background, primary logo centred, "Luxury. Quiet. Refreshing." in Cloud White.
