# Spin360: do-not-use rules

Status rules for the atlas: **BANNED** = never use. **CAUTION** = use only with the fix noted. **OK** = fine.
Add client rules here as they come in (with date and source).

## From the style guide (v1, Aug 2025)
- BANNED: any colour outside the six palette colours for graphics, type, backgrounds or end cards.
- BANNED: any typeface other than Inter for supers and captions (Helvetica/Arial only if Inter is impossible).
- BANNED: retyping the wordmark; always use a logo file from `brand/logo/`. Do not recolour the logo outside the
  four approved versions.
- CAUTION: lifestyle shots missing the required look (curtains in a breeze, open window/door, sea/sky view, modern
  luxury decor). Fine as a cutaway; not as a hero shot.

## Assets that came with the guide
- BANNED as-is: `brand/images/element_soft-wave_THIRD-PARTY-LOGO.png`. The Soft Wave sample in the guide has a
  **WATERMAN Paris** logo in the middle (another company's mockup). Rebuild the wave pattern or crop the logo out
  before any use; never show the Waterman mark.
- CAUTION: `inspo_*` and `photo_*` images are mood references from the guide. Their source and licence are unknown
  (some look like stock or AI renders). Use them as generation/look references, not in published ads,
  until the client confirms rights.

## Working rules (not from the guide; mine, until the client says otherwise)
- No claims about scents, noise level (dB), cooling power or health that are not in the client's materials.
- No loud/aggressive SFX, fast flashy edits or meme-style captions; they contradict "Quiet".
