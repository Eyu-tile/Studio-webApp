# Spin360 claims register (from the library footage)

Every claim spoken or written on screen in the footage, found while mapping it (2026-11-14). **Nothing here is approved.**
Before a line goes into an ad, the client must confirm the proof (or the line is cut). Clip ids: `resources/ATLAS.md`.

| risk | meaning |
|---|---|
| 🔴 HIGH | health, medical, baby/child safety, longevity, research or patent claims: do not use without written client + legal sign-off |
| 🟠 PROOF | measurable product claims (quiet/dB, cooling, energy cost, price/offer): use only with current proof |
| 🟢 OPINION | personal experience / superlative opinion: usually fine as a testimonial, client to confirm |
