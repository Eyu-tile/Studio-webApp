## The product as it appears in footage
- **Two colourways**: black (black sparkle) and white (white pearl). Gold halo ring under the top cover, clear 360° cage of slats.
- **Height-adjustable**: the pole takes an extension section (Avery "adjusting height" ed6f4baf, verified). Short = tabletop setup of
  the same unit, tall = floor setup. Real short-unit footage is fine; keep one height per ad.
- **Pole LED ring**: user-selectable colour (blue / red / green / off; Vicky IMG_8045). Blue is the on-brand default.
- **Display**: round black display on the pole, speeds F1-F9, can show the Spin360 logo. Best close-ups: lake-house sunroom IMG_3849
  (F9→F1→F9, logo sharp), IMG_9752 (off→F1→F9), "Button press" 63-80 s, "POV, turning on" 3.5-9 s.
- **Remote**: black (lake house, Lauren "using remote") and white (nursery).
- **Scent**: either a clear curved tray clipped into the side of the cage (lake house IMG_3808/3809/3810) or a pod/pad dropped into a
  well under the twist-off centre cap on top (Vicky, Kylie, Lauren). Pods: **Lux** (gold label, yellow gel), **Fresh** (dark label,
  blue gel), **Clean** (green gel); sold sealed with a hinged clear lid; shipped in a blue organza bag.
- **Packaging**: light-blue/white retail box ("Gentle Breeze", "360° AIRFLOW", head photo before curtains, pedestal drawing),
  pulp trays, poles in plastic, insert card "STOP please read this" with founder tips.
- **Watch for**: yellow warning sticker on the pole (several Avery, Lauren, Kylie, IMG_97xx units); white tape label (lake house
  IMG_3783); paper card left inside the cage (Lauren "Closing blinds V1" 36-40 s); visible power cords (lake house).
- **Not retail**: prototype renders/parts (spiral wire cage + teal core; purple flower blade; clear butterfly / five-petal blades in
  founder hooks and UT_April_FanCleaning: confirm with client whether the five-petal blade is the retail blade).
