What v2 takes from it:
- **One recurring "she".** TC0217 follows one woman through her routine. v2 uses one real creator, Wren Presley (6 clips), as that woman:
  - the hook (blowing her nose in bed)
  - "bad night"
  - "everything people try" (vacuuming)
  - "washing cleans the sheets" (holding sheets)
  - "waking up the same way for years"
  - the end card (the box landing on her bed)
- **Person above, mites below.** TC0217 keeps cutting from her cleaning above the mattress to the mites living underneath. v2 does the same: a hand on a bare mattress, then mites inside the mattress; the fitted sheet coming off, then mites in the fibers.
- **A strong opening shot that holds.** TC0217's first shot runs 5.7 s. v2 holds one real shot (Wren) through the whole first sentence, with a punch-in instead of 3 quick cuts.
- **Literal routine shots.** TC0217 matches every routine word with a literal action (strips the bed, hot wash, vacuum). v2 does the same with real people: pill, sheets, vacuum, sheet off, spray bottle thrown in the bin on "No sprays".
- **End card on the product, in the woman's own bed.**
