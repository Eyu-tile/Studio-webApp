# TrueClean / CaptureCards — House Ad Style Guide
Learned from the "previous ads" Frame.io share (60 folders, 1,499 files; ~45 concepts, 344 finished-ad files incl. hook variants & revisions).
Status: built from every concept's latest cut + all 4 winners (deep). Stats below from 194 unique ad files processed so far.

## 1. Format numbers
| | House norm |
|---|---|
| Aspect | 9:16 (1080×1920) is the master; 1:1 and 16:9 only for winners re-cut for Google |
| Length | median **~114s**. Bands: 35–50s (short "reasons"/sale cuts), 70–140s (core), 200–365s (long VSL / story) |
| Cut rate | median **2.6s per shot** (most ads 2.0–3.1s). Never a static hold >4s except a talking head under b-roll |
| Voice pace | ~3.3 words/s — fast, conversational, no dead air |
| Loudness | ~−14 LUFS |
| Music | Low bed under VO (most ads), dropped entirely on some. VO always dominant |
| Hooks | 3 hooks per concept (sometimes 4–8), **shared body**, identical after the hook join |

## 2. Script skeleton (every concept follows it)
1. **Hook (0–6s)** — one of the formulas in §3. Often repeats as an on-screen headline.
2. **Symptom mirror** — hyper-specific, lived detail: "lower nostril", "3am", "clears within minutes of standing up", "fine on vacation", "itching starts the minute you get into bed", "puffy eyes", "brain fog after 8 hours".
3. **Misattribution reveal** — "That's not pollen / not your sinuses / not menopause / not your skin. The problem never leaves your bed."
4. **The colony** — "dust mites… a colony of 100,000 to 10 million… deep in the fibers… feeding on the skin you shed… inches from your face for eight hours a night." Often "Der p 1 / droppings / 20 a day" for mechanism concepts.
5. **Why everything failed (the wrong layer)** — hot wash cleans fabric on top, covers seal them in, sprays put chemicals where your face lies / dead mites still allergenic, purifier cleans air not bed, vacuum only surface, new mattress gets colonised again. Often with red ✕ on each.
6. **Product reveal** — "That's what CaptureCards fix." / "Most mite products try to kill them. This one pulls them out."
7. **Mechanism** — plant-derived pheromone lure (sometimes "neryl propionate") draws mites up out of the deep fibers → adhesive locks them on the card → throw it away. "Same capture method pest-control pros have used for decades."
8. **Proof** — "In lab testing, captured up to 99%" (lab / Y-maze / scientist shots) + "over 30,000 homes".
9. **Use** — "slide one under the mattress, one near the pillow… one card keeps working ~3 months… no spray, no plugs, no laundry marathon."
10. **Offer + CTA** — "90-day money-back guarantee (no return needed)" + sale % when running ("up to 65% off", Halloween/BF/Cyber Monday 75–79%) + "tap/click below".

## 3. Hook formulas that recur (and winners)
- **Diagnostic "if… that's not X"** (WINNER 3am Nose): "If it's always the lower nostril, the side you're lying on, that's not pollen." Variants: "Follow the clue", "If you sleep away and wake clear… that's your mattress."
- **First-person discovery** (WINNER Peri): "I thought my new allergies were just part of menopause, until I learned what was inside my mattress." Variants with age: "I'm 59…", "At 62…", "I thought it came with turning 70."
- **UGC comment/quote as hook**: real-sounding complaint read out ("Pretty weird. I go to sleep unblocked and wake at 3am with one side blocked…").
- **List takedown** (WINNER HK2): "Everyone with blocked mornings buys the same five things. Here's why none of them work."
- **Timeline / experiment** (WINNER TC0093): "Week 1 vs Week 12 — what a single card pulls out of a clean mattress." "Here's what happens if you slide two cards under… for four weeks."
- **Contrarian/pattern-interrupt**: "Put the dust mite spray down. It works. That's the problem." / "Killing dust mites was never the same thing as removing them."
- **Character POV**: mites narrating ("She thinks changing the sheets gets rid of us. Cute.") — TC0217.
- **Stat/authority**: "Researchers found dust-mite allergen in 84 out of every 100 beds." / "The Japanese secret…"
- **Curiosity/metaphor**: "100,000 tenants who don't pay rent — evict them for <$30", "To a dust mite this card acts like a hallucinogen."
- **Rage/story** (TC0297 animated): "I told my GP to go f*** herself… 12 weeks later."
- **Sale "apology"**: "I need to publicly apologize… I lied — our Halloween price wasn't our best."
- **Audience call-outs**: menopause/peri, parents of kids with night cough, diagnosed dust-mite allergy, itchy skin at night, brain fog.

## 4. Visual language
- **Protagonist**: women 40–70 in a bedroom (warm lamps, white/grey bedding). UGC creators and AI realistic women; men rarely.
- **Formats in rotation**:
  - Full AI cinematic b-roll + VO (3am Nose, Follow the Clue, Japanese) — moody blue/amber bedroom light.
  - UGC talking head (creator holding the box/card, sat on bed or sofa) cut with b-roll every 2–4s (TC0175, TC0291, TC0295, Revised).
  - Animated styles: Pixar-like 3D story (TC0297), claymation (TC0156, What They Leave Behind), anime-style (TC0293), mite-character 3D (TC0217).
  - Doc/stat: web-article screenshots, lab scientists, microscope circles (84% study).
- **Required beats on screen**: symptom close-up (blowing nose, pinching nose, rubbing eyes, 3am awake) → x-ray/airflow nose graphic (blue/red) → macro mites in fibers → failed fixes (washer, vacuum, spray, purifier) → card on mattress with lure glow/scent trails → exploded card layers (teal shell / mesh / yellow adhesive) → hand sliding card under mattress & by pillow → card dropped in bin → social-proof grid of customers holding cards → lab/99% → product hero with guarantee badge + CTA button.
- **End card**: teal card(s) + "90-DAY MONEY-BACK GUARANTEE" badge + "CLICK BELOW / TAP BELOW / Link below" button with cursor tap; sale versions add % off, price ($89.99 for 20), card counts, seasonal art.
- **Sale overlay**: persistent top banner (Halloween/Black Friday/Cyber Monday "UP TO 75–79% OFF + UP TO 8 FREE CARDS").

## 5. Caption systems (pick one per ad, keep consistent)
1. **White box, black text** (most common): 2–6 words, lower-middle, sentence case. Used on winners.
2. **Bold uppercase with keyword colour** (yellow / green / red highlight words) — UGC & claymation styles.
3. **Teal box, white text** + yellow keyword (e.g. "capture card") + big beat labels ("WEEK 1", "DAY 1", "✕ No plugs") — Google/feed winners.
4. Headline card at top for the hook ("If You Go To Bed Breathing Fine And Wake Up At 3am…", red "LISTEN TO THIS").
Captions = exact VO words, chunked; emojis/✓/✕ icons for list beats.

## 6. Compliance shift (why "Remove visible mites" winners exist)
Older winners used phone-light inspections, dark specks on used/cut cards, week-12 dirty card reveals, mite pile-ups. The current brief bans these → the winners were re-cut with them removed. When adapting any old winner: keep script & pacing, **swap**: phone-light/catch shots → card under mattress/lure animation; "see what it caught" lines stay only if the brief allows. Also avoid old "MITE-CAPTURE CARD"/cream card designs; use current teal TrueClean card & box.

## 7. Revisions — what changes between rounds (from 2nd/3rd/Final, TC0189, TC0291, TC0300…)
- Hooks get shorter and more specific; first 3s gets a headline overlay.
- Visual fixes: more b-roll cutaways over talking head, consistent card design, CTA end card standardised (teal + guarantee + button).
- Problem/visible-catch shots removed; lab/99% + "30,000 homes" added.
- Loudness/pace tightened (shorter gaps).

## 8. Checklist for every new ad
- [ ] Hook in first 1–2s, matches a formula above; 3 hook variants on one body
- [ ] Shot every 2–3s; each visual matches the spoken line
- [ ] Symptom → misattribution → colony → wrong-layer → pull-them-out → lure+adhesive → 99%/30k → place → guarantee + CTA
- [ ] Captions = exact words, one consistent system
- [ ] Current teal card/box only; no phone-light, cut-open, visible catch, before/after
- [ ] End card: product + 90-day guarantee + tap/click below (+ sale banner if running)
- [ ] VO clearly on top; −14 LUFS

### Hook patterns (new)
- **Myth of the advice**: "Just stop scratching. If I had a dollar…" / "Switch your detergent. Try an antihistamine. It's probably stress. I heard all of it."
- **Self-experiment**: "So one night I slept on the couch, just to see what would happen." / "She dragged a yoga mat onto her living room floor…"
- **Cycle/loop reframe**: "That antihistamine… isn't solving anything. It's resetting the cycle."
- **Guarantee-first objection kill**: "I know what you're thinking… every Facebook ad promises…"
- **Confession/apology**: "I need to publicly apologize… because I lied."
- **Cold-open dialogue**: "Mark, what's wrong? / Nothing."
- **Age specificity**: "New hives at 47", "At 70, I figured…", "If you're a woman over 55…".
- **Timeline promise**: "Here's what happens to your airways if you… for four weeks."

### Recurring body beats & devices
- Failed-fix montage with **big red ✕** over each object (spray, vacuum, purifier filter, laundry, poisons) → "No sprays / No plugs / No filters / No laundry" — often stacked ✅ list or ✕ list.
- **Metaphors**: overflowing drain (bail vs turn off tap), snake in house, tenants not paying rent.
- **Research beat**: 2am laptop, "why am I itchy in bed but can't find bugs?", article screenshot "The Tiny Roommates in Your Bed".
- **Talking-head PiP**: narrator cut-out in lower-left corner over b-roll (TC0309, TC0383).
- **Placement variants**: "four cards, four corners", "one under the mattress, one near the pillow", "under your pillow and across the mattress"; "replace every 90 days / three months".
- **Closers**: "You don't have to be convinced. I wasn't. You just have to be willing to find out." · "Breaking the cycle costs nothing." · "It's not a cure. It's one concrete step." · "I'll put the link below." · "If you're still seeing the link, there's still stock."
- End card: TrueClean logo top + real teal card + green **SHOP NOW** pill (newer ads) or Black Friday graphic (79% off, 12 free cards, $89.99, CLICK NOW).

### Caption systems (new ones)
- Navy/blue highlight box with white bold text (TC0334).
- ALL-CAPS white, keyword in **purple** box (TC0235) or **yellow** (TC0383, TC0392).
- ALL-CAPS lime/yellow dialogue (TC0306 drama).
- White sentence case, black outline, profanity in red (TC0297).
- Thin white text, no box (TC0336); italic small white-box (TC0303).

### Compliance evolution (newest ads)
- Persistent top label **"Dramatization informed by research"** (TC0309).
- Hedged medical language: "allergens can trigger reactions in sensitized people… what's driving any individual's symptoms is something only a doctor can work out", "it's not a cure", "help manage the mites where you sleep".
- Older cuts in these shares still contain backlit/phone-light catch shots ⚠ — do not reuse those beats.

## 10. PRODUCT LOOK — hard rule (client guideline video "Continued", Drive folder 1Z2FFGNqWVWxVOUmJE8OFrl_8Oa6CL0M9)
- The real card is **dark green**, soft fabric "card sponge", **only the TrueClean logo** + yellow band. **No "MITE-CAPTURE CARD" text.**
- NEVER show: bright blue/turquoise AI cards, illustrated/comic cards, any card with "Mite-Capture Card" printed, old cream/white card, AI exploded layers built on the old card.
- For lure/adhesive mechanism beats, use AI animations **with no card in frame** (scent trail through fibers, mites onto a plain pad) and show the card itself only with real footage (UGC, REAL PRODUCT REFERENCES, CTA folder).
- UGC where the card reads light blue on camera (Claire Fisher 12, Lynch 20/21) → avoid; prefer Ailani 12/31, Ryan Suzuki 9, Lynch 24/33, Alan Choi 7/26, B-Roll 6.

## 11. Winner Room (22 winning videos + 8 statics) — see 03_winning_ads/WINNERS_PLAYBOOK.md
- Pace to beat: avg shot 1.6–2.2 s, VO ~220 wpm with pauses ≤0.6 s, 97–100 % of cuts on word starts, −14 LUFS.
- Music bed under VO in ~90 %; transition SFX essentially absent (hard cuts) — keep house SFX sparing.
- "Only AI" is proven: TC0142 (100 % AI, $114K) and TC0156 (AI claymation) are winners.
- Itch reference: TC0205 (UGC story, B&W problem section, white-box captions, dark-green card).
- Do not copy winners' old teal card, phone-light/cut-open reveals, 1–2 card placement, or flagged claims (99 %, 50 cm, survey %).
