Source: client Drive folder 1Z2FFGNqWVWxVOUmJE8OFrl_8Oa6CL0M9. It holds two guideline videos: "How to create Accurate Dust Mite Visuals" and "Continued". Full transcripts are in this folder, and reference frames are in `reference_frames/` (`card.jpg` shows the real card). These rules override anything seen in older ads or winners.

## Dust mites (visuals)
- Mites are microscopic arachnids, 0.2–0.3 mm long, and spider-like. They have **no eyes**.
- Never show insects, bed bugs or fleas as dust mites. Never show any bug with eyes.
- Approved mite visuals are the eyeless, translucent macro mites already in the library.
- The ATLAS marks the banned stylised and insect-like clips (BANNED or CAUTION). Check ATLAS before using any clip.

## Claims
- Dust mite allergy **cannot be cured**, so never say the cards cure it.
- Dust mites **do not bite**.
- You **cannot see** dust mites with the naked eye, and you **cannot feel** them crawling on your skin.
- Not every home has dust mites. It is their droppings (allergen) that some people react to.
- Symptoms vary from person to person:
  - itchy nose, throat or mouth
  - watery, itchy eyes
  - asthma-like breathing at night
  - persistent cough
  - fatigue, looking tired
  - eczema-like skin irritation
- Do not use these unsupported winner-era claims unless they are approved again: 99% capture, 50 cm / 20 in attraction, removes existing allergen, week-by-week symptom timelines, survey percentages, "lower nostril = not pollen", "visible specks = mites".
- Recheck "30,000+ homes", any discount, and the guarantee terms before each ad. The guarantee is 90 days from delivery, no return needed, and original shipping is excluded.

## Skin (itch ads)
- Show mild redness, scratching, and an itchy, uncomfortable feeling.
- **Do not** show aggressive rash, swollen or disease-looking skin, or wounds. The client calls those visuals "a disease, not dust mites".

## The product card (hard rule)
- The real card is **dark green**. It is a soft fabric "card sponge" with a yellow band and **only the TrueClean logo**.
- NEVER show:
  - the old teal card or any card printed with "MITE-CAPTURE CARD"
  - blue or turquoise AI cards
  - illustrated or comic cards
  - the old cream or white card
  - AI exploded-layer cards built on the old design
- **Banned beats:**
  - inspecting the card with a phone light
  - cutting the card open
  - a visible catch or "dark specks" reveal
  - before/after card shots
  - anything from the "Problem Clips" folder
- **Mechanism beats:** use AI scent-trail and sticky-pad visuals with **no card in frame**. Show the real card only in real footage: UGC, REAL PRODUCT REFERENCES, or the CTA folder.
- Avoid UGC where the card reads light blue on camera: Claire Fisher 12, Lynch 20/21.
- Current usage directions: **four cards per bed**, card kept sealed, moved monthly, replaced within 3 months. Older scripts say 1–2 cards and "forget it"; flag that wording to the client.
