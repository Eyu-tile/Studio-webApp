## 2. The argument every winner makes
**"The effort you already make is aimed at a different place than the problem."**
1. Recognisable situation or assumption (washing sheets · nose on the pillow side · tired/puffy · itch only in bed).
2. Reframe: it's not X (pollen / stress / dry skin / you) — it's what lives *inside* the mattress. Microscopic, so a clean bed still has them.
3. Why your fixes failed — each fix does a different job (wash = surface, cover = seams, purifier = air, spray kills but leaves allergen, cream/antihistamine = reaction).
4. The card does the opposite job: **lure → stick → leave with the card** (always 2 parts, always physical).
5. Ease: "No spray. No plugs. No laundry." (3-beat list, usually with ✓ or ✕ graphics).
6. Payoff / proof moment (historically the phone-light catch — now banned; replace with lure→trap→bin).
7. Close: 30,000+ homes · 90-day money-back · discount · "tap/click below".
Validation lines that recur: "you're doing everything right", "it's never just you being lazy", "your bedroom can look spotless".

## 3. Hooks (first 1.5–3 s)
Measured first-shot length: median 2.9 s (0.7–6.8). The hook line is always on screen as a title in the first frame.
Families: **mistaken belief** ("I thought washing my sheets was cleaning my bed") · **specific symptom clue** ("If it's always the lower nostril…", "Itchy skin every night… but only after you get into bed?") · **list** ("3 signs…", "3 reasons…", "4 things…") · **counter-intuitive fact** ("A dead mite is just as allergenic as a live one") · **timeline/what-happens** ("Week 1 vs week 12", "What happens 48 hours after…") · **negative/warning** ("3 reasons I regret…", "Don't buy another… until you hear this") · **authority/study** ("I found a study…") · **personification** ("She thinks changing the sheets gets rid of us. Cute.").

## 5. Visual language
- **AI can carry an entire winner**: TC0142 (100% AI realistic people + anatomical cutaways + 3D card), TC0156 (100% AI claymation), TC0217 (AI personified mites), TC0128 (AI-heavy with AI presenter), TC0175/TC0232 (AI presenters). → "Only AI" briefs are proven.
- AI recipes that recur: macro mites in fibre/foam; "zoom into the mattress" cutaways; magnifier circles on a bed; nose/sinus anatomical overlays with glowing airflow; lure shown as glowing scent trails / concentric rings pulling mites to the card; exploded card layers with mites stuck; AI people sleeping, sneezing, scratching, stripping beds, vacuuming.
- Real-UGC winners (TC0235, TC0205, TC0171, Concept 9) use many different women, morning/bed scenes, and **desaturate the "problem" section to black-and-white**, returning to colour at the solution (TC0205, TC0165).
- Failed fixes get a **big red ✕** over the footage (TC0313, TC0171, TC0158, Concept 9); benefits get ✅ checklists ("✅ No spray ✅ No plugs ✅ No laundry").
- Timeline ads use teal pill title cards: DAY 1 / END OF WEEK 1 / WEEK 2 / MONTH 3.
- Endings: product pack + 90-DAY MONEY-BACK seal + "65% OFF" badge + animated CLICK BELOW / TAP BELOW button with cursor; testimonial grid of customers holding the pack (TC0165, TC0158).

## 6. Captions — 6 systems seen
1. **White rounded box, black bold text, 2–5 words** (TC0205, TC0124 yellow variant, TC0175, TC0178) — our TC0451 style.
2. Small white text with soft shadow, no box, lowercase (TC0128, TC0313, TC0217).
3. Bold uppercase white + coloured highlight on the key word (purple TC0235, green TC0156, yellow box TCC0003).
4. One giant word at a time (TC0171, opening of TC0165).
5. Small phrase-length white box, sentence case (TC0142).
6. Serif white with outline (TC0158).
Hook title: red box with white text, or white box with red second line (TC0223, TC0235).

## 7. Audio — measured
- **Music bed under the VO in ~90 %** (music-only gaps sit 7–10 dB below speech; quieter, ~16 dB below, in TC0175/0313/0124/0217/0232). Only TC0171 (raw selfie UGC) has none.
- **Transition SFX: essentially none.** Whoosh/impact energy at cuts matched random points in the audio (8–13 % of cuts vs 10 % random). Winners are hard cuts with a clean VO + music mix. (Our whoosh/stamp SFX are a deliberate house addition requested on TC0451 — keep them sparing.)
- Masters at −14 LUFS.

## 8. What NOT to copy from the winners (current product notes + Drive guideline)
- Old teal "MITE-CAPTURE CARD" pack shots and illustrated/turquoise cards → current dark-green fabric card, TrueClean logo only.
- Phone-light inspection, cutting the card open, "dark specks" catch reveals, visible catch → banned.
- Placement: old scripts say one card under the mattress / one by the pillow and "forget it". **Current guide: four cards per bed, card kept sealed, moved monthly, replaced within 3 months.**
- Claims the library flags as unsubstantiated: 99 % capture, 50 cm / 20 in attraction, removes existing allergen/droppings, week-by-week symptom timelines, 93/95/89 % survey stats, "lower nostril = not pollen" as diagnosis, visible specks = mites. Recheck "30,000+ homes", discounts and guarantee terms (90 days from delivery, no return, shipping excluded).
